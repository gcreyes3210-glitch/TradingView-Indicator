## Findings that held in every run and both halves of the year
* 5-minute HTF zones lose. 1H-swing-sweep SMTs lose. Breakeven at 1 R costs more winners than it saves.
* **The NQ edge did not transfer to ES/MES (Run J) and did not hold on the prior NQ year (Run K).** Runs C2→I were tuned on one symbol-year and that is the only sample where the rules make money. Across I, J, K: longs were ≥ 0 in all three (+1,495 / +378 / +24), shorts were −1,066 and −2,052 in two of three. Pivot SMTs were positive in both NQ years but negative on ES. Nothing else repeats.
* Across every variant, the 2025-09 → 2026-09 year is the profitable one and 2024-09 → 2025-09 is not, on both symbols and with every gate. The year, not the symbol or the filter, decides the result. That is the definition of no edge.
* Timeframe note: all 365-day runs A–M used 5-minute IFVGs (on a 5m chart the 2m/3m feeds are off and the ladder is empty; the 15m feed only supplies SMTs). Run N tested the 1-minute IFVG entry over two years and it lost on both.
* **Conclusion of this pass: the current signal set has no demonstrated edge on 5m or 1m.** The exit work (3 R target, no breakeven, market entry) is sound mechanics and carries over, but the entry rules need a different idea, not more filters.

## Initiative continuation from prior value (IVC) — spec and IVC1 runs
Source: the AMT / Market Profile notes the user supplied (scenario templates C "open above value + acceptance above VAH → initiative buying / price discovery" and D, sections 5, 7, 14.2). Rationale: every rule that paid on NQ was continuation out of an opening structure (ORB); every fade failed (VWR, ONR, VP fades). This encodes the notes' initiative scenario with objective definitions: prior-day value as the reference, the first hour as the initial balance, acceptance = the first hour never re-entered value, entry = range extension beyond the initial balance in the direction away from value.
Rule (v1, New York time):
- Reference = previous cash session (09:30–16:00) volume profile from 1-minute bars, 1-point rows, POC, 70 % value area expanded two rows at a time on the heavier side (as in `VP_80Rule_strategy.pine`). Also PDH / PDL / PDC.
- Open location at 09:30: above (open > VAH), below (open < VAL), inside (no trade).
- Initial balance (IB) = high / low of 09:30–10:30.
- Acceptance outside value: above-VA day requires IB low > VAH; below-VA day requires IB high < VAL. Otherwise no trade (the day re-entered value; that is the 80 % rule's territory, tested as VP4 and rejected).
- Entry, 10:30–12:00: above-VA day → long on the first 5m close > IB high + 2 ticks + 0.15 × IB width; below-VA day → short mirror. Direction away from value only. One trade per day. Skip if IB width < 4 ticks.
- Stop = other side of the IB ∓ 2 ticks. No target, flat at the 16:00 close. Costs as ORB ($1/side, 1 tick slippage).
- Tags: open location, IB width (pts, % of prior-day range), IB low/high vs VAH/VAL/POC, opening range vs overnight range (ORB's tag), gap, whether ORB v1.3 also traded that day.
Planned single-change variants: (a) acceptance loosened to IB low > POC (below: IB high < POC); (b) reference = prior day's range (open > PDH, IB low > PDH) instead of the value area; (c) IB 09:30–10:00, entries 10:00–12:00; (d) acceptance requirement off (open outside value is enough); (e) retest entry: after the close beyond IB high, a limit at IB high filled on a later bar within 6 bars, else no trade; (f) overnight value area (18:00–09:30 profile) as the reference instead of the prior cash session.

## ICT AMD (accumulation → manipulation → distribution) — spec and AMD1 runs
Requested 2026-09-23: an ICT rule built on AMD in that order, distribution must start with an IFVG or an MSS/BOS, tested on 1m up to 5m. Prior evidence: the ICT indicator (sweep + IFVG reversal, runs A–N) had no edge on 5m or 1m, and every fade of a level has lost on NQ. This spec is narrower: the sweep must take a defined accumulation range, the reversal must be confirmed by structure before entry, and the target is the liquidity on the other side of the range.
Definitions (New York time, all on the detection timeframe TF ∈ {1, 2, 3, 5} minutes, bars resampled from the 1m data, stamped by open time):
- **Accumulation (A)** = high / low of the accumulation window. Default: overnight 18:00–09:30. Variants: Asia 20:00–00:00; London 02:00–05:00; pre-market 08:00–09:30.
- **Manipulation (M)** = the first bar after 09:30 whose high > A high (bearish setup) or low < A low (bullish setup), inside the manipulation window 09:30–10:30. The sweep leg starts at the last 3-bar fractal low (bullish setup: high) formed since 09:30 before the sweep bar; if none, the session extreme so far. Sweep extreme = highest high (lowest low) while price is beyond the range. Manipulation is confirmed by the first close back inside the range. A day whose first excursion is not reversed by 10:30 (no close back inside) has no AMD trade.
- **Distribution (D) trigger**, after confirmation, entries until 11:00, whichever comes first:
  - **MSS**: a close below the sweep-leg start low (bullish setup: above the leg-start high). Entry at that close.
  - **IFVG**: a bullish FVG formed inside the sweep leg (bar[i-2].high < bar[i].low; bullish setup: bearish FVG) that is later closed through (close < FVG low; mirror). Entry at that close.
  - Tag which trigger fired and the bars from the sweep to the entry.
- **Stop** = sweep extreme ± 2 ticks. **Target** = the opposite side of the accumulation range (A low for shorts). Skip if reward:risk < 1.0 at entry. Stop beats target on the same bar.
- One trade per day, first setup only, both directions. Flat at 12:00 if still open (variant: 16:00). Costs: $1/side + 1 tick slippage.
- Tags: TF, trigger (MSS / IFVG), setup side, accumulation width (pts), sweep depth beyond the range (× A width), bars sweep→entry, risk (pts), rr at entry, opening range vs overnight range (ORB's day type), whether ORB v1.3 traded that day and on which side.

## ICT order block (OB) — spec and OB1 runs
Requested 2026-09-23: the highest-probability ICT order-block entry, by ICT's own criteria: the block is the origin of a displacement leg, the leg breaks structure and leaves an FVG, the block is fresh, the trade is with the bias, and it is in the New York AM killzone. Unlike AMD this is a pullback entry with the day's direction, which is the family that has paid on NQ; the retest variants tested so far (ORB6b-style pullbacks, IVC1e) selected the failing breakouts, so the displacement + BOS requirement is the thing on trial.
Definitions (New York time, detection timeframe TF ∈ {1, 2, 3, 5}, bars from the 1m data):
- **Bias** (default): the direction the opening range (09:30–09:45) broke the overnight range (18:00–09:30): broke the high → bullish, broke the low → bearish, both → either, inside → no trade. Variants: bias from the 15-minute structure (direction of the last 15m fractal break before 09:45); no bias.
- **Bullish order block**: a down-close bar (close < open) such that within the next 3 bars a **displacement leg** occurs: a close above the most recent 3-bar fractal swing high formed since 09:30 (the BOS), and the leg (from the OB bar to the BOS bar) contains a bullish FVG (bar[i-2].high < bar[i].low). The OB zone = that bar's low to its high. Bearish mirror. The OB must form between 09:30 and 11:00; the first qualifying OB in the bias direction is the day's candidate.
- **Entry**: a resting limit at the top of the zone (bullish: OB high), valid for 30 bars after the BOS; filled at the limit when a later bar's low ≤ OB high (bearish mirror). If a bar reaches both the limit and the stop, the trade is stopped (conservative). A zone that is not returned to within 30 bars expires with no trade. Entries until 11:30.
- **Stop** = OB low − 2 ticks (bearish: OB high + 2 ticks). **Target** = 2R. Skip if the OB height < 4 ticks. Flat at 12:00 if open. One trade per day. Costs as ORB.
- Tags: TF, bias source, OB height (pts), leg size / OB height, bars from BOS to fill, whether the limit was at the OB high or midpoint, ORB day type, whether ORB v1.3 traded that day and on which side.
Runs, pre-registered: OB1-TF for TF = 1, 2, 3, 5 with the defaults. On the best TF, one change each: (a) limit at the zone midpoint (ICT "mean threshold") instead of the top; (b) stop = OB low − 0.1 × leg size; (c) target = the nearest session extreme beyond the BOS (bullish: the highest high since 18:00 above the entry; skip if reward:risk < 1.5); (d) hold to 16:00, no target; (e) bias from 15m structure; (f) no bias; (g) OB formation window to 12:00, entries to 13:00; (h) drop the FVG requirement (BOS alone). ## ICT order block + IFVG confirmation (OBI) — spec, awaiting first run
Requested 2026-09-23: same order block as OB1, but no limit order. After price taps the block, wait for an inverted FVG in the trade direction and enter on the inverting close. This tests whether a confirmation requirement removes the failing pullbacks that sank OB1, IVC1e and ORB6b.
Definitions (New York time, TF ∈ {1, 2, 3, 5}, bars from the 1m data). - **Tap**: after the BOS, the first bar whose low ≤ OB high (bearish mirror: high ≥ OB low). The tap must occur within 30 bars of the BOS or the block expires.
- **Pullback gaps**: from the BOS bar up to and including the tap and the bars after it, record bearish FVGs (bar[i-2].low > bar[i].high) formed while price is pulling back (bullish mirror for bearish setups).

Must occur within 20 bars of the tap and before 11:30, else no trade. If price closes below the OB low before confirmation, the block is invalidated (no trade).
