# C17 — NQ 1-minute 9 / 20 / 50 EMA pullback

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C17.py`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- Signal windows "closing 09:45–11:30 and 13:30–15:30" = the 1-minute bar's close time (stamp + 1 minute) lies in the
  window: bars stamped 09:44 … 11:29 and 13:29 … 15:29. Touch and trend bars may be before the window.
- Touch = a bar with low ≤ EMA20 on which the trend is up (the trend must be intact on every bar from the touch to the
  entry, both included).
- "Within 5 bars of the touch" = the touch bar itself or one of the next five bars. A bar that touches EMA20 and
  closes up above EMA9 is therefore its own entry bar.
- "First up bar closing above EMA9" is judged without the time window: if the first such bar after a touch falls
  outside the window, that touch gives no entry.
- Every touching bar is a touch. When several touches lead to the same entry bar, the earliest is "the touch", so
  "the low since the touch" is the lowest low from that bar to the entry bar (at most 6 bars).
- Stop = the nearer to the entry of (1 tick below the low since the touch) and (1 tick below EMA50, EMA50 first rounded
  down to the tick grid; up for shorts).
- Target = signal close ± k × |signal close − stop|, `core.tick_round` (nearest).
- Short mirror: EMA9 < EMA20 < EMA50, close < EMA50; touch = high ≥ EMA20; entry = first down bar closing below EMA9.
- For scale: the in-sample median risk is 8.75 points (mean 11.4); the house cost of a round trip (1 tick each way +
  $2) is 1.5 points.
