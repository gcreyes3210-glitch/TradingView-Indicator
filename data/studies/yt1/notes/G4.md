# G4 - TTrades: Fractal Model day trade (`tools/yt1/s_G4.py`)

Spec: `YT4_SPEC.md` Part 2, G4. Bias required. Hourly gate: the first clock-hour candle opening 06:00 ... 09:00 that
is a bullish candle 2 or candle 3 closure with its low inside candle 1's range, and on `cont` days at or above EQ.
After that hour closes: the first 5-minute bullish CISD confirms; entry at the close of the second; bars closing by
11:30. Stop 1 tick beyond the protected low, target 2R, flat bar, one trade a day. Neighbours (default): 1.5R (nb1),
3R (nb2). Reported: `first` = entry at the first CISD; `retest` = limit at the opening price the second CISD closed
through, resting 30 minutes. Short = mirror.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- (R) **Window: from the gate hour's close to 11:30, with no 08:30 start.** The rule names its own window ("after
  that hour closes ... bars closing by 11:30") and does not use the term "the morning", so the default 08:30-11:00
  definition is not applied (G6, by contrast, writes its floor out: "from 09:30 on"). The CISD bars are the 5-minute
  bars that open at or after the gate hour's end and whose last minute is 11:29 or earlier. **This matters:** of the
  389 base entries, 125 are on bars closing at or before 08:30 and 25 on bars closing after 11:00 (`first`: 220 and 9
  of 411).
- (R) **"The first candle that is ..." = the first of the four candles that meets every condition** (closure in the
  bias direction, low inside candle 1's range, EQ on `cont` days). A closure that fails the location test is passed
  over and a later hour can still be the gate. The other reading (the first closure decides; wrong place = no trade)
  would drop 16 of the 414 gate days.
- (R) Closures = `tt.closure2` / `tt.closure3` on the continuous 60-minute series (`ctx.bars(60)`), so the 06:00
  candle is compared with 05:00 (and 04:00). Part 1's "half its minutes" rule is not applied. Either closure counts;
  the tag shows `c2` when the candle is a candle 2 closure (also when it is both), else `c3`.
- (R) "Its low inside candle 1's range": the gate candle's own low, edges included (l1 <= low <= h1). "At or above
  EQ" on `cont` days: low >= EQ. Shorts: the candle's high, high <= EQ. On `fail` days only the range test applies.
- (R) Nothing cancels the sequence between the first and the second CISD (not a break of the first one's protected
  low, not an opposite CISD). Both are `tt.cisd` on the continuous 5-minute series; the run a CISD closes through
  may have begun inside the gate hour.
- (R) Stop = 1 tick beyond the protected low of the bar entered on (second CISD; `first`: the first CISD).
- (R) `retest`: limit at the opening price of the first candle of the run of down-close candles the second CISD
  closed through. **`tt.cisd` does not return that price**, so `_ref_open()` walks back from the CISD bar to the
  start of that run; it asserts on every use that the CISD bar closed through that open and that the lowest low from
  there to the CISD bar equals `tt.cisd`'s protected low (the assert never fired). The order rests on the 30
  one-minute bars after the CISD bar (it may rest past 11:30, up to 11:59); stop as in base; 2R measured from the
  limit price; no fill = no trade that day. 389 orders, 319 fills.
- Bars acted on at `i_last`; on every order that is the bucket's nominal last minute.

## 5. Odd things
- 741 tradable bias days; a gate on 414 (06:00: 172, 07:00: 92, 08:00: 78, 09:00: 72; candle 2 closure 314, candle
  3 closure only 100); on 197 more days there was a closure in the bias direction but none in the right place.
- `retest` has a positive net with a negative mean R. Both are as printed: R is each trade's net over its own risk,
  so small-risk trades weigh as much as large ones in the mean R and less in the dollars (average risk 19.4 points
  against 27.5 in base). I did not look further into it.
