# B01 - Quick Flip Scalper (`tools/yt1/s_B01.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "Body lies entirely outside the box": strictly beyond the edge (long: max(open, close) < box low; short:
  min(open, close) > box high). A body that ends exactly on the edge is not outside.
- The "previous" bar of an engulfing is the 5-minute bar stamped exactly 5 minutes earlier. For the 09:45 signal bar
  that is the 09:40 bar (inside the box window). If that bucket has no bar the engulfing test is false.
- A doji (body 0) passes the hammer test when it has no upper wick and a range of at least 4 ticks: the three sizes
  are applied exactly as written, with no colour condition on the hammer.
- 09:44 close = close of the last 1-minute bar in 09:30-09:44; 09:30 open = open of the 09:30 bar.
- Entry at the next 5-minute bar's open = `etype="open"` on the signal bar's last 1-minute bar. The stop (signal
  bar's low - 1 tick / high + 1 tick) and the target (box high / low) do not depend on the fill, so the module never
  reads the next bar's open. If that open is at or through the stop the harness rejects the order and, the rule
  being "first signal only", the day has no trade (this never happened in-sample: the 2 of 148 orders that gave no
  trade were both on contract-roll days).
- Daily ATR = the day table's `atr` (through the previous trading day).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
