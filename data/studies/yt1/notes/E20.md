# E20 - Turn of the month, the cash sessions only (`tools/yt1/s_E20.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Trading day** = a weekday that is not a US stock-market holiday on a fixed list written in the module (New
  Year's Day, Martin Luther King Day, Presidents' Day, Good Friday, Memorial Day, Juneteenth from 2022, Independence
  Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas; a Saturday holiday is kept on the Friday, a Sunday one on the Monday,
  except New Year's Day on a Saturday, which is not moved). The list is computed from the year alone.
- **Last N of the month**, decided before the 09:30 open of day D: fewer than N calendar trading days remain after D
  in D's month. This count comes from the list only; no bar is read.
- **First M of the month**: fewer than M trading days lie before D in D's month, an earlier date counting only if it
  is on the calendar AND the bars show a cash session on it (so a past unscheduled closure is not counted). Dates
  before the first day the bars cover are taken from the calendar.
- D itself must be a calendar trading day with a cash session. Each such day is one trade: signalled at the close
  of the last 1-minute bar before 09:30, entered at the 09:30 bar's open, no stop, no target, exit at the flat bar
  (the early flat bar on the half days 3 July, the day after Thanksgiving, 24 December); R unit 0.1 x daily ATR(14).
- Tags: `T-4` .. `T-1` = fourth-to-last .. last trading day of the month, `T+1` .. `T+3` = first .. third.

## 2. The calendar against the cash days in the bars (the mismatch that was asked for)
`s_E20.calendar_check(ctx)`, 2019-06-03 -> 2022-12-30: 904 calendar trading days, 926 cash days in the day table.
- Calendar trading days without a cash session in the bars: **none**.
- Cash days in the bars that the list calls a holiday: **22**, every one a futures-exchange half session ending
  13:00 on a US holiday: Independence Day (2019-07-04, 2020-07-03, 2021-07-05, 2022-07-04), Labor Day (2019-09-02,
  2020-09-07, 2021-09-06, 2022-09-05), Thanksgiving (2019-11-28, 2020-11-26, 2021-11-25, 2022-11-24), Martin Luther
  King Day (2020-01-20, 2021-01-18, 2022-01-17), Presidents' Day (2020-02-17, 2021-02-15, 2022-02-21), Memorial
  Day (2020-05-25, 2021-05-31, 2022-05-30), Juneteenth (2022-06-20). Good Friday, Christmas and New Year's Day have
  no cash session at all.

**So "the cash days in the day table" and "weekdays minus the listed holidays" are not the same set, and one had to
be chosen.** The module uses the list: a holiday half session is not a trading day, is not counted and is not
traded (the source is a stock-index study on daily bars, where those days do not exist). This is the one place
where the module departs from the instruction that the month's trading days are the day table's cash days. Size
of the difference, in days only (no result was computed for the other reading): of the 301 turn-of-month days
in-sample, 10 differ. The day-table reading would trade 2019-09-02, 2019-11-28, 2020-07-03, 2020-11-26, 2021-05-31,
2021-07-05, 2021-11-25, 2022-05-30, 2022-07-04, 2022-09-05 (holiday half sessions) in place of 2019-09-05,
2019-11-25, 2020-07-06, 2020-11-24, 2021-05-25, 2021-07-06, 2021-11-24, 2022-05-25, 2022-07-06, 2022-09-06.

## 4. Hand check
- Holiday list printed for 2019-2026 and read against the exchange calendar (Good Friday 2019-04-19, 2020-04-10,
  2021-04-02, 2022-04-15, 2023-04-07, 2024-03-29, 2025-04-18, 2026-04-03; Independence Day kept on 2020-07-03,
  2021-07-05 and 2026-07-03; Christmas on 2021-12-24 and 2022-12-26; Juneteenth on 2022-06-20; no New Year holiday
  in 2022).
- Month by month: 2019-07 trades 1, 2, 3 (T+1..3, the 3rd a half day) and 26, 29, 30, 31; 2019-11 trades 1, 4, 5 and
  25, 26, 27, 29 (Thanksgiving the 28th skipped, the 29th a half day); 2021-05 ends 25, 26, 27, 28 (Memorial Day the
  31st); 2022-07 starts 1, 5, 6 (the 4th a holiday); 2022-12 ends 27, 28, 29, 30. 43 trades on each of T-4 .. T-1 and
  42 on each of T+1 .. T+3 (June 2019's first three days have no daily ATR): 298.
- The module's day set, built looking forward from the calendar, equals the set built in hindsight by ranking the
  realised trading days of each month (301 days; the 3 without an order are the no-ATR days).
- Trades: 2019-10-01 (T+1) 09:30 open 7797.75 -> fill 7798.00, 15:59 close 7701.50 -> exit 7701.25, -$195.50,
  0.1 ATR 13.105 -> -7.46 R. 2021-05-26 (T-3) fill 13686.25 (open 13686.00), exit 13701.50 (close 13701.75), +$28.50.
  2022-12-27 (T-4) fill 11052.25 (open 11052.00), exit 10918.50 (close 10918.75), -$269.50, -4.45 R.
- Look-ahead: `--check` PASS (its "150-day window" note is the daily ATR warm-up on the window, as in E19; dates
  and tags on the windows match the full run). That test keeps the bars' dates, so it cannot see a day list read
  from the future; the orders were therefore also rebuilt on bars cut at eight mid-session times, four of them in a
  month's last week (2019-11-26, 2020-12-29, 2021-05-27, 2022-06-28), and every order before the cut day was
  identical to the full run's for base and nb2.
