# K-E01 - Hour Stats, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_E01.py`)

Claim (nqstats.com/hour_stats.html, video aQzDN7jXXyc, the TradingView port): after the first breach of the previous
hour's high / low, the hour's open trades before the hour ends: 61.5 % overall (17,701 breaches, 2016-2025); 13:00
hour, first 20 minutes 71 %; 09:00 hour, first 20 minutes 87.4 %. No pass / fail: measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions (the source's, research note E entry 1; all fixed before the first run)
- Hours 08:00 ... 15:00 of every cash day in the day table (roll and early-close days included: everything is inside
  one hour pair; the contract never changes between the two hours in-sample). Previous hour = the clock hour before
  (07:00-07:59 for 08:00).
- The hour opens strictly inside the previous hour's range.
- Breach = the first 1-minute bar of the hour trading >= 1 tick beyond the previous hour's high or low. Only the
  first counts. A first breaching bar beyond both levels is counted apart (15 hours).
- Segment = the 20-minute third of the hour the breaching bar is in.
- Reversion = the hour's open touched or crossed after the breach, before the hour ends (the site: a wick touching
  counts). Exact on the bars after the breaching bar. On the breaching bar itself the order is not in 1-minute data,
  so three figures: **headline** = the house intrabar path (open -> nearer extreme -> farther extreme -> close) on
  that bar; lower bound = later bars only; upper bound = any touch of the open on the breaching bar (path reading on
  the hour's first bar, which opens at the hour's open). The three differ by 2.4 points at most overall.
- Difference from the trade rule E01: this counts every first breach in the whole hour, at any distance, including
  those in the hour's first minute (1,235 of 6,393); E01 trades only the first 20 minutes, levels >= 0.10 % away,
  and cannot trade a first-minute breach.
