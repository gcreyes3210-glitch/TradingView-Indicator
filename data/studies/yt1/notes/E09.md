# E09 - noise-area intraday momentum (`tools/yt1/s_E09.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Decision bars = the 1-minute bars 09:59, 10:29, ... 15:29 of a cash day, before its flat bar (early-close days stop
  earlier). "Above" / "below" are strict and tested on that bar's close.
- sigma at a minute = the mean of |close of that minute's bar / that day's 09:30 open - 1| over the previous N cash
  days that have a bar at that minute, today not included (an early-close day drops out of the afternoon minutes).
- Previous close = the previous cash day's last RTH close (`pdc`). VWAP = `ind.session_vwap` (hlc3 x volume) on the
  1-minute bars from 09:30, read at the decision bar.
- The state machine acts once per decision close on the state it had before it: a long that is closed is not
  re-opened at the same close; if that close is also below the lower boundary a short is opened at it (1 such
  reversal in-sample in the base rule, 17 in `opp`).
- As the spec writes it, opening a position needs only the close beyond the boundary, not beyond the VWAP as well.
  So a position can be opened beyond the boundary but on the wrong side of the VWAP and is then closed at the next
  decision. Coded as written.
- Each position is one order: market at the decision close, no price stop, `exit_sig` true only at decision closes
  where the exit condition holds, otherwise the flat bar. R unit = 0.1 x daily ATR(14).
- `opp`: a long is closed only by a close below the lower boundary, where a short is opened; mirror.
- Orders are simulated one by one with `core.simulate` (a reversal's entry is on the previous trade's exit bar);
  roll days and no-ATR days are left out in `trades()`; an assertion checks that positions never overlap.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.

## 3. Hand check
Four days replayed with separate pandas code (sigma from the 14 previous days' bars, boundaries, cumulative VWAP,
the state machine) and compared with the module's trades; all four agree in every entry and exit:
- 2019-10-11 (open 7843.00, previous close 7754.25): 09:59 close 7873.25 > upper 7869.43 -> long 7873.50. Held while
  the close stayed above max(upper, VWAP); 11:59 close 7885.50 < upper 7890.07 -> out at 7885.25, +0.74 R. 14:29
  close 7904.25 > upper 7903.01 -> long again, flat bar 7851.75, -3.69 R.
- 2020-10-23: 09:59 close 11614.75 < lower 11617.49 -> short 11614.50; 12:29 close 11583.75 > lower 11582.70
  (min(lower, VWAP) = lower) -> out at 11584.00, +1.05 R. No later close beyond a boundary.
- 2022-05-09: no trade at 09:59 / 10:29 (closes inside); three shorts 10:59 -> 11:59, 12:59 -> 13:29, 13:59 -> 14:59,
  each closed by a close back above the lower boundary; -0.49, -0.68, -0.85 R.
- 2022-10-27: 09:59 close 11291.50 < lower 11321.70 -> short; 10:29 close 11431.50 > min(lower 11284.41, VWAP
  11371.39) -> out, -3.95 R (no price stop between decisions, as the rule is written).
- The look-ahead test's note "not reproduced on the 150-day window" is the ATR's warm-up in the R unit only: on two
  of the sampled windows the orders have the same times and sides as on the full bars, `r_pts` differs by 0.2-1.1 pt.
- In-sample: 833 orders, 817 trades on 540 days (542 closed at a decision, 275 at the flat bar), 16 orders on roll /
  no-ATR days. 230 of the 817 entries are at the 10:00 close.
