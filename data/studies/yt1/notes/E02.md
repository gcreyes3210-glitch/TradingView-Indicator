# E02 - NQ Stats IB breaks, trade the bias (`tools/yt1/s_E02.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- IB = the 1-minute bars 09:30 .. 10:29 (neighbours: .. 10:14, .. 10:44); the entry is a market order at the close of
  the bar stamped at the IB's last minute (that bar exists on every in-sample day).
- "Set before": the bar that **first** set the IB low is earlier than the bar that first set the IB high; both in
  the same bar = no trade (never happened). "Above the midpoint" is strict (close == midpoint = no trade).
- Short mirror: close below the midpoint and the high set before the low; target 1 tick beyond the IB low, stop 1
  tick beyond the IB high.
- The source's extra condition for its statistic (the 10:30 bar opens inside the IB) is not in the spec text and is
  not known at the 10:29 close; not used (it held on every in-sample day, see K-E02).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
