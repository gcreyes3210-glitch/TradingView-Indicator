# D05 - Absorption candle, POC in the wick, delta flip, at a level (Thraxx)  (tools/yt1/s_D05.py)

Data check of the footprint (grid, alignment with MNQ, the six other-contract days): notes/D03.md, section 0.
No base trade falls on the three days after a roll where the footprint is another contract month.

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Trade count** as D03: any number a day, one position at a time (`base`, `nb1`, `nb2`, `nolevel`); the common
  rule's one trade per day is coded as `day1`, `day1_nb1`, `day1_nb2`, `day1_nolevel` (not in the spec's list).
- **"Within 0.05 x ATR of (or through)"** a low level L: bar low <= L + 0.05 x ATR, with no limit on how far below
  (mirror: bar high >= H - 0.05 x ATR). Either level is enough (pdl or onl; pdh or onh, from the day table).
  This is loose on trend days: of the 501 base orders, 131 have the bar's extreme within +/- 0.05 ATR of a level
  and 370 qualify only as "through" (the bar is beyond a level by more than 0.05 ATR, e.g. 2022-09-13, 400 points
  under the previous day's low). The neighbours move only the near side, which is why they differ little.
- **Bar from MNQ**: low / high / body from the MNQ 5-minute bar of those five minutes; the footprint's
  highest-volume price is compared with that body, and used for the limit, as an MNQ price unchanged.
- **Highest-volume price** = the price with the largest buy + sell. A tie for it = no signal (174 of the 9,630
  sample bars that have a previous bar). "Below its body" = strictly below min(open, close); mirror strictly above max(open, close).
- **Previous bar** = the footprint bar that opened five minutes earlier, 09:30 or later, so the 09:30 bar is never
  a signal. Positive / negative delta are strict.
- **"Resting 15 minutes"** = the 15 one-minute bars T+5 ... T+19 after the signal bar's close.
- **Stop** = 1 tick beyond the MNQ bar's low (high). An order whose limit is not on the trade's side of its stop is
  not placed. **Target** = limit price +/- 2 x |limit price - stop|.
- **One position at a time with resting orders**: the same `one_at_a_time` as D03 (notes/D03.md). In-sample 501
  orders, 381 fill when simulated alone, 363 trades, no same-bar pair (`core.run_orders` would give n 362).
