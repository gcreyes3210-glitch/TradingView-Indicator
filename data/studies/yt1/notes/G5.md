# G5 - TTrades 4-hour power of three  (tools/yt1/s_G5.py)

Rule as coded: bias from `tt.bias`; the 06:00 (10:00) 4-hour candle is a candle 2 closure (`tt.closure2`) in the bias
direction against the 02:00 (06:00) candle; in the 10:00 (14:00) candle the first 15-minute CISD (`tt.cisd`) in the
bias direction; entry at that bar's close, stop 1 tick beyond the protected low / high, target 2R (nb1 1.5R, nb2 3R),
exit at the 4-hour candle's last bar or the flat bar. No "reported" variants are registered for G5.

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **(R) 4-hour candles** are built from the 1-minute bars of the New York clock blocks 02:00-05:59, 06:00-09:59,
  10:00-13:59, 14:00-17:59 on the cash date (`ctx.span`, wall clock). A gate candle and the candle before it must each
  hold at least half their minutes (120), which is Part 1's sentence "a candle needs at least half its minutes to
  count" carried into Part 2. In-sample 6 candles fail it, all on the limit-down mornings of March 2020: the 02:00
  and 06:00 candles of 2020-03-09 (116 and 61 bars), 03-16 (10, 25) and 03-18 (36, 41). Those gates are skipped.
- **(R) "The previous candle"** of the gate candle is the clock block immediately before it (02:00 for the 06:00
  gate, 06:00 for the 10:00 gate). If that block does not count, there is no gate.
- **(R) The CISD is read on the continuous clock-aligned 15-minute series** (`ctx.bars(15)`), so the run of
  down-close candles it closes through may have started before the traded 4-hour candle opened; only the CISD bar
  itself has to open inside it (10:00 ... 13:45, or 14:00 onward). Hand check 4 below is such a case (run = the 13:45
  bar, CISD = the 14:00 bar).
- **(R) "First" means first, traded or not.** One order per traded 4-hour candle: its first CISD in the bias
  direction. If that bar closes at or after the exit bar (the 13:45 bar, the 15:45 bar, or any bar after the flat
  bar) the harness drops the order and no later CISD is taken. 33 of 364 in-sample orders end this way.
- **(R) The default "morning" window and "one trade a day" do not apply**: the rule names its own window (the traded
  4-hour candle) and its own count (one per 4-hour candle, so up to two a day: 10:00 and 14:00).
- **(R) The 14:00 candle** runs to 16:59, so its time exit is always the flat bar (15:59, earlier on short days).
- **Target** = CISD close +/- k x |CISD close - stop|, before slippage, on the tick grid (YT1 common rules).
- **Decision bar** = `i_last` of the 15-minute bar (the 1-minute bar that closes it), as the harness defines it.
- His shallow-wick judgement is not applied (the spec says so).

## 4. Counts and anything odd
- 926 in-sample cash days; bias +1 on 424, -1 on 325, none on 177.
- 364 orders (L10 119, L14 95, S10 80, S14 70): 2 on roll / no-ATR days, 33 signalled at or after their exit bar,
  329 trades (L10 118, L14 79, S10 78, S14 54). Exits: 166 time, 119 stop, 44 target. At most 2 trades in a day.
- Average risk 53.9 points (median 43.75): the 15-minute protected low is far away, so half of all trades end on the
  clock rather than at the stop or target.
- Nothing found wrong in core.py, ind.py, run.py or tt.py.
