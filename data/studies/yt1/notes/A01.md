# A01 - JadeCap Silver Bullet  (tools/yt1/s_A01.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **When the direction is read.** At the close of each FVG's third 5-minute bar, from the sweeps whose 1-minute bar
  is at or before that close. "The first FVG in the trade direction" = the first FVG whose side equals the direction
  read at its own close.
- **No level swept yet.** An FVG that completes before any level has been swept has no direction and is passed over,
  exactly like one pointing the other way; a later FVG can still be the signal once a level is swept.
- **A high and a low swept in the same 1-minute bar** (and that bar is the most recent sweep): no direction at that
  moment, the FVG is passed over.
- **Each level is swept once**, at the first bar that trades >= 1 tick beyond it from its start time (00:00 Asia,
  05:00 London, 10:00 for the 09:00 hour). "Most recent" = the latest of those first-sweep bars.
- **Asia = 20:00-23:59 of the previous calendar day** (Sunday evening for a Monday).
- **The resting limit is not cancelled** by a later sweep that flips the direction; it rests to the 10:59 bar.
- **One order a day**: the first qualifying FVG, whether or not its limit fills. A signal whose third bar is the
  10:55 bar is known at the 10:59 close, so its limit has no bar left to rest on and cannot fill.
- **Target** = limit price +/- k x |limit price - stop| (the harness takes a fixed target; a limit filled at a better
  open keeps it). 1.5R targets are put on the tick grid with `core.tick_round` (nearest).
- **`pm`**: the same three ranges and sweep start times; only the window moves to 14:00-15:00 (limit to 14:59).
- **Candles 1, 2, 3** are adjacent rows of the 5-minute series inside the window (a 5-minute slot without a trade has
  no bar).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was run once and not changed.

Harness observation (no effect on the harness, worked around inside my module only): `ctx.span(date, "20:00", ...)`
adds minutes to local midnight in absolute time, so on the two daylight-saving Sundays a year it returns 21:00-00:59
(spring) or 19:00-22:59 (autumn) instead of 20:00-23:59. Evidence: for Monday 2020-03-09 `ctx.span(2020-03-08,
"20:00", "24:00")` covers 21:00 -> 00:58; for Monday 2020-11-02 it covers 19:00 -> 22:59. The Asia range in
`s_A01._wall_span` is therefore built from wall-clock timestamps. Weekday spans are unaffected.
