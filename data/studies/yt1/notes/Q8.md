# Q8 - VWAP 2-sd reversion scalp with a tick bracket, M12b (`tools/yt1/s_Q8.py`)

Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q8 and "For Q7 and Q8, stated in advance". Claim check that goes with it:
`notes/K-Q78.md`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **VWAP and VWAP sd** exactly as the shared definitions: anchored at the day's 09:30 bar, sum(hlc3 x volume) /
  sum(volume) on 1-minute bars through the bar just closed; sd = sqrt(sum(volume x hlc3^2) / sum(volume) - VWAP^2)
  (`ind.session_vwap` run on each day's bars from 09:30; the same arithmetic as `Aceflw_Levels.pine`). The signal
  bar's own close is compared with the VWAP and sd that include that bar.
- **Entry price = the entry reference price = the signal bar's close.** The target is 20 ticks (5.00 points) from
  that close; the stop is 8 ticks beyond the signal bar's low (long) / high (short). Both are fixed at entry.
- **"Or VWAP at the signal if nearer"**: when the signal bar's VWAP is nearer to the close than the tick target, the
  target is that VWAP put on the tick grid (nearest tick; the common rule rounds targets to the tick). Kept in every
  variant, `x4` included (the variants change only the tick numbers and the sd multiple). In sample the VWAP is the
  target in 6 of 5,199 base orders, 1 of nb1, 0 of nb2 and 237 of x4.
- An order whose rounded target is not beyond the signal close would not be a bracket and is not sent. It never
  happens in sample (0 orders).
- "At or below VWAP - 2 sd" includes equality; "above its open" is strict. Short = the mirror: close at or above
  VWAP + 2 sd and below its open; stop 8 ticks above the bar's high; target 20 ticks below the close or VWAP if
  nearer.
- **Trades per day.** "One position at a time" (Part 4 preamble) read as replacing YT1's "one trade per day": every
  signal that comes while flat is taken; the harness also skips a signal on the bar a trade exits on.
- Signal bars 11:00 ... 13:59. Early-close days are traded up to their flat bar (the entry does not exclude them).
  Roll days and days without a daily ATR are dropped by `run_orders`.
- nb1 = 6-tick stop beyond the bar's extreme with a 10-tick target, 2 sd. nb2 = 2.5 sd with the base bracket.
