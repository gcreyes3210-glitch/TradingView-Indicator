# YT2 T16–T29 — coder notes

Modules `tools/yt1/s_T16.py` … `s_T29.py`, each a trigger only, in the shape of `s_T00.py`; the frame is `frame_t.py`
(not edited). Every reading below was fixed before the first run. No coding error was found after the first run and
nothing was changed after seeing results: the numbers at the bottom are the first and only run.

## How the indicators were checked (all rules)
1. **Plain-loop recomputation, first 3,000 five-minute bars (2019-06-02 → 2019-06-17), every bar compared.** Each
   indicator was recomputed from the raw O/H/L/C values by a plain Python loop written separately from `ind.py` and
   from the modules (windowed sums for SMA / WMA / rolling highs and lows, explicit recursions for EMA and Wilder
   averages, Supertrend transcribed from TradingView's reference script with its own sign convention, McGinley in
   40-digit decimals in the rearranged form MD + (close − MD) × MD⁴ ÷ (n × close⁴)). Largest difference over all bars:
   0 for SMA, WMA, RSI, Bollinger middle, Supertrend line and direction (79 flips), Ichimoku cloud; ≤ 6e-11 for EMA,
   DEMA, TEMA, HMA, MACD, Stochastic %K, McGinley; ≤ 1e-13 for ADX and KST. The NaN warm-up pattern is the same
   everywhere (first values: SMA200 / WMA200 / EMA200 bar 199, HMA100 bar 108, DEMA200 bar 398, TEMA200 bar 597,
   ADX bar 27, cloud bar 77, KST bar 44 and its signal bar 52).
2. **At the hand-checked trades (two per rule, 28 in all, 2019 → 2022):** the same loops run on the 6,000 bars ending
   at the signal bar reproduce the module's values at bars k−1 and k to ≤ 6e-10, and the trigger condition was read
   off the printed bars. For every one of the 28 trades the entry is the signal bar's close (plus the harness's 1 tick
   of slippage), the stop is the 10-bar swing or the own level 1 tick beyond on the tick grid, the tag is right, and
   the exit bar touches the stop / target or is the 15:59 flat bar.
3. `any_prev` (the "previous 10 bars" test), `ind.crossed_up` and the T22 before / after logic were each compared with
   a loop on the same 3,000 bars: identical.
4. `python3 tools/yt1/run.py <ID> --check --cuts 8`: PASS for all fourteen (8 of 8 sampled orders identical under a
   mirrored future, each).

## Per rule
**T16 — SMA50 crosses SMA200.** SMA(n)[k] = mean of closes k−n+1 … k (`ind.sma`); long = `ind.crossed_up(sma50, sma200)`,
short = `crossed_dn`. No reading. Hand check: 2019-08-13 10:05 bar (7565.95 ≤ 7569.03 → 7570.22 > 7569.90, long) and
2020-06-02 11:20 bar (short).

**T17 — WMA50 crosses WMA200, own stop WMA200.** WMA(n)[k] = Σ j × close[k−n+j] (j = 1 … n) ÷ (n(n+1)/2), newest close
weight n (`ind.wma`). The module hands the frame WMA200 at every bar as `stop_l` and `stop_s`; the frame reads it on
the cross bar. Stop kind in the base trades: **570 own, 4 swing** (3-minute: 782 / 5; 15-minute: 216 / 0). Hand
check: 2021-04-07 10:55 bar, WMA200 13574.57 → stop 13574.25; 2021-12-03 10:20 bar, short, WMA200 15966.02 → stop 15966.50.

**T18 — DEMA50 crosses DEMA200.** DEMA(n) = 2 × e1 − e2, e1 = EMA(close, n), e2 = EMA(e1, n); EMA = TradingView's
`ta.ema`, alpha 2/(n+1), seeded with the SMA of the first n values of its input (`ind.ema`, `ind.ema_nan`). Written
in the module (`dema`). No reading.

**T19 — TEMA50 crosses TEMA200.** TEMA(n) = 3 × e1 − 3 × e2 + e3, e3 = EMA(e2, n), same EMA. Written in the module
(`tema`). No reading.

**T20 — Hull MA(100) crosses SMA200.** HMA(100) = WMA(2 × WMA(close, 50) − WMA(close, 100), 10) (`ind.hma`,
10 = round(√100)). No reading.

**T21 — McGinley Dynamic(9) crosses McGinley Dynamic(21).** MD[0] = close[0]; MD[k] = MD[k−1] + (close[k] − MD[k−1]) ÷
(n × (close[k] ÷ MD[k−1])⁴), a plain loop in the module (`mcginley`). Reading: the seed is the first close of the
series, as the spec says (TradingView's own script starts from an EMA instead, to my knowledge; the seed is forgotten
within the warm-up: a run seeded 6,000 bars before each hand-checked trade gives the same values to the last digit).

**T22 — opens and closes above the SMA20 of highs, after a bar that did not.** SMA20 of highs / lows by `ind.sma`, the
bar's own high / low included. Readings: "above" is tested against the band value of the same bar; "after one that
did not" = the previous bar did not both open and close beyond its own band value, and the band existed on that
previous bar (the form of the pattern module's Keltner trigger). Short: opens and closes below the SMA20 of lows.

**T23 — RSI crosses above 70 with ADX ≥ 25, own stop EMA21.** RSI(14) = `ind.rsi` (Wilder); ADX(14, 14) =
`ind.dmi(h, l, c, 14, 14)[2]`, read on the signal bar; EMA21 of the close = `ind.ema`. Short: RSI crosses below 30 with
ADX ≥ 25 (ADX has no direction, so it is not mirrored). Stop kind in the base trades: **856 own, 0 swing**
(3-minute 1,134 / 0; 15-minute 411 / 0).

**T24 — close crosses above the Bollinger middle with RSI below 30 on one of the previous 10 bars.** Middle = SMA20 of
the close (`ind.bollinger(c, 20, 2)[0]`); cross = close[k] > mid[k] and close[k−1] ≤ mid[k−1]; "previous 10 bars" =
bars k−10 … k−1 by a cumulative count, the signal bar not counted. Short: close crosses below the middle with RSI
above 70 on one of the previous 10 bars. Fewest trades of the batch (196).

**T25 — RSI crosses above 70 with a green cloud.** Cloud written in the module (`cloud`) to the C19a definition:
conversion = midpoint of the 9-bar high / low, base = midpoint of the 26-bar high / low, span A = their average,
span B = midpoint of the 52-bar high / low, cloud at bar k = the spans computed at bar k − 26. Green = cloud A > cloud
B at the signal bar; short = RSI crosses below 30 with cloud A < cloud B. The arrays are identical to
`s_C19a.ichimoku`. Remark, not acted on: TradingView's chart draws the spans 25 bars forward (displacement − 1), so
the spec's cloud is one bar older than the one on a TradingView chart.

**T26 — Know Sure Thing crosses its signal with both below zero.** Written in the module (`kst`): ROC(n)[k] = 100 ×
(close[k] − close[k−n]) ÷ close[k−n]; KST = SMA10(ROC10) + 2 × SMA10(ROC15) + 3 × SMA10(ROC20) + 4 × SMA15(ROC30);
signal = SMA9(KST). Reading: "both below zero" is tested on the cross bar (KST < 0 and signal < 0 there). Short:
crosses below with both above zero.

**T27 — RSI crosses above 70 while Supertrend is up, no EMA filter, own stop the Supertrend line.**
`ind.supertrend(h, l, c, 10, 3.0)`: direction +1 = up; direction and line are read on the signal bar. Short: RSI
crosses below 30 with direction −1. `orders` calls `frame_t.orders(ctx, tf, trigger, ema_filter=False)`. Stop kind
in the base trades: **1,100 own, 0 swing** (3-minute 1,365 / 0; 15-minute 615 / 0), as expected since the line is
by construction on the losing side of the close.

**T28 — MACD crosses above its signal while Supertrend is up.** `ind.macd(c, 12, 26, 9)` (signal = EMA9 of the MACD
line) and the Supertrend direction on the signal bar. Short: crosses below with direction −1. No reading.

**T29 — MACD crosses above its signal with %K below 20 on one of the previous 10 bars and the low above EMA200.**
Readings: %K is the %K line of Stochastic(14, 3, 3), i.e. the SMA3 of the raw 14-bar stochastic
(`ind.stoch(h, l, c, 14, 3, 3)[0]`); "previous 10 bars" = bars k−10 … k−1; EMA200 = `ind.ema(close, 200)`, the low
/ high test is on the signal bar. Short: MACD crosses below with %K above 80 on one of the previous 10 bars and the
bar's high below EMA200.
