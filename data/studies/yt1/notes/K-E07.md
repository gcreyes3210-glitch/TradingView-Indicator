# K-E07 - NQ Stats RTH Breaks, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_E07.py`)

Claim (current nqstats.com page): open above the previous RTH high: closes above it 69.9 %, previous RTH low not
breached 88.1 %. Open below: 59.5 %, 90.4 %. Open inside: neither side 17.7 %, one 74.0 %, both 8.3 %.
No pass / fail: measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Previous RTH high / low = the previous cash day's 09:30-16:00 high / low (day table `pdh` / `pdl`). Open above =
  the 09:30 bar's open strictly above that high; below = strictly below that low; anything else is inside.
- Breach, the headline: the study's own definition from the sourcing note, "a bar extending more than 1 tick beyond
  the level", i.e. the session high >= level + 2 ticks or the session low <= level - 2 ticks. The spec line says
  "does not trade the previous RTH low"; the two wordings were both computed (plus "1 tick beyond") and **all three
  give identical figures in-sample**: no in-sample day has its session extreme at a previous level or exactly one
  tick past it (nearest: one tick short on 2020-04-30, two ticks past on 2020-06-02 and 2020-06-19).
- Closes above / below = the last close of the cash session strictly beyond the previous high / low.
- Days: every cash day with a previous cash day, except the 15 contract-roll days (the previous day's levels belong
  to the other contract). With the roll days left in, and without the 29 early-close days: beside the headline.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None; the headline is the first run's. Added after the first run, as an extra labelled line only: the figures
without early-close days AND the day after one. The E08 hand check showed that the day table counts holiday
half-sessions (for example 2021-05-31, 2022-11-24) as cash days, so on the day after one the "previous RTH range" is
a 09:30-13:00 holiday range. No figure moves by more than about 2.5 points.

## 3. Hand check
Six days (two per class, seed 4): the previous day's RTH high / low, the 09:30 open and the session's high, low and
last close recomputed from the 1-minute bars; all equal the day-table values the module uses, and the outcomes follow:
- 2021-08-23 above (open 15127.25 > 15100.00): close 15306.50 above the high; low 15124.75, previous low 14959.25 held.
- 2022-09-12 above (12650.00 > 12614.25): close 12742.75 above; low 12637.50, previous low 12415.75 held.
- 2021-08-19 below (14774.50 < 14842.25): close 14928.75, not below the previous low; high 14999.00 < 15037.00, held.
- 2022-10-26 below (11474.50 < 11514.75): close 11453.25 below; high 11703.50 < 11727.50, held.
- 2019-09-23 inside: high 7865.25 < 7942.25; low 7807.75 is 14 ticks under 7811.25 -> one side (low).
- 2022-11-30 inside: high 12053.00 > 11638.75; low 11498.00 > 11465.00 -> one side (high).
