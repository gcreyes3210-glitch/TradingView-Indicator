# K-D08 - Delta divergence (Trader Dale), claim check  (tools/yt1/k_D08.py)

Spec: "New-session-high up bars with negative delta against the rest: mean move over the next six bars in units of
the bar-range average." No figure is published; the direction claimed is that an up bar at a high with negative
delta warns of a reversal, so its mean move should be lower than the rest's. No pass / fail.

Data check of the footprint: notes/D03.md, section 0. The new-high test reads footprint prices; the up-bar test,
the move and the range read MNQ bars, each as a difference inside one instrument, so the other-contract days do
not distort it.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- New session high and event bars exactly as K-D07: footprint prices, earlier bars from 09:30, events 09:35 ... 11:25.
- Up bar = the MNQ 5-minute bar of those five minutes closes above its open. Delta = sum(buy) - sum(sell) of the
  footprint bar. Divergence = an up bar with delta < 0.
- **"The rest"** (headline) = the other new-session-high UP bars (delta >= 0), so the groups differ only in the
  sign of delta. The wider reading, every other new-session-high bar, is printed beside it
  (`rest_all_new_extremes`).
- Move = MNQ close six bars later minus the event bar's MNQ close; when fewer than six footprint bars remain, the
  day's last footprint bar (11:30, closing 11:35). `full_window_only` = events with all six bars.
- Bar-range average = mean high - low of the day's MNQ 5-minute bars from 09:30 up to and including the event bar.
- Low mirror: new-session-low down bars with positive delta against the other new-session-low down bars.
- Highs and lows are reported with the raw sign of the move. Pooled = both, with the move signed toward the
  reversal (- move after a high, + move after a low): positive = price went back.
- All 419 footprint days are counted.

None. The module was not changed after its first run.

## 3. Hand check
- The event table was rebuilt a second way (pandas group-bys and a pandas 5-minute resample, no shared code): the
  means and counts are identical (highs +0.3162 n 207, +0.0689 n 1190, +0.0901 n 1630; lows -0.0904 n 192,
  -0.1753 n 1005, -0.1415 n 1427).
- 2021-03-04, 09:35 bar by hand: new high 12765.00 (09:30 high 12748.00); MNQ 12746.00 -> 12752.50 (up), delta
  -378: divergence. Six bars later the 10:05 bar closes 12541.00; ranges so far 98.75 and 35.00, mean 66.875;
  move = (12541.00 - 12752.50) / 66.875 = -3.163. Matches.
- Same day, 11:00 bar: new high 12767.00, up, delta +48: rest. Six bars later = the 11:30 bar, close 12701.50;
  move (12701.50 - 12754.75) / mean range = -0.855. Matches.
- Same day, 10:05 bar (low mirror): new low 12518.50, MNQ 12543.75 -> 12541.00 (down), delta +511: divergence;
  the 10:35 bar closes 12678.00; move +2.160. Matches. The 10:10 bar also sets a new low but closes up, so it is
  outside the mirror population and counts only under "every other new extreme".
