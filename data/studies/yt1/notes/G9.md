## 1. Readings (R) - all fixed before the grid was evaluated
1. **Decision bar** = the last existing 1-minute bar of the N-minute bucket (`i_last`); the window is judged on that
   bar's New York clock minute, both ends inclusive (ldn 02:00-04:59, am 08:30-10:59, open 09:30-10:59, sb
   10:00-10:59, pm 13:30-14:59). 14 five-minute and 4 fifteen-minute decision bars are not on the bucket's last clock
   minute (the minute is missing). A long and a short CISD on one bar: the long first (0 cases).
2. **Day of a London signal.** Cash date D of the decision bar = trading date D (18:00 on D-1 onwards); cand1, bias
   and EQ are `tt.bias(D)`, the candle that closed at 17:00 on D-1. Asserted: every decision bar's cash date equals
   its trading date.
3. **`sess` levels** = lowest low / highest high from the first bar of the trading day (18:00) through 08:29; for a
   row of the London window through 01:59. Frozen: later bars never move them (also for `pm`).
4. **M.** `d18` = YT5's `O below` (open of the first bar of the trading day). `mid`: open of the first 1-minute bar at
   or after 00:00 of the cash date; it is the 00:01 bar on 9 dates (1,046 signals; 4 tradable days, 469 tradable
   signals: 2019-07-03, 07-16, 09-11, 10-07) and is never unknown. `both`: that and the open of the first bar at or
   after 08:30, used only once that bar has opened (at or before the decision bar): a later bar on 3 dates (2020-03-13
   08:35, 03-16 09:30, 03-18 08:31; 159 signals, 115 tradable on 2 days). In the London window the 08:30 open is
   never known, `m_both` is false and those combinations are not in the grid. Strict inequalities.
5. **B `inv`.** Hour candles = the clock-hour blocks of `g8_signals._htf` (YT5 reading 4); "completed" = its last
   1-minute bar is at or before the decision bar (so the hour that ends on the decision bar counts); "since 18:00" =
   the candles of this trading day; a candle with fewer than 30 one-minute bars is not used (the YT5 detail on
   higher-timeframe candles; 53 of 21,204 hour candles; allowing them changes 6 signals, none tradable). "Beyond EQ"
   is strict. `tt.bias` must be +1 or -1 (it is 0 on its `roll` rows) and the trade's side is the opposite. `B any /
   cont / fail` are unchanged: the trade agrees with the bias.
6. **R `pd_brk` / `sess_brk`** = protected low strictly above the high level (cand1's high; the session high of the
   row's window). Nothing else is required: the words in the spec's parenthesis describe what that condition means.
7. **cand1 on `tt.bias` `roll` rows is missing** (YT5 reading 3 of notes/G8.md, kept): E, R `pd`, R `pd_brk` false, `pdx`
   does not qualify; `liq` leaves cand1's level out and takes the nearest of the other two. This touches the third
   day after a roll (16 tradable in-sample days, 2,009 tradable signals, 1,590 of which qualify for `liq` at 1R).
8. **X.** R = |signal close - stop| (includes the 1-tick buffer). A level qualifies when its distance from the signal
   close, in the trade's direction, is >= m x R (m = 1; neighbours 0.75 and 1.5). `liq` = the nearest qualifying one of
   {cand1's high, the session high of the row's window (reading 3), the highest high from the first bar of the
   trading day through the decision bar}; equal levels -> the first listed. The target is the level itself.
9. **2R targets** (YT1 common rule: entry +/- k x |entry - stop| before slippage, on the tick grid) from: the signal
   close (`close`); the limit price (`retest`; a fill at a better open keeps that target: 2,175 of 98,163 retest fills
   at 2R); the open of the entry bar (`pos`).
10. **Retest.** Limit at the opening price of the first candle of the run the CISD closed through (`cisd_ex` start, on
    the trigger timeframe); it is always on the far side of the signal close (asserted for all 118,090 signals).
    "Resting 30 minutes" = the 1-minute bars stamped t+1 ... t+30 after decision bar t (and not past the flat bar).
    "Cancelled if the stop price trades first" = `cancel_lo` / `cancel_hi` at the stop price in `core.simulate`.
    Fills follow the house limit rule in `core.simulate`: a touch fills at the limit, a bar opening at or through the
    limit fills at its open, 1 tick against the fill. Because the limit lies between the market and the stop, the
    cancel only ever acts when a bar opens at or through the stop: 17 of the 14,516 unfilled retest orders.
11. **Positional.** Entry bar j = the first existing 1-minute bar at or after the next clock-hour boundary (the
    boundary is taken in UTC nanoseconds; New York offsets are whole hours); one tradable signal has a later bar
    (2020-03-09 02:17 -> 03:02). The order is placed at the close of bar j-1 (that is its `i`). "The stop price trades
    before then" = a low <= stop (high >= stop) on a bar after the decision bar and before j -> no order (59,693 of
    112,679 tradable signals). If bar j opens at or through the stop, `core.simulate` rejects the order (stop on the
    wrong side of the fill): no trade (7 signals). "The open is at or beyond the target" (level targets only; it
    cannot happen at 2R): see section 9 for how it is written without reading that open.
12. **Flat for the London window** = the last existing 1-minute bar before 08:30 of the cash date (08:29 on 882 of
    883 tradable days; 08:28 on 2020-03-13). All other windows: the day's flat bar.
13. **One order a day** = the first signal of the cash day, in time order, inside the window, that meets every
    confluence and the target's qualification, among tradable signals. If its order does not fill, is cancelled or is
    never placed, the combination has no trade that day. `n` (trades) = filled orders; `orders` = those rows.
14. **`tradable`** = the day passes the `run_orders(skip_roll=2)` day filter (not a roll day or the day after, has a
    daily ATR, is a cash day) and the signal has a later flat bar. In-sample no signal sits at or after its flat bar
    (the early-close sessions have no bars in the `pm` window), so `tradable` = the day filter; such a signal could
    only follow the tradable ones of its day, so filtering before or after "first of the day" gives the same orders
    (the harness module does it after).
15. **Ranking ties in t**: the grid's own order (first-listed levels first). notes/G8.md used the name; for the picks
    here both give the same result (the only exact tie at a pick is pick 3 with its `E prem` twin, identical trades).
    **Overlap identity**: decision bar + side of the ORDERS, filled or not.
16. **Consensus (pick 6).** Level averages = the per-level table (each eligible combination counts once). A level no
    eligible combination uses cannot be chosen. A combination that is not in the grid (`W ldn` x `M both`) counts as
    having no trades. In the fallback each confluence is changed at most once; "first-listed level" is replaced by
    "the level with the second-highest average" for T and W both for the margin and for the new level; equal margins
    -> the confluence listed first; a confluence already at its first-listed level is not a candidate.
17. **Reality check**: the statistic of notes/G8.md section 7 (day-level t, 0 on days without a trade, the same
    statistic observed and resampled); days = the 884 tradable cash days.
18. **For the later out-of-sample phase** (not run): Spearman over the in-sample-eligible combinations with at least
    one out-of-sample trade; "top 10 %" = by in-sample t; the neighbours of a pick are run through the harness module
    on the bars; full-span eligibility = 150 full-span trades.

## 2. The columns, one line each (long written; shorts mirrored; all from bars up to the decision bar `i`)
- `tf, i, date, time, side, tod`: trigger timeframe, 1-minute decision bar index, its cash date, timestamp, +1 / -1, minute of day.
- `entry_ref`: close of the CISD bar (= close of bar `i`). `prot`: `cisd_ex` protected low. `stop`: `prot` - 1 tick.
- `rt_px`: open of the first candle of the down-close run the CISD closed through (the retest limit).
- `w_ldn, w_am, w_open, w_sb, w_pm`: `tod` in 120-299, 510-659, 570-659, 600-659, 810-899.
- `bias`: 1 if `tt.bias(date).bias == side`. `kind`: its kind when `bias` is 1, else ''.
- `b_inv`: `tt.bias` is -side, and the lowest (bullish bias) / highest (bearish) close of this trading day's completed hour candles of >= 30 minutes is beyond EQ.
- `e_disc`: close < EQ = (h1 + l1) / 2 of `tt.bias`. `e_prem`: close > EQ.
- `m_d18`: close < open of the trading day's first bar. `m_mid`: close < 00:00 open. `m_both`: `m_mid` and close < 08:30 open (known only once that bar has opened).
- `r_pd`: `prot` < l1 and close > l1. `r_sess`: `prot` < session low and close > it (session = 18:00-08:29; 18:00-01:59 for a London row).
- `r_pd_brk`: `prot` > h1. `r_sess_brk`: `prot` > session high (same session range as `r_sess`).
- `h_h1` / `h_h4`: the last completed hour / 4-hour candle is a `closure2` or `closure3` in the trade's direction (as G8).
- `s_smt`: (`prot` < MNQ reference low) != (ES run low < ES reference low), false on missing bars (as G8).
- `x_pdx`: h1. `q_pdx_075 / _100 / _150`: h1 - close >= 0.75 / 1 / 1.5 x R.
- `x_liq_075 / _100 / _150`: the nearest of {h1, session high, today's high through bar `i`} at least that far above the close (NaN: none); `q_liq_*`: one exists; `a_liq_src_*`: which (0 / 1 / 2).
- `a_*`: the values behind them (daily bias, EQ, the extreme hour close used by `b_inv`, the three opens and whether they come from the exact minute, raid levels, break / target levels, today's extreme, run start, SMT values).
- `outcomes()`: for X in 2R / pdx / liq and N in close / retest / pos: `R_X_N, pnl_X_N, risk_X_N, f_X_N` (filled) from `core.simulate` on the order of `order_of`; plus `day_ok, tradable, exit_i, j_retest, j_pos`.

## 4. What `core.simulate` cannot express, and what was done
- **Positional entry with a 2R target.** The target is 2R from the fill, the open of the entry bar, which an order
  dict can only carry by reading `open[i + 1]`: the exception written in `s_Z00.py`. `core.causal_check` mirrors that
  open, so it fails by construction on such an order (shown below). `s_G9.orders()` therefore returns these orders
  without the target, and `s_G9.trades()` adds it (`open_target=True`: target = tick_round(open + k x |open - stop|),
  nothing else). Affects only N `pos` x X `2R`: picks 1 and 5 and checks 2 and 13.
- **"Cancelled if the open is at or beyond the target"** (positional, level targets) would need the same open to
  decide whether the order exists. It is written instead as a limit order one tick inside the target that rests on
  the entry bar only and is cancelled at the target (`cancel_hi` / `cancel_lo`): on the 0.25 grid (asserted for every
  bar price) the bar's open is either at / beyond the target (cancelled at the open) or at / inside the limit (filled
  at the open, as a market order is). `outcomes()` asserts on every 7th such fill that a plain market-at-open order
  with that target gives the same fill bar, pnl and R. Cancelled this way: 4,063 signals with a `liq` target, 948 with
  a `pdx` target.
