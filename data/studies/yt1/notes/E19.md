# E19 - Turnaround Tuesday, the cash session only (`tools/yt1/s_E19.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Days are the rows of the harness day table. Tuesday = a row dated on a Tuesday; Monday = the row before it, which
  must be the Monday of the day before (no Monday session -> no trade); "Friday" = the row before that Monday: the
  Friday, or the last cash day before it when that Friday had no session (the video's daily-bar test compares
  Monday's close with the previous close). In-sample 1 of the 78 base trades has a non-Friday there (tag `|noFri`).
- RTH close / low = `rth_c` / `rth_l` of the row; "below" is strict.
- nb1: Monday's close below the RTH low of that "Friday". nb2 ("any down day -> next day"): every cash day whose previous
  cash day closed below the cash day before it.
- Entry at the 09:30 bar's open, signalled at the close of the last 1-minute bar before 09:30 (`etype='open'`); no
  stop, no target, exit at the flat bar; R unit 0.1 x daily ATR(14).
- Two things the day table brings with it, kept as the text has them and marked in the tag so they can be split:
  - `|pdEarly`: the "Monday" was one of the futures exchange's half sessions on a US holiday (Martin Luther King
    Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day ...), whose "RTH close" is the 12:59 bar. 8 of 78 base trades.
  - `|xroll`: the Monday was a contract-roll day, so its close and Friday's are two different contracts (8 of the 15
    in-sample rolls fall on a Monday). The common rule drops roll days only, not the day after, so these Tuesdays
    are traded. 4 of 78 base trades (net +$470).
