# H2 — Pat's 01:00–05:00 candle (YT8_SPEC.md, Part 2)

Module `tools/yt1/s_H2.py`. Variants: `base` at least 3 consecutive 5-minute closes outside, `nb1` at least 2, `nb2`
at least 4. Written from the spec text, `core.py`, `run.py`, `s_Z00.py`; `L_playlist.md` (Model E) opened for "the
excursion". In-sample bars only (2019-06-03 → 2022-12-30). Day rules: `core.run_orders(..., max_per_day=1,
skip_roll=2)`.

## 1. Readings (R) — all fixed before the first run
1. **Range.** High / low of the 1-minute bars stamped 01:00 … 04:59 on the cash day's calendar date, whatever bars
   exist. Every cash day has them, Mondays and days after holidays included (186 of 186 Mondays; the session opens
   18:00 the evening before). 81 days have fewer than 240 bars in the window.
2. **5-minute bars (R).** Clock-aligned (`ctx.bars(5)`), those opening 05:00 … 10:55: "by 11:00" = the entry candle
   has closed by 11:00. A signal is known at the close of the candle's last 1-minute bar. "Consecutive" =
   consecutive bars of that series; a 5-minute bucket with no trade is not a bar (5 days have one between 05:00 and
   10:55).
3. **A close exactly on a range edge (R).** "Outside" is strict (close > range high, or close < range low); a close
   exactly on an edge is inside. It therefore ends a run, and directly after a long-enough run it is the close back
   inside and is entered. 322 five-minute closes in the window land exactly on an edge; 37 of the 619 base trades
   were entered on one.
4. **"Then the first close back inside" (R).** The bar directly after the run. A run that jumps to the other side
   with no close inside is not a signal (12 runs of 3 or more do that); the bar that closed on the other side starts
   a new run there. A run shorter than N followed by a close inside is nothing, and the count starts again.
5. **Excursion (R).** The bars of the run plus the entry bar. Stop = their highest high + 1 tick (run above; short) /
   lowest low − 1 tick (run below; long). Bars before the run's first close outside are not part of it.
6. **Target.** The far side of the range (range low for a short, range high for a long), a fixed price.
7. **One trade a day; target beyond the entry (R).** Every such signal before 11:00 is an order and the first that is
   a trade is the day's trade. A signal whose target is not beyond its entry price is no trade and is not an order
   (a later signal that day could still trade). In-sample it happens once, in `nb1` only: 2020-03-09, the limit-down
   morning, range 8094.25 / 8093.25, two closes above and then a close at 8093.25 = the range low = the target. No
   later signal that day, so the other reading (such a signal ends the day) gives the same trades in every variant.
8. No minimum stop distance (the spec gives H2 none). Trades are flat at the flat bar.

## 5. Odd
- **Two thirds of the trades are entered before 09:30** (base 412 of 619, nb1 488 of 677, nb2 360 of 560): the spec
  starts the 5-minute bars at 05:00, so the rule is mostly an overnight / pre-market trade held with its stop and
  target into the cash session. Base, entered before 09:30: 412 trades, +0.131 R, +$1,570; from 09:30: 207 trades,
  +0.036 R, +$2,224.
- Mean risk is 45 points, and the target (the far side of the range) is not a fixed multiple of it.
- The harness day table counts exchange holidays with a half futures session (for example 2019-07-04) as cash days,
  so the rule trades on them like every YT1 module. Not changed.
