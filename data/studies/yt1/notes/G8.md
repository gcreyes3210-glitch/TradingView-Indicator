# G8 - YT5 confluence grid (YT5_SPEC.md)

Files: `tools/yt1/g8_signals.py` (signals + confluence columns + outcomes), `tools/yt1/g8_grid.py` (grid, selection,
reality check, the later out-of-sample scoring), `tools/yt1/s_G8.py` (standard harness module), `tools/yt1/g8_verify.py`
(the checks quoted below). Building blocks from `tt.py` unchanged (`bias`, `daily`, `cisd_ex`, `closure2`,
`closure3`). `core.py`, `ind.py`, `run.py`, `tt.py`, `YT5_SPEC.md` not edited. In-sample only (bars to 2022-12-30);
no later bar exists in the lab and none was looked for.

Outputs: `data/studies/yt1/is/G8_signals.parquet`, `is/G8_grid.csv`, `is/G8_levels.csv`, `is/G8_reality.json`,
`data/studies/yt1/G8_selected.json` (the picks; carries the sha256 of the grid CSV and of the signal file),
`is/G8_<variant>.csv|json` from the standard runner.

## 1. Readings (R) - all fixed before the grid was evaluated
1. **Decision bar** = the last existing 1-minute bar of the N-minute bucket (`i_last`, the harness convention); the
   window is judged on that bar's New York clock minute, both ends inclusive (am 08:30-10:59, open 09:30-10:59, sb
   10:00-10:59, pm 13:30-14:59). In-sample 2 five-minute and 2 fifteen-minute decision bars are not on the bucket's
   last clock minute (the minute is missing).
2. **Two signals on one bar** (a long and a short CISD on the same bar): the long is first. It cannot happen by the
   CISD definition and does not (0 cases).
3. **E and R `pd` on roll days.** The spec says "where those are missing (roll) the confluence is false". `tt.bias`
   returns candle 1's high / low on its `roll` rows (they are NaN only on the first two days of the data). Taken as:
   false where `tt.bias` has no candle 1 **or** says `roll`. The roll day and the day after are not traded anyway;
   this makes E (`disc`, `prem`) and R `pd` false on the third day (16 tradable in-sample days, 1,197 signals:
   2019-06-21, 09-18, 12-18, 2020-03-18, 03-19, 06-19, 09-18, 12-16, 2021-03-19, 06-16, 09-15, 12-15, 2022-03-18,
   06-17, 09-16, 12-16). B is 0 on those days in `tt.bias` itself. R `sess` and O do not use candle 1 and are computed.
4. **Higher-timeframe candles** = New York clock blocks holding at least one 1-minute bar (hours; 4-hour blocks
   18:00, 22:00, 02:00, 06:00, 10:00, 14:00 by wall clock), in time order. "Last completed candle" = the latest one
   whose last 1-minute bar index is <= the decision bar index. `tt.closure2` / `tt.closure3` run on that series, so
   "the previous candle" is the previous candle of the series. A closure counts only if every candle it reads (2 for
   candle 2, 3 for candle 3) holds at least half its minutes (30 / 120); H = candle 2 or candle 3 closure. No extra
   condition that the candles be clock-adjacent: 184 signals (1h) / 465 (4h) read a trio with a whole block missing
   (2019-06-17/18, 2019-09-13, the March 2020 halts, 2020-07-01); `h_h1` is true on 8 of them (0 tradable), `h_h4` on
   105 (54 tradable). 103 / 277 signals read a thin candle among the three.
5. **S (SMT).** Run = from the first existing 1-minute bar of the run's first candle (`cisd_ex` start) through the
   decision bar. Reference = the 1-minute bars in the 120 clock minutes ending just before that bar, `[t0 - 120 min,
   t0)`. MNQ's run low is the protected low. ES lows over the ES bars inside the same two clock intervals (ES matched
   by timestamp). "Missing ES bars" = any MNQ minute of the run or the reference without an ES bar at the same
   timestamp, or an empty ES span -> false (261 signals); no MNQ reference bars -> false (0 signals). Under the
   looser "only an empty ES span is missing" 8 signals (5 tradable) would differ.
6. **O**: the open of the first bar of the trading day (18:00; one trading day starts at 20:00, 2020-06-30).
   **R `sess`**: lowest low / highest high of the bars from that first bar through 08:29, frozen, also for `pm`.
7. **Strict inequalities** everywhere (a close equal to EQ is neither `disc` nor `prem`: 28 signals).
8. **B**: `bias` = `tt.bias` of the cash day equals the trade's side; `kind` is the kind behind it when it agrees.
9. **`tradable`** = the day passes the `run_orders(skip_roll=2)` day filter (not a roll day or the day after, has a
   daily ATR, is a cash day) and `core.simulate` returns a trade (it returns none at / after the flat bar; 0 such
   in-sample). A signal at or after the flat bar can only follow the tradable ones of its day, so filtering before
   or after "first of the day" gives the same trades.
10. **Ranking ties** in t: by name. **Overlap identity**: decision bar + side (the bar fixes the day), whatever the
    trigger timeframe or stop.
11. **Reality check**: "days of the period" = the cash dates with at least one tradable signal = the 884 tradable
    cash days (same set, checked). The statistic is the day-level t (section 7).
12. **Per-level table**: averaged over the eligible combinations (as the task asks), each combination weighted equally.
13. For the later out-of-sample phase (not run): Spearman over the in-sample-eligible combinations with at least one
    out-of-sample trade; "top 10 %" = by in-sample t; "whole grid" printed twice (all eligible; every combination with a
    trade); per-level table on the in-sample-eligible set in both periods; full-span eligibility = 150 full-span trades.

## 2. The columns, one line each (long written; shorts mirrored; all from bars up to the decision bar `i`)
- `tf, i, date, time, side, tod`: trigger timeframe, 1-minute decision bar index, its cash date, timestamp, +1 / -1, minute of day.
- `entry_ref`: close of the CISD bar (= close of bar `i`). `prot`: `cisd_ex` protected low (high). `stop`: `prot` -/+ 1 tick.
- `w_am, w_open, w_sb, w_pm`: `tod` in 510-659, 570-659, 600-659, 810-899.
- `bias`: 1 if `tt.bias(date).bias == side`. `kind`: `tt.bias(date).kind` when `bias` is 1, else ''.
- `e_disc`: close < EQ, EQ = (h1 + l1) / 2 of `tt.bias`. `e_prem`: close > EQ. (short: `disc` = close > EQ.)
- `o_below`: close < open of the trading day's first bar. (short: close > it.)
- `r_pd`: `prot` < l1 and close > l1. (short: `prot` > h1 and close < h1.)
- `r_sess`: `prot` < lowest low of [first bar of the trading day, 08:29] and close > it. (short: highest high.)
- `h_h1` / `h_h4`: the last completed hour / 4-hour candle is a `closure2` or `closure3` in the trade's direction (reading 4).
- `s_smt`: (`prot` < MNQ reference low) != (ES run low < ES reference low), false on missing bars (reading 5).
- `a_*`: the values behind them (EQ, open, pd level, session level, run start index, MNQ reference, ES run, ES reference).
- `outcomes()`: `R15 pnl15 R20 pnl20 R30 pnl30 risk_pts reason day_ok tradable` from `core.simulate` (entry at the
  close of bar `i`, the stop, target = entry_ref +/- k x |entry_ref - stop| on the tick grid, exit_i = the day's flat bar).

## 3. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None that changed a number. The first grid run stopped before printing anything because the result table had two
columns called `R` (the confluence and the mean R); the statistics were renamed `mR, mR15, mR30`. After that the
signal file, the grid CSV and `G8_selected.json` were regenerated once more at the end and are byte-identical
(sha256) to the first complete run. No reading was changed at any point.

  slices on the 1-minute bars, the CISD re-derived by walking back from the signal bar, higher-timeframe candles
  rebuilt block by block, ES sliced by timestamp): 1,479 signals (600 random + up to 60 per timeframe with each of
  r_pd, r_sess, s_smt, h_h4, h_h1 true; 762 / 401 / 316 on 1 / 5 / 15 minutes) and a second sample of 1,137:
  **0 differences in every column** (tod, entry_ref, prot, stop, run start, 4 windows, bias, kind, e_disc, e_prem,
  o_below, r_pd, r_sess, h_h1, h_h4, s_smt).
- **`tradable` against the harness.** `outcomes()` simulates each signal itself with the day filter read from
  `run_orders` (`roll2_dates`, `noatr_dates`, `dayn < 0`), then passes the 2R orders of all 67,821 signals through
  `core.run_orders(..., one_at_a_time=False, skip_roll=2)` and asserts that the trades returned are exactly the
  tradable rows with identical pnl and R: 64,636 of 67,821, identical. Days with a tradable signal = the 884

  non-cash date with signals is 2021-04-02.
- **pick() against the vectorised scan**: identical rows on 120 random combinations (asserted at every grid run).
- **Reality check** recomputed the slow way (resampling the day rows, numpy mean / std per column): identical to six
  decimals (observed 1.678904; resampled mean 2.796888, median 2.783271, 95 % 3.494982; p 0.9995).
- **Overlap rule** recomputed from the harness trade files: pick 1 / pick 2 share 0.407 of the smaller set; every other
  pair <= 0.112; the one combination passed over (rank 4, `T5.Wam.Bnone.Enone.Obelow.Rnone.Hh4.Snone`, n 409) shares

- The full-phase code path was executed once on a made-up split of the in-sample signals, writing to a scratch
  directory, only to see that it runs; its numbers mean nothing and are not reported. `--phase full` proper stops
  here ("no out-of-sample bars").

## 7. The reality check: how t is computed (the same statistic observed and resampled)
For each of the 624 eligible combinations j, x_j = its 2R R on each of the D = 884 tradable days, 0 on a day without a
trade. t_j = sqrt(D) x mean(x_j) / sd(x_j), sd with D - 1. Observed: t_j on x_j as it is; the statistic is max_j t_j.
Resampled: x_j - mean(x_j) for every j; D day indices drawn with replacement (`numpy.random.default_rng(1)`,
`integers(0, D, D)` per resample, the same days for every combination); t_j recomputed on the resample with the
resample's own mean and sd; the statistic is max_j t_j; 2,000 resamples. p = share of resamples with max >= observed.
The ranking in the selection uses the trade-level t (mean / (sd / sqrt(n)) over a combination's trades); for the top
combination the two are 1.682 (trade-level) and 1.679 (day-level), same combination.
