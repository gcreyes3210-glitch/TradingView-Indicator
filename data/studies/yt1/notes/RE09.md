# RE09 — E09 noise-area intraday momentum, second independent coding

Module `tools/yt1/s_RE09.py`, written from the spec text (E09 + Common rules) and research note entry 9 only. The
first coder's module, notes and results were not opened.

## 1. Readings made where the spec text did not settle something (one per line)
1. Decision bars are the 1-minute bars stamped 09:59, 10:29, ... 15:29 (12 a day); the price compared is that bar's close.
2. sigma for a decision minute on day d averages the previous N rows of the harness day table (`ctx.days`), today not included; every row counts as a day, including holiday half-sessions (Thanksgiving Day, 4 July ...) and roll days.
3. A previous day with no bar at that minute (the afternoon of an early close) is left out of the mean: sigma is the mean of the values present among the previous N cash days, not "N values required". (With "N required" every afternoon decision would be lost for N days after each of the 29 in-sample early closes.) If none is present there is no sigma.
4. The first N cash days of the data have no sigma and are not traded (N previous cash days must exist).
5. In sigma, "09:30 open" is each past day's own 09:30 bar open and close(t) is the close of the 1-minute bar stamped at the decision minute (09:59 for the 10:00 decision).
6. "Previous close" = `days.pdc`: the last 09:30-15:59 1-minute close of the previous cash day in the day table (after an early close: that short session's last bar), not the 16:14 or 17:00 futures close.
7. "Lower mirror" = min(09:30 open, previous close) x (1 - sigma).
8. Band levels are not rounded to the tick (nothing rests at them; they are only compared with a close).
9. Comparisons are strict: close > upper to go long, close < max(upper, VWAP) to leave a long; equality does nothing.
10. Mirror of the exit: a short is closed when close > min(lower, VWAP), and a long is opened if the close is also above the upper.
11. VWAP is `ind.session_vwap` (volume-weighted hlc3) restarted at every 09:30 bar; the value used is the one at the decision bar, that bar included.
12. One evaluation per decision close, on the position held coming into it. After an exit the only entry allowed at the same close is the reversal to the other side: a long closed with upper < close < VWAP is not re-opened at that close (it can be at the next decision close).
13. A flat entry needs only close > upper (the text does not ask for close > VWAP as well), so a long can be opened below VWAP and is then closed at the next decision unless it has cleared max(upper, VWAP).
14. Entries are allowed at every decision close including 15:30; there is no decision at 16:00, the flat bar closes whatever is open.
15. "Each position is one trade" is read as overriding one trade per day: any number of positions a day, one at a time.
16. A decision minute with no bar, with no sigma, or at / after the flat bar (early-close days) is no decision: the position is simply held.
17. Entries and exits are market fills at the decision bar's close with 1 tick of slippage (`etype='close'`, `exit_sig`).
18. No price stop, so R unit = 0.1 x that day's daily ATR(14) (`ctx.days.atr`).
19. Roll days and days without a daily ATR are dropped in `trades()` via `ctx.roll_dates` / `ctx.noatr_dates`; the machine starts flat every day, so dropping a day changes no other day.
20. `opp` (reported): a long is closed only by close < lower, which is also the short entry, so every `opp` signal exit is a reversal; mirror for shorts; VWAP is not used.

Not expressible / harness points: none. `core.run_orders` is not used because it skips an order signalled on the
previous trade's exit bar (a reversal); each order is passed to `core.simulate` and `trades()` asserts that the exit
bar simulate finds equals the bar at which the state machine closed the position (holds for all trades, all variants).
