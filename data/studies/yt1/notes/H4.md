# H4 — Fabio's opening-range break with a volume condition, bars only (YT8_SPEC.md, Part 2)

Module `tools/yt1/s_H4.py`. Variants: `base` stop 1 tick beyond the signal candle, target 1R; `nb1` the same stop,
target 2R; `nb2` stop 2 ticks beyond the far side of the range, target 1R. Written from the spec text, `core.py`,
`run.py`, `s_Z00.py`; `J_fabervaale.md` (Model A) opened for "the body of the candle closing above the range" and
"more volume than the previous one". In-sample bars only (2019-06-03 → 2022-12-30). Day rules:
`core.run_orders(..., max_per_day=1, skip_roll=2)`.

## 1. Readings (R) — all fixed before the first run
1. **Range.** High / low of the 1-minute bars stamped 09:30 … 09:59.
2. **Candles "from 10:00 to 11:25" (R)** = the clock-aligned 5-minute bars (`ctx.bars(5)`) that open 10:00 … 11:25,
   the 11:25 one included (it closes 11:30). A candle is known at the close of its last 1-minute bar.
3. **"Open and close both beyond the range" (R)** is strict: long = open > range high and close > range high; short =
   open < range low and close < range low. The candle's colour is not tested, because the spec asks only where its
   open and close are: 155 of the 633 signal candles close against the trade (for example a down candle whose body
   is above the range is a long).
4. **"The previous 5-minute candle" (R)** = the bar stamped exactly 5 minutes earlier; for the 10:00 candle that is the
   09:55 candle, the last of the range. If that bucket had no bar the candle would not qualify (0 cases). "Larger" is
   strict. Volume = the sum of the candle's 1-minute volumes.
5. **First candle (R).** The first candle that meets the body condition and the volume condition together, on either
   side, is the day's only signal (an earlier candle with its body beyond the range but no volume increase is passed
   over, and the search goes on).
6. **Stop less than 2 ticks from the entry (R).** If the stop is less than 2 ticks from the signal candle's close there
   is no trade that day; a later candle is not looked for. 2 days in `base` and `nb1` (2019-07-09, 2019-12-19: the
   candle closes on its own extreme, stop 1 tick away). The test cannot bite in `nb2`, so `nb2` trades those 2 days:
   606 trades against 604.
7. **Stops.** Long: signal candle's low − 1 tick; short: its high + 1 tick. `nb2`: range low − 2 ticks (long), range
   high + 2 ticks (short).
8. **Target kR (R).** close ± k × |close − stop|, measured on the candle's close before slippage (the house rule for R
   targets); always on the tick grid here.

## 5. Odd
- **The neighbours do not have the same sign as the base** in-sample: `base` and `nb1` are negative, `nb2` (the far
  stop) is positive. `nb2` is a different trade in practice: its stop is six times as far and half its trades (297
  of 606) reach neither stop nor target and are closed at the flat bar.
- The harness day table counts exchange holidays with a half futures session (for example 2019-07-04) as cash days,
  so the rule trades on them like every YT1 module. Not changed.
