# Q6 — MNQ 5 / 30 / 200 EMA (M17, a TradingView script)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q6.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q6.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"Set-up bar closing 09:30 to 15:44" was read as the 1-minute bars *stamped* 09:30 … 15:44** (orders live in the
  bars 09:31 … 15:45), the same convention as the other Part 4 entries, which name a close by its bar ("the close
  of the 09:30 bar", "a close in 09:30–11:29"). Under the clock reading of YT1's common rule ("a bar closing by
  11:00 has its last minute at 10:59") the set-up bars would be those stamped 09:29 … 15:43 (the first one a
  pre-market bar, orders live 09:30 … 15:44). Fixed before the first run and not revisited. To switch, change `WIN`
  to `(9 * 60 + 29, 15 * 60 + 43)` and let the 09:29 bar through the `live` test.
- EMA(5), EMA(30), EMA(200) = `ind.ema` on the continuous 24-hour 1-minute closes. "Above" / "below" are strict.
- Order: `etype="stop"`, price = set-up high + 1 tick, `expire = i + 1` (live in the next bar of the series only),
  stop = set-up low − 1 tick. Short: price = low − 1 tick, stop = high + 1 tick.
- Target = order price ± 3 × |order price − stop| (the common rule: the entry price before slippage). It is fixed
  when the order is placed; if the next bar opens beyond the order price the harness fills at that open and
  measures R from the real fill, with the target where it was. `nb1` 2R, `nb2` 4R.
- "After any exit the next set-up bar is at least 5 bars later": with an exit on bar k the first bar that may be a
  set-up bar again is k + 5 (bars of the series). An order that is not filled blocks nothing: the next qualifying
  bar is a new set-up.
- `fresh`: the bar before the set-up bar in the series (whatever its time) did not close on that side of all
  three EMAs. The five-bar rule applies as in base.
- `run_orders` cannot express the five-bar gap (its rule is "signal bar after the previous exit bar"). `trades()`
  therefore walks the orders in time order itself and calls `core.simulate` for each one that is allowed, leaving
  out roll days and days without a daily ATR as `run_orders` does. Every fill, exit and P&L is the harness's.
