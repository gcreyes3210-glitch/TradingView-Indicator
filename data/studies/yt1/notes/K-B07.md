# K-B07 - the DR rule, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_B07.py`)

Claim: after the first 5-minute close beyond the 09:30-10:29 range, the opposite extreme holds for the rest of the
session. Claimed: 80 % ("DR/IDR V1" listing; one video title says 90 %). No pass / fail: measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Wick range (DR): highest high / lowest low of the 1-minute bars 09:30-10:29. Body range (IDR): highest / lowest
  5-minute body (max / min of open, close) of the twelve 5-minute bars 09:30-10:25.
- Confirmation: the first 5-minute bar opening 10:30 or later and closing by 16:00 whose close is strictly beyond the
  range; one a day, the first on either side. (B07 the trade rule stops taking signals at 15:00; the claim is about
  the session to 16:00, so the check does not. 25 of the 855 wick confirmations come at 15:00 or later.)
- Held (the headline): from the first 1-minute bar after the confirming bar to the last bar before 16:00, no bar
  trades strictly beyond the opposite extreme of the same range (a touch is not "through").
- Every cash day in the day table is counted, including roll days (the range is intraday) and the 29 early-close
  days, which end at their halt; the figure without early-close days is given beside it.
- Secondary figures in the JSON, each labelled: not even touched; not traded through at any time from 10:30 on
  (i.e. including a wick through the far side BEFORE the confirmation, the "it is the low of the day" wording);
  body version only: the opposite body extreme not closed through on a later 5-minute close, and the opposite DR
  (wick) extreme not traded through after a body confirmation (LuxAlgo's "early indication" wording).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
