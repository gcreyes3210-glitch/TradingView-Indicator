# C06 - EMA 8 / 14 / 50 + Stochastic RSI + ATR bracket

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C06.py` (bar size is the parameter `bar`; base 5, nb1 3, nb2 15).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Stochastic RSI (3, 3, 14, 14): the entry names no settings; these are the ones the spec gives for C03 and the
  sourcing note gives for this video. No zone condition on the cross (the spec has none).
- "%K crosses above %D" = `ind.crossed_up` on completed bars (K[k] > D[k], K[k-1] <= D[k-1]). The EMA stack
  (EMA8 > EMA14 > EMA50, strict) and ATR(14) are read on the signal bar k.
- "From entry" = from the signal bar's close (the entry price before slippage, as in the common R-target rule).
- Stop = close -/+ 3 x ATR(14)[k], placed on the first tick at or beyond that level (down for a long, up for a
  short): exactly the bars that touch the unrounded level touch that tick. Target = close +/- 2 x ATR(14)[k],
  `core.tick_round` nearest.
- ATR(14) is Wilder's, on the continuous series, so the true range of the first bar after the 17:00 break or a
  weekend includes the gap.
- "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = clock close in [09:35, 15:00] (3-minute: first signal bar opens 09:33).
- Decision bar `i` = `i_last` of the signal bar, as the brief defines it. Orders on a bar whose last minute has no
  1-minute bar: 2 of 5,099 in base, 1 of 8,201 in nb1, 1 of 1,777 in nb2; none of them became a trade.
