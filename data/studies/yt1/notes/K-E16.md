# K-E16 - news-candle data high / data low, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_E16.py`)

Claim (EzTrades): on news days the 08:30 candle's high and low are the "data high" and "data low"; after one side is
swept, "around 85% of the time, the untapped side gets taken out the same day". No sample given. No pass / fail:
measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Event day: a cash day of the day table whose `ev` set holds CPI, NFP, PPI, retail_sales, GDP_advance or PCE and
  for which `data/events.csv` lists that release at 08:30. The harness calendar has the advance GDP estimate only
  (14 in-sample), not the second and third estimates. One PCE release in the calendar is listed at 10:00; it is
  outside the in-sample span.
- News candle: the 5-minute bar 08:30-08:34 built from its 1-minute bars; data high / low = its high / low.
- Traded through: a 1-minute bar from 08:35 on trading at least one tick beyond the level. The first such bar on
  either side is the sweep; "the other side by 16:00" = a later bar up to 15:59 (the halt on a short day) trades
  through the opposite level. One bar through both levels would count as swept and completed (none on event days).
- Headline = days with the other side traded through / days with one side traded through.
- Every event day in the day table is counted, roll days included (the levels are intraday). No event day
  in-sample is a short day. The two release days without a cash session (Good Friday 2020-04-10 CPI and 2021-04-02
  NFP) are not in the day table and are not counted.
- Control, labelled as such: the same measure on the 700 cash days without any of these releases that have 08:30
  bars (there the 08:30 bar is an ordinary pre-market bar, median range 12 points against 32 on event days).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.

## 3. Hand check
Five event days drawn at random and recomputed from the 1-minute bars with pandas; all agree with the module:
- 2020-05-13 PPI: candle 9166.00 / 9150.75; low traded through 08:35, high 10:27 -> completed.
- 2020-06-16 retail sales: 9980.00 / 9958.75; high 08:36, low 09:35 -> completed.
- 2020-12-11 PPI: 12329.25 / 12315.00; high 08:58, low 09:32 -> completed.
- 2021-05-12 CPI: 13272.50 / 13090.00 (a 182-point candle); low 10:33, high never -> not completed.
- 2021-10-28 GDP: 15683.25 / 15665.00; low 08:39, high 09:32 -> completed.
- The one event day with neither side traded: 2022-12-09 PPI, candle 11748.00 / 11516.50 (231.5 points), rest of the
  day 11718.50 / 11550.00 inside it.
- An independent loop over all event days gives the same 223 and 165.

biggest releases (NFP 57 %, CPI 69 %). The same statement about an ordinary 08:30 bar is true 89 % of the time, so
the figure says the bar is narrow against the day's range, not that a news candle draws price to its far side: on
release days the candle is wider and the far side is reached less often. In 93 % of cases (207 of 223) the first
sweep comes before 09:30, and only 22 % of sweeps are completed by 09:30.
