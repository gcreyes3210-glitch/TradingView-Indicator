# A05 — Candle Range Theory on the 05:00–09:00 candle

Module: `tools/yt1/s_A05.py`. Variants: `base` half at Mid + half at the opposite boundary, `nb1` all at Mid, `nb2`
all at the opposite boundary, `pdc` (base with the previous-cash-close filter).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **"Skipped if the close is already past Mid"** = the day has no trade. The first signal is the only one; a later
  signal the same day is not looked for. In-sample: 750 days have a first signal, 67 of them are skipped this way.
- **"Past Mid"** is strict and uses the exact average (short skipped when close < Mid, long when close > Mid). A close
  exactly at Mid trades (1 day in-sample).
- **Mid as an order price.** The average of the high and low is between two ticks whenever the range is an odd number
  of ticks (461 of 926 days). The order price is `core.tick_round(Mid)` (nearest, the helper's tie rule); the skip
  test uses the exact average. The same Mid price is used in `base` and `nb1`.
- **A bar that gives both signals at once** (high above the range high, low below the range low, close inside): the
  first signal is on both sides in the same bar, so no trade that day (7 days in-sample). The spec does not cover it.
- **Half / half** is `parts=[(Mid, 0.5), (opposite boundary, 0.5)]`; the stop does not move after the first half (the
  spec moves nothing).
- **Stop** = highest high of the bars from 09:30 through the signal bar + 1 tick (shorts); lowest low − 1 tick (longs).
- **Window.** 5-minute bars opening 09:30 … 11:55 (closing by 12:00). Earlier bars may already have closed outside
  the range; the spec asks only for the first bar that trades beyond and closes back inside.
- **`pdc`** filters the base trade (a first signal on the wrong side of the previous cash close gives no trade; it
  does not go on to a later signal), so `pdc` is a subset of `base` (checked). The price compared with `days.pdc` is
  the signal bar's close, i.e. the entry price.
- **Range bars.** The range uses whatever 1-minute bars exist in 05:00–08:59 that calendar day; 51 days have at least
  one minute without a bar, 3 have fewer than 120 bars.

## 3. Hand check
Bars printed around 4 base trades (2019-09-23 long, 2020-01-15 short, 2020-12-17 short, 2021-11-22 short). In each:
the range matches the 05:00–08:59 bars; no earlier 5-minute bar traded beyond an edge and closed back inside; the
close is not past Mid; stop = extreme since 09:30 ± 1 tick; fill = signal close ± 1 tick; the two halves exit at Mid
and at the opposite boundary, each 1 tick worse.
Example, 2019-09-23: range 7848.75 / 7817.00, Mid 7832.875 → order price 7833.00. The 10:05 bar trades below 7817.00
but closes at 7817.00 (not above: no signal); the 10:10 bar closes below; the 10:15 bar has low 7811.75, close
7822.00 → long, fill 7822.25; lowest low since 09:30 is 7807.75 → stop 7807.50; halves out at 7832.75 and 7848.50
(average 7840.625), net $34.75 = +1.18 R.
An independent re-derivation from 1-minute bars (own 5-minute buckets) gave the same 676 orders (bar, side, stop, both
exit prices). 676 orders → 659 trades: the 17 others are on contract-roll or no-ATR days.
`python3 tools/yt1/run.py A05 --check`: **PASS** (16 of 16).
