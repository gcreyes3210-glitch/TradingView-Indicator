# G7 - TTrades Silver Bullet, no daily bias  (tools/yt1/s_G7.py)

Rule as coded (short; longs mirror): range = high / low of 09:00-09:59. Raid = the first 1-minute bar from 10:00
with high > range high (or low < range low). Each bar from the raid bar on: the raid's high is the highest high since
the raid bar; the level is the latest 3-bar swing low (`ind.fractal_low`, one bar each side) whose confirming bar
closed before the bar holding that high opened; the first close below the level is the confirmation. Entry: sell
limit at the near edge (bottom) of the latest bearish 1-minute gap (`ind.fvg_bear`) whose three candles lie between
the bar holding the raid's high and the confirmation bar, resting from the next bar to the 10:59 bar. Stop 1 tick
above the raid's high, target the range low, stop to entry once 3R has traded (`be_trigger`), flat bar. nb1 = limit
at the gap's midpoint, nb2 = at its far edge. No "reported" variants are registered for G7.

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **(R) "The other side raided first -> no trade" - the reading with the most weight.** Taken as: if price trades
  beyond the other side of the range at any bar from the raid bar through the confirmation bar, there is no trade
  that day ("first" = before the confirmation, the sentence it follows). The other possible reading, "only the side
  raided first is traded", is already what "Raid = the first bar to trade beyond one side" says, and is also true of
  this code. The two differ only on days where the far side is reached before the swing break: 3 in-sample days.
- **(R) A bar that trades beyond both sides** as the first raid bar: no first side, no trade (1 in-sample day).
- **(R) "The raid's high"** = the highest high from the raid bar through the bar being judged; it moves as price
  extends. **"The bar holding it"** = the first bar that printed that high (a later equal high does not move it).
- **(R) "Confirmed before the bar holding the raid's high"**: a swing low at bar p is confirmed at the close of bar
  p + 1; that close must come before the high's bar opens (p + 1 <= e - 1). "Latest" = the most recent such swing;
  no look-back limit other than the 18:00 open of the trading day (never reached: 0 days without a known swing).
  Strict inequalities on both sides, as `ind.fractal_low` defines it.
- **(R) "A 1-minute close below" = the first such close**, judged bar by bar with what is known at that bar; it may
  be the raid bar or the bar holding the high. It is the day's only confirmation: if it leaves no gap there is no
  trade, and a later close below the level is not a second confirmation.
- **(R) The gap must be complete at the confirmation bar**: its three candles k-2, k-1, k satisfy e <= k-2 and
  k <= confirmation bar (both ends included). A gap whose third candle is the bar after the confirmation bar does
  not count, even though that is the usual picture when the displacement candle is itself the confirming close; 192
  of the 630 confirmed raids have no gap inside the span. "Latest" = largest k.
- **(R) No "unfilled" test on the gap**: the spec asks for the latest gap, not an untouched one. A gap traded back
  through before the confirmation bar is still used (hand check 2).
- **(R) Near edge** = the edge price meets first on the way back (short: the gap's bottom, high[k]; far edge = its
  top, low[k-2]). **Midpoint** off the tick grid is rounded toward the far edge (up for a sell, down for a buy), the
  convention of A10's `ce`.
- **(R) Order timing**: decided at the close of the confirmation bar, resting from the next bar through the 10:59
  bar; a confirmation on the 10:59 bar cannot be traded. The raid itself may come at any minute from 10:00.
- **(R) Stop** = raid extreme as known at the confirmation bar, 1 tick beyond (YT1 "beyond X"). The limit lies
  between price and that extreme, so the extreme cannot move before the fill.
- **(R) 3R** = limit price -/+ 3 x |limit price - stop|, before slippage. The target is the far side of the range
  whether it is nearer or farther than 3R.
- **(R) An entry at or beyond the target** (limit not inside the range side of the target) would be no trade. It
  never occurs in-sample.
- **(R) One order a day**, whether or not it fills. Positions are closed at the flat bar (the spec's reading).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was run once and not changed. (Before the first run it was reorganised so that the per-day setups
are computed once and shared by the three variants, and so that the gap test calls `ind.fvg_bear` / `ind.fvg_bull`.)

## 4. Counts and anything odd
- 926 in-sample days: no raid 10:00-10:59 on 86; both sides in the raid bar 1; raid of the high 447, of the low 392.
  Of those 839: other side reached before the confirmation 3; no confirming close by 10:58 206; confirmed 630, of
  which no gap inside the span 192 and a setup 438 (219 long, 219 short).
- 438 orders, 13 on roll / no-ATR days. Filled: near edge 305, midpoint 283, far edge 272.
- Base exits: 223 stop (21 of them at the entry price after 3R had traded), 71 target, 11 at the flat bar. The
  target is nearer than 3R on 116 of the 305 trades, where the break-even rule cannot act.
- Gap width on the 438 setups: median 2.75 points, mean 4.0, largest 22.75; 1 or 2 ticks on 12% of them. On 210 of
  the 438 the gap's third candle is the confirmation bar itself.
- Nothing found wrong in core.py, ind.py, run.py or tt.py (tt.py is not used: the rule has no daily bias).
