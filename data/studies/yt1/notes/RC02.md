# RC02 — second, independent coding of C02 (8-55 EMA pullback on NQ, long only)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_RC02.py`. Written from the spec text (C02 entry, Family C common frame, Common rules) and research
note block 2 only. No `s_C*` / `s_E*` module, no other note and nothing in `data/studies/yt1/is/` was opened.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (one per line; each is a place a comparison could differ)
1. Signal window: an N-minute bar is a signal bar when its OPEN time is 10:00 to 15:50, both ends included, on every timeframe. This replaces the family's 09:35 → 15:00 window. On 3-minute bars the last signal bar opens 15:48; on 15-minute bars the 15:45 bar closes on the flat bar and the harness drops it, so the last usable one opens 15:30.
2. "A low was below EMA55 within the last 6 bars": bars k−5 … k, the signal bar included; each bar's low is compared with that same bar's EMA55; strictly below.
3. "The close is above EMA8" and both parts of "bullish" are evaluated at the signal bar with the comparisons exactly as written (close > EMA8 strict, EMA55 ≥ EMA165, EMA8 > EMA55).
4. An order is produced on EVERY bar in the window where the long condition holds (a state, not only the first bar of it). The harness's one-position-at-a-time rule decides which become trades. After an exit the next qualifying bar re-enters, the same day, with no daily cap; this includes the close of the N-minute bar inside which a trailing-stop exit happened.
5. Trailing stop, which bars: the rule's own N-minute bars, not 1-minute bars. The level changes only at the close of a completed N-minute bar and applies from the next 1-minute bar.
6. Trailing stop, "since entry": only N-minute bars that open after the entry count. The signal bar's own high is not counted (it was made before the entry at its close). So the trade has no stop at all until the first N-minute bar after the entry has closed.
7. Trailing stop level on the tick grid: high × 0.9925 is rounded DOWN to the tick. The trigger bars are the same as with the unrounded level; the stop fill is up to one tick lower.
8. "A bar that turns bearish": the first N-minute bar after the entry that is bearish (EMA55 ≤ EMA165 and EMA8 < EMA55) at its close; the trade is closed at that bar's close. Coded as a flag on every bearish bar, which is the same thing because the entry bar is bullish.
9. Neighbours: EMA lengths 8 / 55 / 165 and the 6-bar look-back are counted in bars of the variant's timeframe (not rescaled to keep the same clock length).
10. EMAs are `ind.ema` (seeded with the SMA of the first n closes) over every N-minute bar of the 24-hour series from the start of the data.
11. Nothing from the script that the spec does not state is coded: no previous-day-volume filter, no short side.
12. R unit: 0.1 × `ctx.days.atr` of the signal day; `stop=None`.

Implementation detail, not a reading: each order needs its own trail (the running maximum starts at its own entry), and
one full-length array per candidate order (4,283 of them) does not fit in memory, so the trail is a small array-like
(`_DayTrail`) holding the values from the signal bar to the flat bar and NaN everywhere else. `simulate` reads it by
index and the look-ahead test reads it through `np.asarray`. Checked on one order: full length, NaN at the signal bar,
first value 5 bars later, non-decreasing, on the tick grid.
