## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)

Clock and decisions
1. "k minutes later", "L minutes earlier", "the next 1-minute bar" = the bar **stamped** that many minutes away, found
   by its time, never by position. A minute without a bar is missing: nothing is carried to a neighbouring bar.
2. A decision exists only if both the decision bar and the exit bar (k minutes later, at or before the flat bar
   `days.i_end`) exist. In sample 37 in-session gaps (2019-06-17/18, 2019-07-04, four halt days of March 2020) remove a
   few decisions; a full session has exactly 360, 180, 120, 72, 36, 24, 12.
3. The clock grid is anchored at 09:59 every day (09:59 + m x k), also on early-close days and after a gap.
4. Days not traded: `days.roll` and days without `days.atr`, the same set `core.run_orders(skip_roll=True)` drops
   (898 of 926 cash days in sample are traded, 2019-06-21 on).
5. One position at a time: a trade ends at the close at which the next decision is made. `core.run_orders` with its
   default `one_at_a_time=True` would skip an order signalled on the previous trade's exit bar (every second trade
   here), so the replay through the harness uses `one_at_a_time=False`; the grid itself never calls it.

s, s_d, VWAP
6. s = `ind.stdev` (population standard deviation around the mean, TradingView's `ta.stdev`) of the 390 most recent
   returns. A return ln(close[b] / close[b-1]) counts only if b and b-1 are bars of the same regular session (09:30 bar
   to the flat bar of a cash day of `ctx.days`; on an early close the bars after the flat bar are outside), b-1 is
   stamped one minute before b, and both are the same contract. Sessions of roll days and of days without an ATR are
   in the window (they are regular sessions; they are only not traded). Fewer than 390 returns: no s, no trade.
7. s_d: the population standard deviation of (ES 1-minute log return - MNQ 1-minute log return) at those same 390
   bars; a bar at which ES has no 1-minute return (ES minute missing at b or b-1, or an ES contract change) is left
   out of the standard deviation, the window stays the same 390 bars. In sample this is one ES minute
   (2019-07-04 12:24) at in-session MNQ bars.
8. VWAP and its sd: `ind.session_vwap` restarted at every 09:30 bar (the spec's formulas).

Signals
9. `mom` / `rev`: the side is the sign of the price difference close[t] - close[t-L]; z uses the log return. The bar
   t-L is used even when it lies before 09:30 (only L = 30 at the 09:59 decision: the 09:29 bar). Same for `es`.
10. `es` / `esr`: ES bars only through `ctx.extra("ES")`, matched to MNQ bars by timestamp. ES missing at t or at
    t-L: no trade at that decision.
11. A return across a contract change (MNQ or ES instrument id differs between t-L and t) is no signal. It never
    happens in sample (0 decisions: the contracts change at 19:00 / 20:00); it is a guard for the later bars. What does
    happen in sample, over all cash days at k = 1: the bar at t-L is missing at 28 / 35 / 58 / 93 decisions
    (L = 1 / 5 / 15 / 30) and ES is missing at t or t-L at 1 or 2.
12. "z >= 0" still needs a z: if s (or s_d, or the VWAP sd) cannot be computed there is no trade. A VWAP sd of
    exactly 0 with close != VWAP gives z = infinity (trade). In sample the only decisions without s or s_d are those
    of the first session (2019-06-03), which has no ATR and is not traded, and the VWAP sd is positive at every
    decision.

Entries and P&L
13. `lim`: "the next 1-minute bar" is the bar stamped one minute after the decision (missing: not filled). The fill
    is at the limit price also when that bar opens through it (no price improvement, as the spec's "at the limit
    price"). With k = 1 the fill bar is also the exit bar: the fill needs low <= limit - 0.25 (high >= limit + 0.25)
    and the exit is that bar's close.
14. Net: `mkt` = gross - $3.00 (2 ticks + $2); `lim` = gross - $2.50 (1 tick on the exit + $2). Gross = side x
    (exit close - decision close) x $2 for both (for `lim` the decision close is the limit price).
15. "Share of orders filled" = `lim` trades / the `mkt` trades of the same signal, strength and holding time (the
    decisions at which the signal says trade are the orders).

Grid columns and selection
16. Sessions on which a combination could trade = traded days with at least one decision of its holding time (the
    same 898 for every combination in sample). Trades a day = trades / sessions (`lim`: filled trades). These are
    also the "days with at least one decision" of the bootstrap.
17. t = mean / (sd / sqrt(D)) of the daily sum of (gross $ / the day's 0.1 x ATR x $2) over the D sessions, a
    session without a trade counting 0, sd with D - 1.
18. Win % = share of trades with net > 0. Max drawdown = on the daily net over the sessions in date order.
19. Selection: no sign condition (the spec states none): pick 1 is the tier's highest t whatever its sign, pick 2 the
    tier's largest net $ a day whatever its sign. If the tier's largest net $ a day is pick 1 or pick 1's mirror the
    tier has no pick 2 (it is not replaced by the next one). Ties: the grid's order. Tiers: 100 <= A, 30 <= B < 100,
    10 <= C < 30 on trades a day.

Out-of-sample step (coded, not run on later bars)
20. "3 of 4 calendar years" = at least 3 of 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 with net > 0. "Neighbours net positive" = total
    out-of-sample net > 0 for each of: the next holding time down and up in 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 where they exist,
    and **every** adjacent strength level (z 0 -> 1; z 2 -> 1; z 1 -> both 0 and 2), same signal and entry.
21. Bootstrap = `core.boot_p` (share of 10,000 resampled means <= 0, seed 1) on the pick's daily net over its
    out-of-sample sessions; passes needs p < 0.05 / 6 whatever the number of picks.
22. ORB v1.4 = `core.trades_df(core.run_orders(ctx, cal_orb.orders(ctx), skip_roll=False))`, trades by entry date
    from 2023-01-01 to the last traded day; its max drawdown is on its daily P&L. Net / max drawdown of a pick is on
    its daily net over its sessions.
23. White's reality check: the statistic is the one YT5 defines for this registry (each daily series centred on its
    mean, days resampled 2,000 times with seed 1, the same days for every combination, the largest day-level t across
    the grid; p = share of resamples whose largest t is at least the observed one), on the net $ a day of all 756 over
    the out-of-sample sessions (0 on a day without a trade; a combination without any trade is left out). White's
    original statistic (the largest mean net $ a day, not divided by its standard deviation) is printed beside it from
    the same resamples.
24. Spearman: gross $ per trade, frozen in-sample grid against out of sample, over the combinations with a trade in
    both. Hindsight top 10: by total out-of-sample net $. Break-even: the tier's (tiers by out-of-sample trades a day)
    highest gross $ per trade / $0.50 = round-trip cost in ticks (the house cost is 6 ticks at the market, 5 with a
    limit entry).

COST1
25. Regular session = 09:30 bar to the flat bar of every cash day of the day table, roll days and days without an ATR
    included (nothing is traded in Part 1). The move over h minutes needs the bar stamped h minutes later in the same
    session. Dollars a year = round trips a day x the cost x the sessions of that calendar year in the bars (2019
    starts in June: 151 sessions); the `all` row uses 252 sessions.
26. MES is available through `ctx.extra("MES")` and is measured: its bars are placed in the same sessions by
    timestamp (34 in-session MNQ minutes have no MES bar in sample; they are simply absent from the MES figures).
27. The break-even hit rate is printed as the formula gives it, also when it is above 1 (MES 2019, 1 and 2 minutes:
    no hit rate breaks even).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results

None that changed a number. The selection, the tier sizes and every table of the first in-sample run are identical to
the final log (`logs/G12_is.txt`); the picks did not change between runs (the grid prints a NOTE if they ever do).
After the first run only these were edited: two print statements of `g12_grid.py` (the pooled `lim` table lost a
column that is 0 by construction; the NOTE about an existing selection file now says whether the picks changed), and
part (e) was added to `g12_verify.py`. Before any result: one crash in the checker's slow reference (a numpy boolean
subtraction), fixed before it had compared anything.

## 3. What the verification showed (`python3 tools/yt1/g12_verify.py`, `logs/G12_verify.txt`, 73 s)

Last line: `PASS  G12 verification, parts abcde: every check agreed` (42 checks).

- **(a) Replay through the harness.** Every order of 11 `mkt` combinations (six fixed in advance, plus the picks in
  their mkt form) on 132 cash days (100 random, all 28 roll / no-ATR days, 6 early closes) through
  `core.run_orders(one_at_a_time=False, skip_roll=True)` -> `core.simulate(etype="close", exit_i=..., r_pts=0.1 x ATR)`:
  79,961 trades, the same decision bar, fill bar, exit bar, side, fill prices (close +/- 0.25) and R, P&L equal to the
  cent (largest difference $0.0000); the orders on untraded days are dropped by `run_orders` and absent from the grid.

  2020-02-10 12:57 (not filled: next low 9465.50 above 9465.00), 2020-07-29 15:52 (short, next high 10646.75 = limit

  scalar arithmetic: all agree.
- **(c) Look-ahead.** Bars truncated at six random mid-session times (2021-07-23 15:06 ... 2022-10-14 12:40), 12
  combinations (six fixed, plus the picks): every trade that had exited by the cut is identical (628,367 to 1,009,869
  trades per cut), and side and z of all 18 signals are bit-identical at every bar up to the cut.
  Two things to know: (i) the harness's day table reads a session that stops early as an early close and would put
  its flat bar 10 minutes before the last bar, so the test sets the cut day's flat bar to the last bar present
  (cuts are after 12:15 so that the cut day is a session of the truncated table); (ii) the exited-trades test alone
  cannot see a peek shorter than the holding time (the peeked bar is inside every exited trade): a deliberately
  leaky signal reading 1 bar ahead passes it on 12 of 12 combinations, which is why the signal-level test is run as
  well (it fails 18 of 18 leaky signals; with a 31-bar peek the trade test fails 7 of 12).
  The harness's own mirrored-future test (`core.causal_check`, 5 sampled bars each) also passes on three `mkt`
  combinations (`mom-15.z1.k5`, `es-5.z0.k10`, `vw.z1.k15`), ES mirrored as well.

Things a reader should know before the tables:
- No combination in tiers A, B or C nets a positive amount in sample; the 27 of 756 (3.6 %) that do all trade under

  of the 378 `mkt` combinations (exactly half, each is the mirror of another) and 54 of the 378 `lim`.

- Pick A2 (`rev-1.z0.k3.lim`) has a negative gross and t = -8.83: in tier A everything loses several hundred dollars
  a day and "the largest net dollars a day" is the combination that trades least there. It is the rule as written.
- A limit entry is filled on about 94 % of orders and its gross per filled trade is about 0.35 points lower than the
  same orders taken at the market (the unfilled 6 % are the ones that moved away at once).
