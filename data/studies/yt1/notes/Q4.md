# Q4 — EMA 9 / 21 cross with RSI and a volume surge, held three bars (M13, MetroTrade)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q4.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q4.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"At a close in 09:30–11:29 or 14:30–15:54"** = the close of a 1-minute bar *stamped* in one of those ranges
  (the bars inside the source's 9:30–11:30 and 14:30–16:00; 15:54 + five bars is exactly the flat bar), on a traded
  cash day and before the flat bar. Under the clock reading of YT1's common rule the windows would be the bars
  stamped 09:29 … 11:28 and 14:29 … 15:53.
- EMA(9), EMA(21) = `ind.ema`, RSI(14) = `ind.rsi`, on the continuous 24-hour 1-minute closes.
- "Crosses above" = `ind.crossed_up` (EMA9 > EMA21 at this bar, EMA9 ≤ EMA21 at the bar before).
- "The mean of the previous 20 bars" = the 20 bars before the signal bar in the 24-hour series, the signal bar not
  included (for the 09:30 bar these are pre-market bars); "above" is strict.
- Short mirror: EMA9 crosses below EMA21, RSI below 50 and below its value one bar earlier, the same volume test.
- "Three bars later" = the third bar after the signal bar in the series (`exit_i = i + 3`), or the flat bar if that
  comes first. `nb1` one bar, `nb2` five bars. No stop: `r_pts = 0.1 × that day's daily ATR(14)`.
- One position at a time = `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)`: a signal on a bar at or before the previous trade's
  exit bar is skipped (38 of 1,999 signals in base fell on the previous trade's exit bar; 117 skipped in all).
