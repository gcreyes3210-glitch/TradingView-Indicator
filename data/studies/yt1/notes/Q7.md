# Q7 - momentum scalp with a tick bracket, M12a (`tools/yt1/s_Q7.py`)

Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q7 and "For Q7 and Q8, stated in advance". Claim check that goes with it:
`notes/K-Q78.md`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Entry price = the entry reference price = the signal bar's close.** Stop and target are fixed prices 6 and 16
  ticks from that close (1.50 and 4.00 points), set at entry. The house fill is 1 tick worse on each fill, so a
  winner nets +$5.00 and a stop nets -$6.00 (R unit = fill to stop = 1.75 points).
- **"The previous 20 bars"** = the 20 one-minute bars before the signal bar on the continuous 24-hour series
  (`ind.sma(volume, 20)` shifted one bar). For signals before 09:50 these are partly pre-market bars (for a 09:31
  signal: 09:11 ... 09:30). "Above 1.5 x" is strict.
- The 09:30 bar has no earlier bar from 09:30, so the first possible signal bar is 09:31; the last is 10:29.
- "Closes above the session high so far" is strict; the session high / low keeps running through the day whatever
  the trades do. Short = the mirror: a close below the lowest low of the earlier bars from 09:30, same volume test,
  stop 6 ticks above, target 16 ticks below.
- **Trades per day.** Part 4's preamble says "One position at a time"; that was read as replacing YT1's common
  "one trade per day": every signal that comes while flat is taken. The harness's one-at-a-time rule also skips a
  signal on the bar a trade exits on.
- The bracket runs until stop, target or the flat bar (in sample only 3 trades of `x4` reach the flat bar; none of
  base, nb1 or nb2 does).
- Early-close days are traded (the window is before any early flat bar). Roll days and days without a daily ATR are
  dropped by `run_orders`.
