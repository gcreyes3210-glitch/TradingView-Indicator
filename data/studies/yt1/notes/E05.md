# E05 - NQ Stats ALN sessions (`tools/yt1/s_E05.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- For cash day D: Asia = 20:00-23:59 on the calendar day before D (Sunday for a Monday; the evening of a holiday
  Monday for the Tuesday) plus 00:00-01:59 on D; London = 02:00-07:59 on D. Built from calendar dates with
  `ctx.span`. A day on which either session has no bars is skipped (none in-sample; the thinnest is 2020-03-16 with 48 Asia
  bars and 19 London bars).
- "Above" is strict; "inside Asia's range" includes its edges (Asia low <= London low <= Asia high). Mirror: London's
  low below Asia's low and London's high inside Asia's range -> short, target 1 tick beyond London's low, stop 1
  tick beyond London's high.
- Entry = market at the close of the bar stamped at the entry minute; no such bar = no trade (the 07:59 bar is
  missing on 4 limit-down days of March 2020).
- Neighbours: the pattern is still fixed by 02:00-07:59. "Target or stop traded first" = any bar from 08:00 through
  the entry bar itself reached the target price (London high + 1 tick for a long) or the stop price.
- The trade is entered before the cash open and is flat at the day's flat bar like any other.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
