# A09 - ICT Silver Bullet, first FVG in the hour with the midnight-open side rule  (tools/yt1/s_A09.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **"Counted only if"**: an FVG that fails the side rule is not counted and the search goes on; the signal is the
  first FVG in the hour that is counted. (`nofilter` = simply the first FVG, either direction.)
- **Side rule**: bullish gap top = low of candle 3, strictly below the midnight open; bearish gap bottom = high of
  candle 3, strictly above it.
- **Midnight open** = the open of the 1-minute bar stamped 00:00 on the cash day's calendar date. A day with no bar
  at 00:00 has no midnight open and is not traded: 9 of the 926 in-sample days (2019-06-06, 06-14, 06-18, 07-03,
  07-16, 09-11, 09-17, 10-07 and 2020-03-16). `nofilter` does not use it and trades those days.
- **One order a day**, the first counted FVG, whether or not its limit fills. A signal whose third bar is the 10:57
  bar is known at the 10:59 close, so its limit has no bar left to rest on and cannot fill.
- **Target** = limit price +/- k x |limit price - stop| (fixed before the fill), on the tick grid.
- **`pm`**: the same side rule with the window 14:00-15:00 (limit to 14:59).
- **Candles 1, 2, 3** are adjacent rows of the 3-minute series inside the window.
