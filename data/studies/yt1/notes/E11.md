# E11 - Larry Williams open +/- prior range (`tools/yt1/s_E11.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- W = the previous cash day's RTH high - low (`pdh - pdl` of the harness day table). That table includes US-holiday
  short sessions, so on the day after a holiday W is the holiday session's (small) range.
- Signal bar = the 09:30 bar (the levels need its open). Both stop orders rest from the 09:31 bar through the last
  bar before 15:00 (the 14:59 bar), never past the flat bar. If the 09:30 bar has already carried price beyond a
  level, the harness fills that order at the 09:31 open.
- Levels off the tick grid: an order at the exact level trades at the first tick at or beyond it, so the buy stop
  is rounded up and the sell stop down (away from the open).
- "Stop and target 0.5 W from the entry" is measured from the order's price, not from the fill (the harness takes
  the bracket as fixed prices with the order). The protective stop goes on the tick grid away from the entry (a
  stated distance, so no extra tick); the target goes to the nearest tick (`core.tick_round`; an exact half tick
  goes to the even tick). The neighbours change the 0.25 entry factor only; the bracket stays 0.5 W.
- "The first to fill only": `orders()` returns both candidates; `trades()` simulates each on its own and keeps the
  one with the earlier fill bar. Both filling in the same 1-minute bar = no trade that day. Roll days and days
  without a daily ATR are dropped in `trades()` (`ctx.roll_dates`, `ctx.noatr_dates`).
- `lw1`: levels +/- 1.0 W, no stop, no target, closed at the close of the last bar before 15:00 (the flat bar on
  an early-close day). No orders at all on Mondays and no sell stop on Fridays: the day-of-week rule removes the
  order before "first to fill" is applied, so a Friday buy stop stands alone. R unit 0.1 x daily ATR(14).
