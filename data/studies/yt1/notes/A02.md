# A02 — Casper 5-minute candle + 1-minute FVG break

Module: `tools/yt1/s_A02.py`. Variants: `base` 2R, `nb1` 1.5R, `nb2` 3R, `c1030` (candle 3 at 10:29 or earlier),
`bar2` (stop beyond candle 2's extreme).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Which candle anchors the stop.** "The first of those three bars that closed outside the range" is read as closed
  beyond the edge on the trade side (above the range high for a long, below the range low for a short), the same
  condition that makes the signal. Reading "outside" as either edge would pick a different candle on 3 of the 921
  in-sample signal days.
- **All three candles 09:35 or later.** Candle 1 is the 09:35 bar at the earliest, so the earliest signal is the close
  of the 09:37 bar; the latest is the close of the 10:59 bar (`c1030`: 10:29).
- **Three candles** are three consecutive 1-minute bars of the series inside the window. A minute with no bar is not a
  candle (as on a chart). This touches 1 signal in-sample.
- **First signal of the day** = the first FVG, either direction, that meets the close condition. Only that one is
  offered. If its stop were not on the losing side of the entry price the harness would reject it and the day would
  have no trade; this happened 0 times in-sample (base and `bar2`).
- Closes are compared strictly (close > range high). Stop = anchor candle's low − 1 tick (high + 1 tick). Target =
  close of candle 3 ± k × |close − stop|, rounded to the nearest tick (only 1.5R can be off the grid).
- Nothing in the spec sets a minimum stop distance, so none is coded: 7 base trades have a risk of 2 points or less
  (smallest 0.75), where the two fills and the commission are a large part of R.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after the first run.

## 3. Hand check
Bars printed around 4 base trades (2019-12-10 short, 2021-04-05 long, 2022-01-21 short, 2022-06-24 long), covering
the stop anchored on candle 1, 2 and 3. In each: the range matches the 09:30–09:34 bars; the three bars form the gap
(low[3] > high[1] or the mirror); no earlier three-bar window qualified; the anchor is the first of the three that
closed beyond the edge; stop = its extreme ± 1 tick; fill = close of candle 3 ± 1 tick; target = 2 × (close − stop)
from the close; the exit bar is the first to touch the stop or target, filled 1 tick worse.
Example, 2019-12-10: range 8370.50 / 8356.00; bars 09:37–09:39, high[09:39] 8348.50 < low[09:37] 8356.50; 09:38 is the
first to close below 8356.00 (8345.25), its high 8357.50 → stop 8357.75; entry 8347.50 (fill 8347.25); target 8327.00;
stopped at 09:45 (high 8357.75), exit 8358.00.
An independent vectorised re-derivation of the signal bar and side gave the same 921 (bar, side) pairs.
921 orders → 893 trades: the 28 others are on contract-roll or no-ATR days, dropped by the harness.
`python3 tools/yt1/run.py A02 --check`: **PASS** (16 of 16).
