## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Trade count.** Family D says only "One position at a time". Read as family C's frame: any number of trades a
  day, one position at a time (`base`, `nb1`, `nb2`). The other reading, the common rule's one trade per day, is
  coded as `day1`, `day1_nb1`, `day1_nb2` (not in the spec's variant list) so that neither reading needs a code
  change later.
- **A price with no footprint row traded nothing**: its volume in the diagonal comparison is 0. So the lowest row
  of a bar is a buy imbalance when its buy volume is >= 10, and so is a price just above an untraded price.
- **Bar shape from MNQ**: up bar, wick and range use the MNQ 5-minute bar built from the five 1-minute bars; delta
  = sum(buy) - sum(sell) of the footprint rows. The bar is used at the close of the MNQ 1-minute bar T+4.
- **Stack** = a maximal run of >= 3 adjacent ticks that are all buy imbalances; "the highest such stack" = the one
  with the highest top. Mirror: down bar, lower wick <= 25 % of the range, negative delta, the lowest stack of sell
  imbalances; sell limit 1 tick below its bottom, stop 2 ticks above its top.
- **Every signal bar places its own limit**; none is cancelled by a later signal; all rest to the 11:59 bar.
- **One position at a time with resting orders** (`one_at_a_time` in the module; `core.run_orders` takes orders in
  signal order, which would let an order that fills at 11:30 block one that filled and closed at 10:00). Each order
  is simulated alone by `core.simulate`; trades are then taken in the order their fills happen: an order signalled
  while a position is open is not placed (the harness's rule); an order that fills while a position is open, or in
  the bar it exits, is lost; two orders filling in the same 1-minute bar: same side -> the one nearer the market,
  both sides -> neither. In-sample: 1,177 orders, 812 fill when simulated alone, 781 trades; one same-bar pair.
  For reference `core.run_orders` on the same orders gives n 729, R -1.288.
- **Target** = limit price +/- 2 x |limit price - stop|. 267 of the 1,177 limits are at or through the signal bar's
  close (the stack is at the top of the bar); the harness fills those at the next bar's open when it opens there.
- **Not expressible / consequence of the text:** none not expressible. As written the risk is tiny: a 3-tick stack
  gives limit-to-stop 1.25 points (median risk after slippage 1.5 points = $3), against $2 commission and 2 ticks
  of slippage, so a stopped trade costs about -1.8 R and a 2R target nets about +0.7 R.
