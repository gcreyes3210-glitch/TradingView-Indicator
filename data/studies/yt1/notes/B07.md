# B07 - DR / IDR defining range, trade the confirmation (`tools/yt1/s_B07.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "Close beyond the DR" is strict: above the DR high or below the DR low.
- Neighbours (DR ending 10:14 / 10:44): the signal bars start when that DR ends (5-minute bars opening 10:15 or
  later / 10:45 or later); "closing by 15:00" is unchanged.
- `port`: same signal and entry as base. The stop is exactly 0.5 x IDR height beyond the opposite IDR side: a stated
  distance, so the common 1-tick "beyond" buffer is not added on top; when the level falls between ticks it goes to
  the next tick away from the entry. The target (0.5 x IDR height beyond the near IDR side) is rounded to the nearest
  tick. The flat bar still applies.
- `port`, as written, can put the target at or behind the entry: the entry is a close beyond the DR (wick) high while
  the target hangs off the IDR (body) high. In-sample 93 of 830 orders had the entry close already at or beyond the
  target; the harness then exits them on the next bar that trades the target (a scratch less costs, counted as "TP").
  Coded as written, not worked around.
- IB helper (`ib_table`, copied into s_B08, s_B09, s_B14, k_B07): 1-minute bars 09:30 .. end inclusive; the extreme's
  minute is the FIRST bar that set it; the "end close" is the close of the window's last bar. In-sample every window
  ends on its 10:29 bar; four windows have fewer than 60 bars (2019-07-04: 59; the limit-down opens 2020-03-09,
  03-12, 03-16: 46-47).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
