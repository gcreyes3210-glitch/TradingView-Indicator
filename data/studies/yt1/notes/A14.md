# A14 - Candle Range Theory, hourly  (tools/yt1/s_A14.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Hourly candles** are the 1-minute bars of the clock hour (range candle h = 08 ... 13, sweep candle h + 1). Each
  hour is tested on its own, so an hour can be the sweep candle of one set-up and the range candle of the next.
- **"Trades beyond"** = high > range high or low < range low; **"exactly one"** = one and not the other.
- **"Closes back inside"** = the sweep candle's close is strictly back past the swept extreme (close < range high
  after a sweep of the high, close > range low after a sweep of the low), the wording the spec itself uses in A05. A
  close exactly on the swept extreme is not a signal.
- **Side**: a sweep of the high is traded short with a bearish FVG, a sweep of the low long with a bullish FVG.
- **FVG bars**: the three 5-minute bars open in the clock hour that follows the sweep candle, so the gap also
  completes inside the 60 minutes. The limit rests from the bar after the signal to that hour's :59 bar.
- **1R** = |limit price - stop|. **"Nearer than 1R"** = distance from the limit price to the range candle's opposite
  extreme < 1R; equal is traded. The opposite extreme itself (no buffer) is the second target.
- **Only the first FVG against the sweep is the signal.** If its stop is not beyond its entry (price has since moved
  through the sweep candle's extreme and the gap formed beyond it) or the opposite extreme is nearer than 1R, the
  set-up gives no trade; the search does not move on to a later FVG.
- **Neighbours** change the exit only (all at 1R / all at the opposite extreme); the "nearer than 1R" condition stays.
  Because trades are one at a time, an earlier exit can free a later order: nb1 has 240 trades against 236.
- **`mkt`**: market at the close of the sweep candle's last 1-minute bar, stop beyond the sweep candle's extreme,
  single target at the opposite extreme, and the "nearer than 1R" condition kept, measured from that close.
- "At most 2 trades a day, one at a time" = `run_orders(max_per_day=2)`: two filled trades by signal date; an order
  signalled while a trade is open is dropped.
- **Candles 1, 2, 3** are adjacent rows of the 5-minute series inside the hour.
