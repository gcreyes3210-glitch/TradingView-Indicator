# Q2 — 1-minute VWAP with EMA 9 / 21, cross version (M14, AlgoTest)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q2.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q2.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"At a close from 09:35 to 15:44"** = the close of a 1-minute bar *stamped* 09:35 … 15:44 (the spec names a
  close by its bar, "the close of the 09:30 bar"), on a traded cash day and before the flat bar. Under the clock
  reading of YT1's common rule ("a bar closing by 11:00 has its last minute at 10:59") the window would be the bars
  stamped 09:34 … 15:43, one bar earlier at each end.
- VWAP = `ind.session_vwap` on the 1-minute bars 09:30–15:59, restarted each day; EMA(9), EMA(21) = `ind.ema` on
  the continuous 24-hour 1-minute closes.
- "Crosses above on that bar" = `ind.crossed_up`: EMA9 > EMA21 at this bar and EMA9 ≤ EMA21 at the bar before in
  the 24-hour series. All other comparisons are strict; a close equal to VWAP is neither an entry nor an exit.
- Short mirror: EMA9 crosses below EMA21, close below VWAP and below both EMAs; exit at the first close where EMA9
  crosses above EMA21 or the close is above VWAP.
- Exit = the harness's `exit_sig`: a bool array that is true at a bar when that bar's own close meets the exit
  condition (nothing after the bar is used); `simulate` reads it only on bars after the entry bar and closes at
  that bar's close. `exit_i` = the flat bar. No stop: `r_pts = 0.1 × that day's daily ATR(14)`.
- `nb1` = exit on the EMA cross only; `nb2` = exit on the VWAP close only.
- One position at a time = `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)` as the harness defines it: a signal on a bar at or
  before the previous trade's exit bar is not taken. So a signal that appears on the very bar that closes the
  previous trade (a long closed by the cross down while the close is also below VWAP and both EMAs is a short
  signal on that bar) is skipped: 547 of 8,077 signals in base, 1,245 in nb1, 547 in nb2. Every skipped signal
  in base is of that kind. The rule does not say "reverse", so it was not coded as a reversal.
