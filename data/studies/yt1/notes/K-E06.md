Claim: 98.9 % of sessions (2,545 of 2,572) touch the 08:00 open +/- 0.25 SD between 08:00 and 12:00; 74 % of those
return to the 08:00 open by 12:00 (first touch in the 08:00 hour: 79 %). No pass / fail. The check also fixes which
SD E06 uses.

to the sessions before 2023-01-01, so a full-span run reports the same choice.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Session = a cash day of the day table that has an 08:00 bar (four in-sample days of March 2020 have none: the
  market was halted); window = the 1-minute bars 08:00 .. 11:59. Roll days and early-close days are counted.
- SD = sample standard deviation (n - 1) of the 20 values BEFORE the session, never its own.
  `cc`: % change of the trading-day close (last bar of the 18:00 -> 17:00 trading date, the same daily bars as the
  day table's ATR) from the previous one, over the 20 trading dates before the session's date; the series is used
  as it is, roll jumps included. `am`: close of the last bar before 12:00 / open of the 08:00 bar - 1, over the 20
  previous sessions.
- Levels = 08:00 open x (1 +/- 0.25 x SD), not rounded. Touch = high >= upper or low <= lower; the first such bar is
  the first touch. When that one bar reaches both levels (5 `cc` days, 8 `am` days, all at 08:00 or 08:29 / 08:30)
  the side is taken from the harness path (open -> nearer extreme -> farther extreme -> close); such a bar has
  crossed the 08:00 open on the way, so it counts as reverted whichever side is called first.
- Reversion = after the touch, price trades at the 08:00 open again (a touch of it counts) in the rest of the touch
  bar's path or in any later bar up to 11:59.
- Tie in the selection rule -> `cc` (listed first in the spec). Not needed.
