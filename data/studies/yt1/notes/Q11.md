# Q11 - the lunch pattern, N11 (`tools/yt1/s_Q11.py`)

Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q11.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Base: short at the close of the 10:59 bar, out at the close of the 11:59 bar; long at that same close, out at the
  close of the 13:59 bar. Both legs every day; each leg is one trade with its own costs; the summary line scores
  the two together (n counts legs).
- **Neighbours.** "The long leg ending 12:59" = out at the close of the 12:59 bar (a bar stamp, as the base is
  written). "The short leg starting 11:30" = short at the close of the 11:29 bar: 11:30 is a clock time, as the
  base's 11:00 start is the close of the 10:59 bar. The other leg is unchanged in each.
- **Reported** `short` = the 10:59 -> 11:59 short leg alone; `long` = the 11:59 -> 13:59 long leg alone.
- **Early-close days are traded** (the entry does not exclude them, unlike Q9). An exit time after the day's flat
  bar becomes the flat bar (common rule "Flat"): on the 29 half-days in sample the long leg ends at 12:49 or 13:04
  instead of 13:59 (nb1: at 12:49 on the 13:00 halts, at 12:59 on the 13:15 halts).
- A leg whose entry or exit minute has no bar is no trade; the other leg is still taken (in sample: one long leg of
  nb1, 2020-03-18, no 12:59 bar).
- No stop: `r_pts = 0.1 x` the day table's `atr` for each leg.

## 2. `run_orders` and the back-to-back legs
The short leg's exit bar (11:59) is the long leg's signal bar. `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)` skips an order whose
signal bar is at or before the previous trade's exit bar, so it drops every long leg (checked: 898 trades, 0 long
legs). The module therefore calls `run_orders(one_at_a_time=False)`: 1,796 trades. The positions still never
overlap: the short is closed at the same close the long is opened at (checked: no trade's entry bar is before the
previous trade's exit bar), so "one position at a time" holds by construction. Roll and no-ATR days are dropped by
`run_orders` as usual.
