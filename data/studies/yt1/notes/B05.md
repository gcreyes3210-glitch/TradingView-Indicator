# B05 — Scarface first-candle break and retest

Module: `tools/yt1/s_B05.py`. Variants: `base` 2R, `nb1` 1.5R, `nb2` 3R.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Zone bar** = the last 1-minute bar, from 09:30 up to the bar before the breakout bar, whose colour is opposite to
  the breakout: close < open before a long breakout, close > open before a short one. A doji has no colour.
- **"If that is not beyond the entry"** tests the zone bar's extreme itself against the limit price, strictly (zone
  low < range high for a long). Because every bar before the breakout closed inside the range, the zone bar's extreme
  can only equal the edge, never lie on the wrong side of it; this case occurred 0 times in-sample.
- **No zone bar at all** (every bar from 09:30 to the breakout has the breakout's colour or is a doji). The spec does
  not say. It is treated like a zone bar whose extreme is not beyond the entry: the breakout bar's extreme is used,
  else no trade. In-sample this is 33 of 923 orders (30 of 822 trades); the trade `tag` marks them `brk-nozone`
  against `zone`. Under the other reading (no zone bar = no trade) the base would be the 792 `zone` trades.
- **"Else no trade"**: 3 days, where the breakout bar's low is not below the range high either (it gapped over it).
- **Breakout** = the first 1-minute close strictly outside the range at 09:35 or later; it sets the side whatever
  happens next (nothing in the spec cancels the resting order). A first close outside on the 10:59 bar or later
  leaves no bar for the order to rest on (0 days in-sample: every day broke out by 10:58).
- **Order.** Buy limit at the range high (sell limit at the range low), resting from the bar after the breakout bar
  through the last bar at or before 10:59. Stop = extreme ∓ 1 tick. Target = limit price ± k × |limit price − stop|,
  rounded to the nearest tick; both are fixed at the breakout bar's close, so a fill at a better open keeps them.

## 3. Hand check
Bars printed around 5 base trades (2019-07-10 long, 2019-10-31 long, 2021-05-26 short, 2021-12-09 long with no zone
bar, 2022-02-16 short). In each: the range matches 09:30–09:34; the breakout bar is the first close outside from
09:35; the zone bar is the last opposite-colour bar before it; stop = its extreme ∓ 1 tick; the limit fills on the
first later bar that trades back to the edge, 1 tick worse; the exit is the first touch of stop or target after the
fill (on the fill bar, by the harness's path).
Example, 2021-05-26: range 13708.75 / 13679.00. 09:45 is the first close below (13670.00). The last up bar before it
is 09:44 (13679.50 → 13681.00), high 13687.00 → stop 13687.25. Sell limit 13679.00, target 13662.50. 09:46–09:48 do
not reach 13679.00; 09:49 (high 13681.25) fills at 13678.75; 09:50 (low 13661.75) reaches the target, exit 13662.75,
+1.76 R.
Example of the fallback, 2021-12-09: all five range bars are up bars, 09:35 closes above 16390.00; no zone bar, so the
stop is the breakout bar's low 16387.50 − 1 tick = 16387.25; filled and stopped inside the 09:36 bar.
An independent vectorised derivation of the breakout bar and side matches every order, and every order's stop and
target match the bar named in its tag. 923 orders → 822 trades: 27 on contract-roll or no-ATR days, 74 never filled.
`python3 tools/yt1/run.py B05 --check`: **PASS** (16 of 16).
