# E15 - Camarilla pivots, four set-ups pooled (`tools/yt1/s_E15.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- H, L, C = `pdh`, `pdl`, `pdc` of the day table (previous cash day's 09:30-16:00 high, low, last close). The day
  table also holds the futures exchange's half sessions on US holidays, so the day after one of those takes its
  levels from a 09:30-13:00 session (49 of the 1,440 base trades).
- Signal bars: the N-minute bars opening at 09:30 or later whose last minute is 14:59 or earlier.
- "Each set-up once a day" = a set-up's one order of the day is its FIRST qualifying bar. An order signalled while
  another trade is open (or on the 1-minute bar on which it exits) is skipped by `run_orders` and is not replaced by
  a later bar of the same set-up. In-sample 52 of 1,533 orders were lost this way.
- Conditions are tested on the exact levels, strict "above" / "below". Stop and target sit AT the level (the entry
  says "stop S4", not "beyond S4"); a level between two ticks becomes the first tradable price at or through it in
  the direction price must travel (long: stop rounded down, target rounded up; short mirrored).
- One bar qualifying for two set-ups: orders issued in the spec's order (1), (2), (3), (4); the first is the trade.
  In-sample at 5 minutes: 4 bars (s1+s2 twice, s1+s4 once, s3+s4 once); at 15 minutes 17 bars.
- Tag of every order and trade = its set-up: `s1`, `s2`, `s3`, `s4`.

## 2. What the spec text produces that it probably did not intend (coded as written, not changed)
- **Stop not on the losing side of the entry: 0 orders** at every bar size. It cannot happen with these four
  set-ups: each enters on a close beyond the level that is nearer than its stop (close > S3 > S4, close > R4 > R3,
  and the mirrors).
- **Target already passed at the entry: 94 orders, 88 trades at 5 minutes** (orders: s1 3, s2 39, s3 50, s4 2;
  3-minute 81 trades, 15-minute 126). These are gap days: the first close above R4 is already above R5 (or below
  S5 for set-up 3). The spec gives no rule for it, so the order goes to the harness as written and the target fills
  at the next bar's open: a one-minute round trip. Those 88 trades: net -$278, mean R -0.017. The other 1,352:
  net +$14,546, mean R +0.055. Left in, as the text has it.

## 3. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
