# H3 — the previous-day box, edges only (Pat's Model D, Jdub's rejection play) (YT8_SPEC.md, Part 2)

Module `tools/yt1/s_H3.py`. Variants: `base` target 2R, `nb1` 1.5R, `nb2` 3R. Written from the spec text, `core.py`,
`run.py`, `s_Z00.py`; `I_jdub.md` (Model C) opened for the confirmation candle's wording. In-sample bars only
(2019-06-03 → 2022-12-30). Day rules: `core.run_orders(..., max_per_day=1, skip_roll=2)`.

## 1. Readings (R) — all fixed before the first run
1. **Previous regular session's high / low** = `days.pdh` / `days.pdl`: the previous cash day's 09:30–15:59 bars
   (checked against the raw bars in the hand check).
2. **"Open inside" (R)** includes the edges: `pdl ≤ 09:30 bar's open ≤ pdh`. 538 of 925 days; on 2 of them the open
   is exactly on an edge (2020-12-07 and 2021-11-03, both at the previous high).
3. **"From 09:30 to 11:00" (R)** = the 1-minute bars stamped 09:30 … 10:59. The touch and the entry bar are both
   among them; a touch before 09:30 does not count.
4. **"Touches" (R)** = a bar's low at or below the previous low (high at or above the previous high), the grid's
   definition of a touch. The first such bar at or after 09:30 is "the touch" and stays so for the rest of the
   window: the entry may come many bars later, and the stop then spans all of them.
5. **Entry (R).** The first bar, the touch bar or later, whose close is above the previous 1-minute bar's high and
   above the previous session's low (both strict); mirror: close below the previous bar's low and below the previous
   session's high. "The previous bar" is the 1-minute bar just before in the series; for the 09:30 bar that is the
   last pre-open bar (12 entries are on the 09:30 bar itself).
6. **First such trade of the day (R).** The first bar that qualifies on either edge; both edges are watched at once
   and one bar cannot qualify on both. Nothing after it that day.
7. **Stop.** Lowest low from the touch bar through the entry bar − 1 tick (short: highest high + 1 tick).
8. **Target kR (R).** close ± k × |close − stop|, measured on the entry bar's close before slippage (the house rule
   for R targets), `core.tick_round` nearest (only 1.5R can fall between ticks).

## 5. Odd
- Base net is +$62 with mean R −0.017: the dollar total and the mean R have opposite signs. Both are as printed.
- 256 trades on 926 cash days (28 %).
- The harness day table counts exchange holidays with a half futures session (for example 2019-07-04) as cash days,
  so "the previous regular session" on the day after one is that half session's 09:30–12:59 bars, and the rule also
  trades on such days. 3 of the 256 base trades have an early-close or holiday session as their previous day. Not
  changed (harness, not mine).
