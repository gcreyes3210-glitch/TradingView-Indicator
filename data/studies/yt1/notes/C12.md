# C12 — TTM Squeeze fire

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C12.py`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- Squeeze on = upper Bollinger (20, 2) strictly below upper Keltner and lower Bollinger strictly above lower Keltner;
  Keltner = EMA20 ± 1.5 × ATR(20) (Wilder), `ind.keltner` defaults.
- Fire = a bar with band values that is not "on" and whose preceding run of consecutive on bars is ≥ 6. A fire bar that
  does not meet the long or the short conditions gives no trade (later off bars are not "the first").
- Momentum = `ind.linreg(close − (midpoint of 20-bar high / low + SMA20) / 2, 20)`. "Rising" = above the previous bar's
  value, "falls" = below it (an equal value is neither).
- Stop = 1 tick beyond the lowest low (highest high) of the on bars of the run that just ended; the fire bar itself is
  not part of the run.
- Exit = `exit_sig` on the 1-minute index, set at the last minute of each completed N-minute bar whose momentum is
  below (long) / above (short) the previous bar's. No target. The stop stays in place.
- Signal window "closing 09:45 → 15:00" = the bar's clock close in [09:45, 15:00]: 15-minute bars opening 09:30 … 14:45;
  10-minute bars opening 09:40 … 14:50 (the 09:30 bar closes at 09:40, before the window); 30-minute bars opening
  09:30 … 14:30.
- A fire outside the window is not traded and is not carried forward.
- Data quirk: on 2020-03-12 (circuit breaker) the 09:30 15-minute bar has no minute after 09:36, so the harness's
  `i_last` puts that signal at 09:36. There is no bar from 09:37 to 09:49, so the entry price is the same as at 09:45.
