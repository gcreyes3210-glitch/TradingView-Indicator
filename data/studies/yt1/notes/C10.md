# C10 - 9 / 20 EMA "Bone Zone" first pullback

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C10.py`. Variants: `base` 3R, `nb1` 2R, `nb2` 4R (`k`), all on 5-minute bars (`bar=5`).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **Where the day's sequence starts (the main addition).** The spec gives a window only for the signal bar and says
  "first pullback" and "for the day" without saying when the day's count begins. Coded: once per cash day and per
  direction, on that day's 5-minute bars **opening at or after 09:30** (EMA9 / EMA20 themselves run on the continuous
  24-hour series). Pre-market bars take no part in the run, the pullback or the entry. Consequence: the earliest
  possible signal bar closes 09:50 (09:30, 09:35, 09:40 clear of EMA9; pullback 09:45), so the 09:45 start of the
  window never binds. The alternative, starting the count at 18:00 or midnight, would let an overnight pullback be
  "the first" and leave almost nothing for 09:45 -> 11:00; it was not coded and not run.
- **Trend established** = the first bar t (09:30 or later) at which the current run of consecutive bars with
  low > EMA9 (each bar against its own EMA9) is at least 3 long and EMA9[t] > EMA20[t]. If the run reaches 3 while
  EMA9 <= EMA20, the trend is established on the later bar of the same run where EMA9 > EMA20 first holds.
- **The pullback** = the first bar after t with low <= EMA9. One per day and direction: after it the day's long
  sequence can only enter or end, it never restarts.
- **Entry / end**, bar by bar from the pullback bar on: a close below EMA20 ends the set-up for the day; otherwise the
  first bar with close > open and close >= EMA20 is the signal bar (it may be the pullback bar itself). If that bar
  closes after 11:00 there is no trade.
- **Stop = 1 tick beyond the pullback BAR's low** (the spec defines "first pullback" as that bar; the source says
  "the low of the pullback candle"). Later bars before the entry may trade lower. When the entry close is not above
  that stop the order is invalid and the harness drops it; it is not replaced by a later bar. In-sample: 17 of 499
  candidate orders.
- **Target** = close + k x (close - stop), `core.tick_round` nearest.
- Short mirrored: run of highs < EMA9 with EMA9 < EMA20; pullback = high >= EMA9; a close above EMA20 ends it; first
  bar with close < open and close <= EMA20; stop 1 tick above the pullback bar's high.
- "One per direction per day" = at most one long and one short order a day; `run_orders` adds one position at a time.
- Not coded (judged by eye in the source, not in the spec): decreasing volume, "orderly" pullback, the half exit.
