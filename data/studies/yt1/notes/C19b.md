## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- Keltner as stated: EMA20 basis ± 2 × ATR(10) (Wilder), `ind.keltner(n=20, mult=2.0, atr_len=10)`.
- "Opens and closes above the upper Keltner" compares the bar's open and close with the upper line's value at that same
  bar. "After one that did not" = the previous bar (with a channel value) did not both open and close above its own
  upper line.
- Short mirror: opens and closes below the lower line after a bar that did not; close < EMA200.
