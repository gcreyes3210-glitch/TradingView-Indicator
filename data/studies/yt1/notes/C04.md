# C04 - Donchian(20) + 200 EMA

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C04.py` (bar size is the parameter `bar`; base 5, nb1 3, nb2 15).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Bands = `ind.donchian(high, low, 20)`: highest high / lowest low of the last 20 bars including the current one.
  "The upper band rises on this bar" = upper[k] > upper[k-1]; "the lower band falls" = lower[k] < lower[k-1].
- **What counts as a "band move".** Only the two moves the rule names: the upper band rising and the lower band
  falling (the source: "the upper band makes a new higher high, after lower band had made a lower low"). The passive
  moves, the upper band stepping down or the lower band stepping up when an old extreme leaves the 20-bar window,
  are not events. So a long is the first rise of the upper band after a fall of the lower band, with no other
  upper-band rise in between. The words "the latest band move" could also be read to include the passive moves;
  that reading would be far stricter (and would leave the order of two moves on one bar undefined on most trending
  bars, where the far band steps passively as the near one breaks out). It was not coded and not run. An example of
  the difference is the first hand-checked trade below.
- "Before it" = on an earlier bar of the continuous series (overnight bars included), whatever the close was doing
  against EMA200 at the time: the event history does not depend on the filter.
- **Event order between the two bands.** A bar is treated as one instant. When both bands move outward on the same
  bar (a bar through both 20-bar extremes), (a) the signal on that bar looks only at earlier bars, so it is a long
  if the latest earlier event was a lower-band fall, a short if it was an upper-band rise (the EMA side then
  decides which can trade); (b) for later bars that bar is the latest move of both kinds, so it qualifies the next
  upper-band rise as a long and the next lower-band fall as a short. The 1-minute bars inside the bar are not used
  to order the two moves.
- Stop = lower[k] - 1 tick (upper[k] + 1 tick), the band on the signal bar. Target = close +/- 1.5 x |close - stop|,
  `core.tick_round` nearest.
- "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = clock close in [09:35, 15:00] (3-minute: first signal bar opens 09:33).
- Decision bar `i` = `i_last` of the signal bar, as the brief defines it. Orders on a bar whose last minute has no
  1-minute bar: 0 in base, 0 in nb1, 2 of 525 in nb2, of which 1 became a trade (2020-03-09, the circuit-breaker
  open, -1.00 R).

## 3. Checks
Indicators (once, on the 5-minute series): `ind.donchian` equals pandas rolling max / min over 20 bars including the
current one (the bar 20 back is excluded); lower <= low and upper >= high on every bar. `ind.ema` equals an
independent pandas recursion.

The event logic was re-derived with a plain bar-by-bar state machine (state = rise / fall / both): it gives the
same long and short bars as the vectorised module on every tradable bar (683 / 562 in the window against the
module's 681 / 561; the 3 extra are window bars at or after the flat bar of an early-close day).

How often the tie happens (5-minute series, all hours): both bands move outward on 123 bars of 252,566. Of the
1,242 base signals, 42 are on such a bar and 62 have such a bar as their latest earlier move (nb1: 190 bars, 17 and
84 of 1,800 signals; nb2: 69 bars, 22 and 32 of 525).

Hand check, bands recomputed with pandas:

  band rising at 12:05 (9528.75 -> 9535.75). Close 9502.00 < EMA200 9567.20. Stop 9535.75 + 1 tick = 9536.00, entry
  9501.75, target 9502 - 1.5 x 34.00 = 9451.00, traded 13:35, exit 9451.25. As reported (+1.45 R). Between 12:05
  and 12:35 the lower band stepped up twice as old lows left the window (9472.50 -> 9486.75 -> 9491.50): those are
  the passive moves that the reading above does not count.
- 2019-11-08, bar 09:35 (long): upper band 8221.25 -> 8228.00 (rises); latest earlier outward move: lower band

- 2022-08-31, bar 09:30 (short): lower band 12411.75 -> 12410.50; latest earlier outward move: upper band rising at
  09:20 (to 12498.25). Close 12411.25 < EMA200 12426.16. Stop 12498.50, entry 12411.00, stopped 09:47, exit
  12498.75. As reported (-1.01 R).
