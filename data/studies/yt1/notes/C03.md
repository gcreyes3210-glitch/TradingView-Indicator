# C03 - Triple Supertrend + Stochastic RSI + 200 EMA

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C03.py` (bar size is the parameter `bar`; base 5, nb1 3, nb2 15).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "%K crosses above %D" = `ind.crossed_up` on completed bars (K[k] > D[k], K[k-1] <= D[k-1]); "the previous %K" =
  K[k-1] (< 20 for a long, > 80 for a short). EMA200, the three Supertrend directions and lines are read on bar k.
- "At least two Supertrends up" = at least two of the three direction flags are up on bar k (down for a short).
- The stop, "the second Supertrend line below the close, counting from the close": the lines of bar k that are
  strictly below close[k] are ordered from the close downwards and the second is taken. With three lines below it
  is the middle one, with two the lower (farther) one. The direction flags are not used for the stop. If fewer than
  two lines are below the close there is no stop and no order.
- A line value is off the tick grid. The stop is placed on the first tick at or beyond the line (down for a long,
  up for a short): exactly the bars that touch the line touch that tick. "The line", not "beyond" it: no extra tick.
- Target = close +/- 1.5 x |close - stop| (the stop as placed), `core.tick_round` nearest.
- "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = clock close in [09:35, 15:00] (3-minute: first signal bar opens 09:33).
- Decision bar `i` = `i_last` of the signal bar, as the brief defines it. Orders on a bar whose last minute has no
  1-minute bar: 0 of 1,645 in base, 1 of 2,497 in nb1, 1 of 396 in nb2; none of them became a trade.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results

## 3. Checks
Indicators (once, on the 5-minute series):
- `ind.supertrend` for (10,3), (12,3), (11,2), (10,1) equals my transcription of TradingView's `pine_supertrend`
  on every bar (line difference 0, direction mismatches 0). The direction turns up exactly on the bars whose close
  is above that bar's upper band and down exactly on the bars whose close is below that bar's lower band; while a
  direction holds the line never moves against it and the close is never through it.
- One property of the TradingView definition itself, not an error of `ind.py`: on a very large bar after a quiet
  stretch the band a turn switches to can be on the wrong side of the close. Of 252,566 bars: (10,1) shows "up"
  with its line above the close on 2 (2021-05-19 02:35, 2021-11-07 18:00, both outside the signal window) and "down"
  with its line below the close on 4 (2019-12-20 09:25, 2020-01-14 13:35, 2020-03-13 09:30, 2020-03-17 04:20); each
  of the wider settings does it once, "down" with the line below the close on 2020-03-13 09:30 (the open after a
  night pinned at limit-up). This is why the stop is taken from the lines' positions and not from the flags. None
  of these bars carries a C03 signal (nor do the 12 such bars of the 3-minute series or the 1 of the 15-minute).
- `ind.stoch_rsi` equals a pandas computation to 1e-9 and stays in 0..100. On 8 bars the 14-bar RSI range is zero
  and `ind.py` sets the raw value to 50 (TradingView would give no value); none is near a signal checked here.
