# B06 — Pre-market high / low break and retest

Module: `tools/yt1/s_B06.py` (the same order routine as `s_B05.py`, copied so the module stands alone). Variants:
`base` 2R, `nb1` 1.5R, `nb2` 3R.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **"As B05"** is applied to everything the entry does not change: 1-minute bars; the zone bar is the last
  opposite-colour bar **at 09:30 or later** before the breakout bar; limit at the broken level from the bar after the
  breakout to 10:59; stop beyond the zone bar's extreme, else the breakout bar's, else no trade; 2R.
- **Level** = high / low of the 1-minute bars 04:00–09:29 of the same calendar day (whatever bars exist; 56 days have
  at least one minute without a bar). **Breakout** = the first 1-minute close strictly beyond either level from the
  09:30 bar on.
- **No zone bar: the reading that matters here.** When the 09:30 bar itself closes beyond the level there is no bar
  "09:30 or later" before it, and the same holds when every bar from 09:30 to the breakout has the breakout's colour.
  The spec does not say what then. As in B05 this is treated like a zone bar whose extreme is not beyond the entry:
  the stop goes beyond the breakout bar's extreme, else no trade. In-sample this is **265 of 844 orders (228 of 740
  trades)**, 135 of them with the 09:30 bar as the breakout bar. The trade `tag` marks them `brk-nozone` against
  `zone`. Under the other reading (no zone bar = no trade) the base would be the 512 `zone` trades.
- **"If that is not beyond the entry"**: tested on the zone bar's extreme against the limit price, strictly. Every bar
  between 09:30 and the breakout closed inside the pre-market range, so the extreme can only equal the level; this
  occurred 0 times in-sample.
- **"Else no trade"**: 5 days, where the breakout bar's extreme is not beyond the level either (the bar opened and
  stayed beyond it).
- Days without an order: 74 with no close beyond either level by 10:59, 3 whose first close beyond is on the 10:59 bar
  (no bar left for the order to rest on), and the 5 above.
- Order, stop buffer and target rounding as B05; the target is measured from the limit price.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after the first run.

## 3. Hand check
Bars printed around 4 base trades (2019-09-13 short with no zone bar, 2019-11-19 short, 2020-02-26 long, 2022-07-11
short with no zone bar). In each: the levels match the 04:00–09:29 bars; the breakout bar is the first close beyond;
the zone bar (when there is one) is the last opposite-colour bar from 09:30; stop, limit fill, target and exit follow.
Example, 2020-02-26: pre-market 8930.50 / 8709.25. 09:33 is the first close above (8935.50). The last down bar before
it is 09:31, low 8887.50 → stop 8887.25. Buy limit 8930.50, target 9017.00. 09:34 (low 8903.50) fills at 8930.75; the
target is reached at 10:35, exit 9016.75, +1.95 R.
Example of the fallback, 2022-07-11: pre-market 12085.50 / 12037.25; the 09:30 bar closes 12025.25, below; no zone
bar; its high 12051.75 → stop 12052.00; sell limit 12037.25 fills in the 09:31 bar, which runs on to 12062.50: stopped
in the fill bar, exit 12052.25.
An independent vectorised derivation of the breakout bar and side matches every order, and every order's stop and
target match the bar named in its tag. 844 orders → 740 trades: 27 on contract-roll or no-ATR days, 77 never filled.
`python3 tools/yt1/run.py B06 --check`: **PASS** (16 of 16).
