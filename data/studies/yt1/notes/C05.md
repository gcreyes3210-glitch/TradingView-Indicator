# C05 - Supertrend(10, 3) flip + 200 EMA

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C05.py` (bar size is the parameter `bar`; base 5, nb1 3, nb2 15).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "Supertrend turns up on this bar" = `ind.supertrend(10, 3)` direction is up on bar k and was down on bar k-1
  (completed bars); mirror for shorts. "close > EMA200" on the same bar.
- "The Supertrend line" = the line's value on the signal bar (after a turn up, the lower band), fixed for the life
  of the trade. The article's use of the line as a trailing stop is an option it did not test; not coded.
- A line value is off the tick grid. The stop is placed on the first tick at or beyond the line (down for a long,
  up for a short): exactly the bars that touch the line touch that tick. "The line", not "beyond" it: no extra tick.
- Target = close +/- 1.5 x |close - stop| (the stop as placed), `core.tick_round` nearest.
- "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = clock close in [09:35, 15:00] (3-minute: first signal bar opens 09:33).
- Decision bar `i` = `i_last` of the signal bar, as the brief defines it. Orders on a bar whose last minute has no
  1-minute bar: 0 in base, nb1 and nb2.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. Nothing was changed after the first run.

## 3. Checks
Indicator (once, on the 5-minute series): `ind.supertrend(10, 3)` equals my transcription of TradingView's
`pine_supertrend` on every bar (line difference 0, direction mismatches 0). It turns up exactly on the bars whose
close is above that bar's upper band (3,079 turns) and down exactly on the bars whose close is below that bar's
lower band (3,078); while a direction holds the line never moves against it and the close is never through it; on
no turn-up bar is the new line at or above the close. (A turn is judged against the band of the turn bar itself,
which can be lower than the line plotted on the bar before; that is TradingView's rule.) One turn-down bar has the
new line below its close, which as a short would be a stop on the wrong side: 2020-03-13 09:30 (the open after a
night pinned at limit-up: close 7640.50, line 7630.10). It is inside the signal window but its close is above
EMA200 (7435.85), so it is not a signal. The 3-minute and 15-minute series have no such turn bar.
