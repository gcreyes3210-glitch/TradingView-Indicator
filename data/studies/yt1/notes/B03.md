# B03 — Casper 5-minute range, break and wick retest, midpoint stop

Module: `tools/yt1/s_B03.py`. Variants: `base` 2R, `nb1` 1.5R, `nb2` 3R, `c1300` (signal bars closing by 13:00).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **The cancellation.** After the break, each later 5-minute bar is taken in order: a close with range low ≤ close ≤
  range high cancels the day (a close exactly on an edge is not outside, so it is inside); a close beyond the broken
  edge with the bar having traded back to it is the signal; a close beyond the broken edge without a touch changes
  nothing. In-sample to 11:00: 364 days signal, 390 are cancelled, 169 break without a retest in the window, 3 never
  break.
- **A close beyond the opposite edge** (after a long break, a bar closing below the range low) is literally not "back
  inside the range", so it neither cancels nor signals and the side stays. This is the literal text; a reader who
  takes it as a cancellation would differ on 1 in-sample order (18 days have such a close while the set-up is live;
  17 of them end without a signal either way).
- **"Closes outside it"** for the signal = closes beyond the broken edge (close > range high for a long).
- **The signal bar is later than the breakout bar**; the breakout bar cannot be its own retest ("a later 5-minute bar").
- **Stop "beyond the range midpoint".** The midpoint is between two ticks on 459 of 926 days. The stop is the first
  tick price at least 1 tick beyond the exact midpoint (long: `tick_round(mid − 1 tick, "down")`), so 1 tick beyond on
  an even-tick range and 1.5 ticks beyond on an odd-tick one.
- **Window.** The signal bar opens 10:55 or earlier (`c1300`: 12:55). The break may be any earlier bar after 09:30.
- Target = signal close ± k × |close − stop|, rounded to the nearest tick.

## 3. Hand check
Bars printed around 5 base trades (2019-07-19 short, target; 2019-10-14 short, 2020-01-09 short, 2020-03-05 long,
2022-09-22 short, all stopped). In each: the range is the 09:30 5-minute bar; the first later close outside sets the
side; no bar closed back inside before the signal; the signal bar touches the broken edge and closes beyond it;
stop = midpoint ± 1 tick (9010.00 on 2020-01-09, where the midpoint is 9009.625); target = 2 × (close − stop).
Example, 2019-07-19: range 7974.75 / 7955.25, midpoint 7965.00. 09:35 closes 7955.00 (below): short side. 09:40 has
high 7959.50 ≥ 7955.25 and closes 7951.00 → short, fill 7950.75, stop 7965.25, target 7922.50, reached at 10:49,
exit 7922.75, +1.86 R.
An independent re-derivation from 1-minute bars (own 5-minute buckets) gave the same 364 orders (bar, side, stop,
target). 364 orders → 354 trades: the 10 others are on contract-roll or no-ATR days.
`python3 tools/yt1/run.py B03 --check`: **PASS** (16 of 16).
