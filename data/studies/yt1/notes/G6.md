# G6 - TTrades scalping model  (tools/yt1/s_G6.py)

Rule as coded: bias from `tt.bias`; a clock-hour candle opening 08:00 ... 14:00 that is a candle 2 or candle 3
closure (`tt.closure2`, `tt.closure3`) in the bias direction; in the next hour, the first of its two opening
15-minute candles (hh:00, hh:15) that is a candle 2 closure in that direction; then the first 1-minute CISD
(`tt.cisd`) in that direction on a bar after that 15-minute candle has closed, not before 09:30; entry at its close,
stop 1 tick beyond the protected low / high, target 2R (nb1 1.5R, nb2 3R), exit at the hour's last bar or the flat
bar. No "reported" variants are registered for G6.

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **(R) Hourly candles** are the clock hours on the cash date (`ctx.span`). The gate candle and the one or two
  candles before it must each hold at least half their minutes (30): Part 1's "a candle needs at least half its
  minutes to count", carried into Part 2. "The previous candle" is the clock hour immediately before; the 08:00 gate
  therefore uses the 07:00 and 06:00 candles.
- **(R) 15-minute candles** are the clock quarters; no minimum number of minutes is asked of them (Part 1's sentence
  names daily, 4-hour and 1-hour candles only). The candle 2 closure of the hh:00 candle is measured against the
  hh-1:45 candle, i.e. the last quarter of the gate hour; that of the hh:15 candle against the hh:00 candle.
- **(R) Which 15-minute candle.** They are taken in time order: if hh:00 qualifies the 1-minute search starts at
  hh:15, otherwise if hh:15 qualifies it starts at hh:30. (If both qualify the first CISD after the earlier one is
  the first CISD in either reading.)
- **(R) "After that 15-minute candle closes"** = the CISD bar is a 1-minute bar opening at or after the quarter's
  end. A CISD printed on the quarter's own last minute is not used and, being the first close through that run, uses
  the run up (hand check 4).
- **(R) "From 09:30 on"** = the CISD bar is the 09:30 bar or later. It only matters for the 09:00 hour.
- **(R) The CISD is read on the continuous 1-minute series**: the run it closes through may have started before the
  15-minute candle closed (or before 09:30); only the CISD bar must be inside the window.
- **(R) The CISD must be inside the same hour**, and it is the first one or none: a first CISD on the hour's last
  bar is dropped by the harness and no other is taken (2 of 781 orders).
- **(R) One trade per hour, not per day**: up to seven hours a day (09:00 ... 15:00) can trade. Every trade is out
  by its hour's last bar, so "one position at a time" never binds; `run_orders` enforces it anyway.
- **Target** = CISD close +/- k x |CISD close - stop|, before slippage, on the tick grid (YT1 common rules).

## 4. Counts and anything odd
- 781 orders; 749 of the 926 days carry a bias (per hour, long / short: 09 55/56, 10 77/52, 11 70/54, 12 50/35, 13 54/39, 14 66/56,
  15 70/47): 6 on roll / no-ATR days, 2 signalled on their hour's last bar, 773 trades. At most 5 trades in a day.
- Exits: 489 stop, 186 target, 98 time. Average risk 14.8 points (median 11.75), so the 2 ticks of slippage and $2
  of commission cost about 0.1R a trade before anything else.
- 70 hourly candles 06:00 ... 14:00 hold fewer than 30 minutes in-sample: the 13:00 and 14:00 hours of the 29
  early-close days (58) and 12 hours between 06:00 and 09:59 on the limit-down mornings of 2020-03-09, 03-13, 03-16
  and 03-18. A gate that needs one of them is skipped.
- Nothing found wrong in core.py, ind.py, run.py or tt.py. `tt.cisd` on all 1.26 million 1-minute bars takes under
  a second; it is computed once per context and reused by the three variants.
