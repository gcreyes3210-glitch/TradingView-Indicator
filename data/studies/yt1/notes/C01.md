# C01 - MACD + 200 EMA

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C01.py` (bar size is the parameter `bar`; base 5, nb1 3, nb2 15).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "Crosses above its signal" = `ind.crossed_up` on completed bars: macd[k] > signal[k] and macd[k-1] <= signal[k-1].
- "With both lines below zero" is read on the cross bar: macd[k] < 0 and signal[k] < 0 (mirror: both > 0).
- "close > EMA200" = close[k] > EMA200[k] of the same bar series (continuous 24-hour series, from the first bar).
- "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = the bar's clock close (open + bar minutes) is in [09:35, 15:00]. On 3-minute bars
  the first signal bar is the one opening 09:33 (the 09:30 bar closes 09:33), on 15-minute bars the one opening 09:30.
- Swing stop = lowest low of bars k-9..k minus 1 tick (highest high plus 1 tick); the 10 bars are of the continuous
  series, so an early signal's window reaches into the pre-market.
- Target = close +/- 1.5 x |close - stop| through `core.tick_round` (nearest; an exact half tick goes to the even
  tick, which is how `round` behaves in the harness).
- Decision bar `i` = `i_last` of the signal bar, as the brief defines it. Bars whose last minute has no 1-minute bar
  (thin minutes in June 2019, the March 2020 halts) are therefore "known" one or more minutes early: 0 orders in
  base, nb1 or nb2 are on such a bar.
