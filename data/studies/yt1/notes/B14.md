# B14 - IB breakout with VWAP side and a 1.5 x IB target, samjNQ (`tools/yt1/s_B14.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- A day's long signal is the first 5-minute bar (opening 10:30 .. 14:25) whose close is above the IB high AND above
  the VWAP at that close, both strict; the short is the mirror. A close above the IB high but under the VWAP is
  passed over and a later bar can still be the signal.
- "One trade per direction per day, one at a time": each day yields at most one long and one short order, run
  through `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)`. Under the harness rule an order whose signal bar is at or before the
  previous trade's exit bar is skipped, and it is NOT replaced by a later qualifying close ("the first close").
  The stop of a long sits 1 tick under the IB low, so by the time a 5-minute bar closes below the IB low the long
  has already been stopped; the only orders lost are those whose signal minute is the very minute the other side's
  stop was hit. In-sample: 3 of 893 orders (2020-07-16, 2021-03-26, 2022-09-20).
- VWAP: hlc3 x volume of the 1-minute bars from the 09:30 bar (`ind.session_vwap`), read at the 5-minute bar's last
  1-minute bar.
- Target = broken level +/- k x W, rounded to the nearest tick. No IB-size filter (the spec has none).

None. The module was not changed after its first run.

  14615.75 + 148.5 = 14764.25, hit 14:30 (high 14766.75; 14:29 high 14763.75). As spec.
- 2022-05-02, both directions: long at 10:34 (close 13015.25 > 12994.25, VWAP 12880.46), stop 12742.25, stopped
  13:49; short at 14:19, stop 12994.50, target 12742.50 - 377.625 -> 12365.00, stopped 15:28. Two trades, one per
  direction, the second after the first had exited. As spec.

- 2021-07-05 (short session): the only signal is at 12:54, after the flat bar -> no trade.
- In-sample: 893 orders, 863 trades, 74 days with two trades, none with more.
