# E06 - NQ Stats AM TBR, fade toward the 08:00 open (`tools/yt1/s_E06.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **SD reading: `am`, the 08:00 -> 12:00 % change**, constant `SD_READING` in the module. It is the reading K-E06's
  rule selected (in-sample touch rate 98.78 % against 95.67 % for close-to-close; published 98.95 %), decided from
  the touch rate before any E06 trade existed. The SD of a session is the sample SD of the 20 previous sessions'
  values, never its own (`k_E06.sd_table`).
- Touch = a 1-minute bar's high >= open x (1 + 0.25 SD) or low <= open x (1 - 0.25 SD), levels not rounded; the
  session's first such bar must lie in 08:00-09:59. A first-touch bar that reaches both levels gives no trade (8
  in-sample days, all at 08:00 or 08:29 / 08:30).
- Sequence: the order is placed at the close of the touch bar and rests from the next bar through the 09:59 bar.
  Upper touch -> sell limit at open x (1 + 0.44 SD); lower touch -> buy limit at open x (1 - 0.44 SD); nearest tick.
  When the next bar already opens beyond the limit price the harness fills at that open (58 of 611 trades).
- Stop = 1 tick beyond open x (1 +/- 1.0 SD), taken to the next tick away from the entry. Target = the 08:00 open.
  Exit bar = the last bar before 12:00 (the 11:59 bar).
- The order is not cancelled when price is back at the 08:00 open before it fills (the entry does not say so).
- `atlevel` is the same code with 0.25 SD: the limit goes in after the touch bar, so it fills at the next open if
  price is still beyond the level, otherwise on a later return to it. An entry inside the touch bar itself is not
  expressible with the decision at that bar's close.
- Sessions without an 08:00 bar (four days of March 2020) have no trade.
