# B09 - IB75, Dan Cooke (`tools/yt1/s_B09.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Direction: IB high set first and the 10:29 close in the top quarter -> buy limit at IB high - W / 4, target the IB
  high; IB low set first and the close in the bottom quarter -> sell limit at IB low + W / 4, target the IB low.
  "Set first" = the 1-minute bar that first set the extreme is the earlier one; same-minute extremes have no "first"
  and give no trade (none in-sample). The quarter includes its line, tested on the unrounded line.
- Stop: 1 tick beyond the level `stop_frac` x W from that extreme (0.50 = the midpoint). The neighbours (0.40 W,
  0.60 W) keep the same 1-tick buffer so that base is the same formula. A level between ticks goes to the next tick
  away from the entry; the entry goes to the nearest tick.
- VWAP: hlc3 x volume of 1-minute bars, restarting at the first bar of the trading day (18:00 the previous evening,
  Sunday 18:00 for a Monday), from `ind.session_vwap`, read at the close of the IB's last bar (10:29). "Lies between
  entry and target" is strict: entry < VWAP < target for a long, target < VWAP < entry for a short.
- The spec gives this rule no cancel-if-target-first, so the limit keeps resting to 14:59 even after the extreme has
  traded again (the 2021-10-20 trade below is such a case).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run. Because the rule trades so rarely the code was checked against
the spec once more, as the brief asks: 926 in-sample days -> high set first 454, of which the close is in the top
quarter on 28; low set first 472, close in the bottom quarter on 12. 40 set-ups, 9 removed by the VWAP filter, 1 on a
roll day -> 30 trades (every order filled). The set-up is rare by construction (the close has to sit next to the
OLDER extreme); the source itself says about once a month. With about 9 a year the full span will not reach 100.
