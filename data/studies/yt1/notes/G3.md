# G3 - TTrades: EQ continuation day (`tools/yt1/s_G3.py`)

Spec: `YT4_SPEC.md` Part 2, G3. `cont` days only. Respect (R): no clock-hour candle since 18:00 has closed below EQ.
Trigger: the first 5-minute bullish CISD of the morning that closes above EQ. Entry at its close, stop 1 tick beyond
the protected low, target 2R, flat bar, one trade a day. Neighbours (default): 1.5R (nb1), 3R (nb2). Reported: `pdh`
= target candle 1's high instead of 2R, no trade if it is nearer than 1R. Short = mirror.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Day and direction: `tt.bias` with kind `cont` (+1 long, -1 short); EQ = (h1 + l1) / 2 from the same row.
- (R) "Clock-hour candle since 18:00" = the 60-minute buckets (`ctx.bars(60)`) of the trading day, from 18:00 the
  evening before (Sunday for a Monday), whose hour has ended by the end of the signal bar (a signal bar ending 10:00
  sees the 09:00 hour). Part 1's "half its minutes" rule for candles is not applied. "Closed below EQ" = close < EQ.
- (R) "Closes above EQ" = close > EQ (strict). A morning CISD that closes at or below EQ is passed over and the next
  one is looked at ("the first ... that closes above EQ"). Respect is permanent: once an hour has closed beyond EQ
  the day is over.
- (R) Nothing is required of the protected low: it may lie below EQ (13 of the 339 base orders).
- (R) `pdh`: the trigger bar is the same one. Target = candle 1's high (`h1` of the 18:00 -> 17:00 candle, not the
  RTH high), shorts candle 1's low. "Nearer than 1R" = target - close < close - stop, before slippage; then there is
  no trade that day (a later CISD is not taken instead). Exactly 1R away trades.
- "Of the morning" applies to the CISD bar (opens at or after 08:30, last minute 10:59 or earlier); `tt.cisd` runs
  on the continuous 5-minute series. Bar acted on at `i_last`; on every order that is the bucket's nominal last minute.
- Target = close +/- k x |close - stop|, rounded to the tick.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module is as first written.
