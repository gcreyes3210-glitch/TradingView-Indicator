## 1. Readings made where the spec text did not settle something (one per line)
1. "The previous RTH low" is `days.pdl`: the 09:30-15:59 low of the previous row of the harness day table, which counts holiday half-sessions as days (for 2019-11-29 the previous day is Thanksgiving Day 2019-11-28, 09:30-13:00).
2. "Below" is strict: 09:30 open < previous RTH low; the base has no minimum gap (1 tick is enough).
3. Neighbour gap = previous RTH low - 09:30 open, taken when gap >= 0.05 (0.10) x daily ATR(14), inclusive.
4. "To 15:00": the order is cancelled at 15:00:00, so the last bar on which it can fill is the bar stamped 14:59. (In-sample the other reading, 15:00 bar included, gives the same trades: on no unfilled day does the 15:00 bar reach the level. On 6 unfilled days the level first trades between 15:01 and 15:59.)
5. The buy stop is placed at the 09:30 bar's close whatever that close is: no extra condition that price still be below the level. If a bar opens at or above the level the harness fills at that open + 1 tick (13 of the 78 in-sample fills, all of them at the 09:31 open after the 09:30 bar itself had already traded back up to the low).
6. The stop price is the previous low itself, no tick buffer (the level is already on the tick grid).
7. One order a day; the position is closed at the flat bar; on early-close days the order rests until the flat bar.
8. No price stop, so R unit = 0.1 x that day's daily ATR(14) (`ctx.days.atr`).
9. `both` (reported): the mirror is 09:30 open above the previous RTH high (`days.pdh`) -> sell stop at that high, same window, same exit; the two set-ups cannot occur on the same day.

Nothing else was added: signal bar = the 09:30 bar, `etype='stop'` resting from 09:31, roll days and no-ATR days
dropped by `core.run_orders`.
