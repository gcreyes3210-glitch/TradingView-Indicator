## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Trade count** as D03: any number a day, one position at a time (`base`, `nb1`, `nb2`, through
  `core.run_orders`, which is exact here because the entry is at the signal bar's close); the common rule's one
  trade per day is coded as `day1`, `day1_nb1`, `day1_nb2` (not in the spec's list).
- **"Top 3 price rows"** = the three highest rows of bar N's footprint table (one row per price traded); mirror the
  three lowest. A price with no row traded nothing (volume 0 in the diagonal comparison). Ratio 3, floor 10.
- **Bars N and N+1** are footprint bars five minutes apart, both opening 09:30 ... 11:25. Bar N+1's close and the
  two bars' highs / lows are those of the MNQ 5-minute bars built from the 1-minute bars; the footprint's imbalance
  price is compared with the MNQ close unchanged; the decision is at the close of the MNQ 1-minute bar T+4 of N+1.
- **"Closes below"** is strict. Mirror: a sell imbalance in bar N's bottom 3 rows and bar N+1 closing above the
  highest such price -> long, stop beyond the lower of the two lows.
- **Both sides at the same close** (bar N has a buy imbalance at its top and a sell imbalance at its bottom, and
  N+1 closes between them) = no trade: 526 of the 4,013 bar pairs with a signal. That leaves 3,487 orders on 419
  days (8.3 a day); one position at a time keeps 1,178.
- **Stop** = 1 tick beyond; **target** = close -/+ 2 x |close - stop| on the tick grid.
- Consequence of the text, not a reading: "closes below the lowest such imbalance price" is met by any close under
  a price near bar N's high, so the pattern fires on about 40 % of bar pairs; the mirror fires when N+1 closes
  anywhere above an imbalance at the low of a large up bar (2020-03-12 below).
