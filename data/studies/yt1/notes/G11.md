# G11 — break-and-retest grid (YT8_SPEC.md, Part 1): coder's record

The coder's session ended before it wrote this file; the coordinator assembled it from the code and logs.

- **Definitions as coded:** the docstring at the top of `tools/yt1/g11_sim.py` states every level, break, entry, stop,
  target, re-entry and filter exactly as implemented (prices handled as integer ticks).
- **Readings (R):**
  - `D fvg`: the three bars of the 1-minute fair value gap must lie among the 1-minute bars of the breaking candle and
    of the two K-type candles before it (the spec's "the two before it" read as candles of the break's timeframe).
  - "No entry after the cut-off": the entry bar must close at or before the cut-off, so a resting limit expires with
    the 10:59 / 11:59 bar.
  - Half-tick midlines: the tick below for a long, above for a short; off-grid `near` stops: the next tick away from
    the entry.
  - `pd` with `edge`: the order waits until the 09:30–09:44 width is known, because its stop needs it.
  - Re-entry only after a first trade that ended at its stop on a bar closing by the cut-off.
  - VWAP: typical price (h + l + c) / 3 weighted by 1-minute volume from the 09:30 bar.
- **In-sample output:** `logs/G11_is.txt` (also `data/studies/yt1/is/G11_printout.txt`).
- **Grid vs standard runner:** `logs/G11_recon.txt` — every pick, neighbour and check variant matches on trades, net to
  the cent and mean R; 96 of 96 random combinations match trade by trade, covering every level of every choice.
- **Look-ahead (mirrored future):** `logs/G11_lookahead.txt` — PASS for all six picks, their neighbours and 14 check
  combinations, and separately for the re-entry orders alone. The first 13 lines were run by the coder, the last 19 by
  the coordinator with the same command (`python3 tools/yt1/s_G11.py --check ...`).
- **Hand-checked days:** `logs/G11_hand.txt`.
- **Counts:** 25,920 combinations, all with a trade; 15,967 with at least 215 in-sample trades; 3,698 of those with a
  positive in-sample mean R.
