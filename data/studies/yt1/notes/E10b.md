# E10b - 15:00 continuation (`tools/yt1/s_E10b.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- The decision bar is the 1-minute bar stamped 14:59 (neighbours 14:29, 15:29). A day without that bar, or on which
  it is at or after the flat bar, is not traded: the 29 early-close / holiday sessions and 2019-06-18 (no 14:59 bar)
  drop out, leaving 896 of 926 in-sample days.
- "The day's range so far" = highest high / lowest low of the 1-minute bars from 09:30 through the decision bar,
  inclusive; "the middle" is their average, not rounded. "Above" / "below" are strict, for the open and the middle.
- Stop = 1 tick beyond the 09:30 open (the common buffer rule). No target: market entry at the decision bar's
  close, out at the stop or the flat bar.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
