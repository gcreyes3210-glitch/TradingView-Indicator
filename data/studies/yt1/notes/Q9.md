# Q9 - the rest of the day predicts the last half-hour, N02 (`tools/yt1/s_Q9.py`)

Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q9.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Previous regular session's close** = the day table's `pdc` (`ctx.days`: the previous cash day's last
  09:30-16:00 close; after a half-day that is the half-day's last regular bar).
- **"Decided at 15:00" / "at 15:45"** (the neighbours) = at the close of the 14:59 / 15:44 bar, as the base's 15:30
  decision is written "the close of the 15:29 bar".
- Long when the decision close is above `pdc`, short when below; an equal close is no trade (in sample: none for
  base, 1 day for nb1, 2022-01-14, and 3 for nb2, 2019-09-06, 2021-06-01, 2021-09-30). A day whose decision minute
  has no bar is no trade (none among the eligible full days in sample).
- "Not on early-close days" = the day table's `early` flag (29 in sample, all calendar half-days).
- No stop: `r_pts = 0.1 x` the day table's `atr` (daily ATR(14) through the previous trading day). Market entry at
  the decision bar's close, out at the flat bar (15:59), house fills.
- `big` = the same, only when |decision close - pdc| is at least 0.5 x that ATR.
- Roll days (where `pdc` is the old contract's price) and days without a daily ATR are dropped by `run_orders`.
