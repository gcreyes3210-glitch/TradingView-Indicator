# C19a — TRADING RUSH Ichimoku cross above the cloud + EMA200

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C19a.py` (also holds `frame_orders`, the frame shared by C19b–e).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- Signal window "bars closing 09:35 → 15:00" = the bar's clock close (open + N minutes) lies in [09:35, 15:00]: 5-minute
  bars opening 09:30 … 14:55, 3-minute 09:33 … 14:57, 15-minute 09:30 … 14:45.
- The swing stop's "last 10 bars" are the last 10 bars of the continuous series (at the 09:30 bar they are mostly
  pre-market bars).
- Cloud at bar k = span A and span B computed at bar k − 26 (as instructed); "above the cloud" = close above the higher
  of the two, "below" = below the lower of the two.
- Cross = `ind.crossed_up / crossed_dn` (above now, at or below on the previous bar).
- Short mirror: conversion crosses below base, close below the cloud, close < EMA200.
- Target = signal close ± 1.5 × |signal close − stop| through `core.tick_round` (nearest; an exact half tick goes to the
  even tick, Python's rounding).
