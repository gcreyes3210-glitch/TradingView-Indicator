# K-E04 - Noon Curve, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_E04.py`)

Claim (nqstats.com/noon_curve.html, video Gpg23dwy2Tk; 2016-2026, n = 2,479): the 08:00-16:00 high and low form on
opposite sides of noon 72.81 %; among those opposite-side sessions, after Q2 broke Q1's high only the PM sets the
high 82.12 %, after the low only the PM sets the low 72.42 %. The spec asks for the second pair **without** the
conditioning on an outcome that is not known at noon. No pass / fail: measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions (the source's, research note E entry 4; all fixed before the first run)
- AM = 08:00-11:59, PM = 12:00-15:59, Q1 = 08:00-09:59, Q2 = 10:00-11:59.
- The PM makes the session high when it trades >= 1 tick above the AM high (an equal high belongs to the AM, which
  set it first); the low likewise. Opposite sides = exactly one of the two; both in the AM = neither; both in the
  PM = both.
- Q2 broke Q1's high = Q2 traded >= 1 tick above it; "high only" = and not 1 tick below Q1's low.
- Sessions: cash days with bars in Q1, Q2 and the PM (all 926; roll days included, the contract never changes within
  the session). **Headline = full sessions** (897; the 29 early-close days have a cut afternoon); all days beside.
- Two tables: "known at noon" (every session with that Q2 outcome; P(PM makes the high), P(PM makes the low), and
  the two "only" shares) and the source's own table restricted to opposite-side sessions.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.

## 3. Hand check
- The split recomputed by a second route (vectorised groupby of AM / PM highs and lows, full sessions): opposite
  72.91 %, both in the AM 21.96 %, n 897: identical.
- 2019-08-07: Q1 7524.75 / 7387.75, Q2 7533.25 / 7420.00 (high only), PM 7577.25 / 7448.25 -> PM makes the high, not
  the low: opposite. Module agrees.
- 2021-02-23: Q1 13030.50 / 12757.50, Q2 13119.25 / 12860.75 (high only), PM 13257.00 / 12884.75 -> PM high only.
- 2022-10-13: Q1 11035.00 / 10484.50, Q2 11113.75 / 10596.75 (high only), PM 11142.25 / 10914.00 -> PM high only.
