# E10a - First hour continuation (`tools/yt1/s_E10a.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- The decision bar is the 1-minute bar stamped 10:29 (neighbours 09:59, 10:59). A day on which that minute has no
  bar would not be traded (it never happens in-sample for 10:29).
- "Above" / "below" the 09:30 open are strict; a 10:29 close equal to the open is no trade (one in-sample day,
  2020-02-14).
- No stop and no target: market entry at the decision bar's close, out at the flat bar. R unit = 0.1 x the day's
  daily ATR(14) (`r_pts`), the common rule for a rule without a stop.
- US-holiday short sessions are rows of the harness day table and are traded like any other day (out at their own
  flat bar).
