# A10 - First presented FVG after 09:30  (tools/yt1/s_A10.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Neighbours on 2- and 3-minute bars**: the three bars open at 09:30 or later and the third bar's last minute is
  09:59 or earlier (it opens by 09:58 on 2-minute bars, by 09:57 on 3-minute bars). The signal is known at the close
  of the third bar's last 1-minute bar.
- **`ce` midpoint off the tick grid**: moved to the first tradable price at or through the midpoint (rounded down
  for a buy limit, up for a sell limit), since a limit at the exact midpoint could not fill a tick earlier. The
  signal is still the day's first FVG, whether or not its limit fills. 2R is measured from the limit price.
- **`2R`**: target = candle 3's close +/- 2 x |close - stop|, on the tick grid.
- **Candles 1, 2, 3** are adjacent rows of the bar series inside 09:30-09:59. A minute without a trade has no bar
  (this happens on thin 2019 days and around the March 2020 halts), so three adjacent bars are not always three
  adjacent clock minutes.
