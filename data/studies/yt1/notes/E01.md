# E01 - NQ Stats Hour Stats, fade the first breach (`tools/yt1/s_E01.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Timing (the one place the harness cannot express the text).** The hour's open is needed for the "opens strictly
  inside" test, the distance test, the target and the R unit, and it is not known at the close of the previous
  hour's last bar. `simulate` cannot rest an order from a bar's open, so the decision bar is the hour's **first**
  1-minute bar (the HH:00 bar; its open is the hour's open) and the two limits rest from HH:01 through HH:19, not
  from HH:00. If that first bar has itself already traded at either limit price, the hour's first breach is over
  before an order can rest and the hour is skipped. Size of this: on traded days 1,050 hours are skipped for it; in
  143 of them the level touched was >= 0.10 % from the open (i.e. would have been a base trade with an order resting
  from the open). The other 907 would have been "first breach at a level too near" = no trade anyway.
- **"The first to fill is the trade; the level must be >= 0.10 %".** Both limits always compete for the first fill.
  The first to fill is the hour's trade, and it is taken only if its own level (previous hour's high for the sell,
  low for the buy; the level, not the limit price 1 tick beyond it) is >= x % of the hour's open away from the
  hour's open. A first fill at a nearer level = no trade that hour, even if the far side fills later (that would not
  be the first breach). `orders()` returns both orders and marks a too-near one with a tag ending in "x".
- Hours are clock hours; the previous hour of 09:00 is 08:00-08:59, taken from whatever bars it has (skipped if none).
- R unit = |limit price - hour's open| (the order's price, before slippage). `stop1`: stop = limit price plus /
  minus that same distance.
- Exit bar = the hour's last 1-minute bar, or the day's flat bar if earlier. Both filling in the same bar = neither
  (never happened in-sample). Roll days and days without a daily ATR are dropped in `trades()`.
