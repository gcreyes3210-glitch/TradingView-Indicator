# Q5 — Bollinger fade with an ADX filter, 5-minute bars (M15, CrossTrade)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q5.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q5.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"Signal bar closing 09:50 to 15:25" was read as the 5-minute bars *stamped* 09:50 … 15:25** (last minutes 09:54
  … 15:29, entries at the opens of the bars stamped 09:55 … 15:30), the same convention as the other Part 4
  entries, which name a close by its bar ("the close of the 09:30 bar", "a close in 09:30–11:29"). **This is the
  one place where the text can be read another way that matters:** YT1's common rule says "a bar closing by 11:00
  has its last minute at 10:59", under which the signal bars would be those stamped 09:45 … 15:20 (one 5-minute bar
  earlier at each end). The reading was fixed before the first run and not revisited. To switch, change `WIN` in
  the module to `(9 * 60 + 45, 15 * 60 + 20)`.
- 5-minute bars = `ctx.bars(5)`; a bar is used only at the close of its last 1-minute bar (`i_last`). ADX(14) =
  `ind.dmi(...)[2]`, ATR(14) = `ind.atr`, Bollinger(20, 2) = `ind.bollinger` (SMA 20, population sd), all on the
  continuous 24-hour 5-minute series.
- Body = |close − open|. "At most 25" is ≤; "at or below the lower band" is ≤; the wick test is strict (a bar with
  no body and any lower wick passes).
- Stop = signal low − ATR(14) at the signal bar, put on the tick grid with `core.tick_round` (nearest).
- Target = the middle band at the signal bar, `core.tick_round` (nearest), fixed. "No trade if that is not above
  the signal close" is applied to the target as placed (the rounded value must be above the signal close).
- Entry: `etype="open"` with `i` = the signal bar's last 1-minute bar, so the fill is the open of the next 1-minute
  bar = the next 5-minute bar's open. Stop and target are not moved for that open; the harness refuses an order
  whose stop is already on the wrong side of the fill. If the open is already beyond the target the harness still
  books the target at its price on the bar's first favourable leg (its rule, not worked around).
- Time exit: the entry bar is the bar after the signal bar; the exit is the close (`i_last`) of the 15th bar after
  it (signal bar + 16 in the series, 79 minutes after the entry bar opens), or the flat bar if that comes first.
- Short mirror: high at or above the upper band; upper wick (high − max(open, close)) more than 1.5 × the body and
  the close below the high; stop 1 × ATR above the high; target the middle band, which must be below the close.
- `nb1` band 2.5; `nb2` ADX at most 20; `noadx` no ADX test; `m1` the same code with every bar a 1-minute bar
  (stamps 09:50 … 15:25, indicators on 1-minute bars, time exit 15 one-minute bars after the entry bar).
- One position at a time = `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)`: a signal on a bar at or before the previous trade's
  exit bar is skipped.
