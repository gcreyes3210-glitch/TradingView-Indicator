# K-D15 - Exhaustion prints (Orderflows), claim check  (tools/yt1/k_D15.py)

Spec: "New-session-high bars whose total volume at the high price is <= 9 contracts against the rest: share whose
high holds for the next six bars." No figure is published; the direction claimed is that a small print marks the
end of the move, so its extreme should hold more often than the rest. No pass / fail.

Data check of the footprint: notes/D03.md, section 0. This check reads only footprint prices.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- New session high and event bars exactly as K-D07: footprint prices, earlier bars from 09:30, events 09:35 ... 11:25.
- Exhaustion print = buy + sell at the high price <= 9 contracts; the rest = more than 9.
- "Holds for the next six bars" = none of the next six footprint bars trades above the high (equal is a hold).
- The window ends at the day's last footprint bar (11:30), so events opening after 11:00 have fewer than six bars
  to survive. Headline = every event, window cut there; `full_window_only` = events with all six bars (opening by
  11:00). Both are in the JSON and below.
- Low mirror (new-session-low bars, volume at the low price, the low holds), and pooled = both together.
- All 419 footprint days are counted. On NQ a print of <= 9 contracts at the extreme is the usual case (2,349 of
  3,456 events), not the rare one the video describes on bonds.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
