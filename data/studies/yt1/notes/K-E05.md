## 1. Definitions (the source's, research note E entry 5; all fixed before the first run)
- For cash day D: Asia = 20:00-23:59 on the calendar day before D plus 00:00-01:59 on D; London = 02:00-07:59; New
  York = 08:00-15:59. All 926 cash days have bars in all three and one contract throughout (roll days included).
  An early-close day (29) ends at its halt; the table without those days is printed beside.
- A break = trading >= 1 tick beyond the level. P1: London broke Asia's high and low; P2: neither; P3: the high
  only; P4: the low only. (London entirely outside Asia's range: 0 days, so P3 / P4 here are exactly E05's patterns.)
- New York breaks London's high = some bar 08:00-15:59 with high >= London high + 1 tick; low mirror. High first =
  its first breaking bar is the earlier one; both first broken in the same bar is counted apart. Nothing before
  08:00 excludes a day (the source states no such exclusion).
