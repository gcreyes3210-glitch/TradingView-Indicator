# Q10 - the overnight drift hour, N06 (`tools/yt1/s_Q10.py`)

Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q10. The hour-by-hour description that goes with it: `notes/K-Q10.md`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Windows written as clock times.** The base is written with bars: buy at the close of the 01:59 bar, sell at
  the close of the 02:59 bar, which is the clock window 02:00-03:00 (N06's hour). The neighbours and `eu` are
  written as clock windows and were read the same way: a-b = from the close of the bar ending at a to the close of
  the bar ending at b.
  - nb1 01:30-03:30 = the 01:29 bar -> the 03:29 bar. nb2 02:00-03:30 = the 01:59 bar -> the 03:29 bar.
  - `eu` 23:30-03:30 = the **23:29** bar -> the 03:29 bar. (The coder's brief speaks of "the 23:30 bar"; by the
    base's own convention the 23:30 start is the close of the bar stamped 23:29. One minute's difference.)
- **Trading day** = a cash day of the day table (a day with a regular session). Its night starts at 18:00 the
  evening before. The 01:xx-03:xx bars carry the cash day's own calendar date; the 23:29 bar of `eu` is on the
  previous calendar date (a Sunday for a Monday) and belongs to the same trading day. Bars are found by time
  (`ctx.idx`), never by index arithmetic. Trading days without a regular session (full holidays on which the night
  session ran) have no ATR or roll flag in the day table and are not traded.
- A day on which the entry minute or the exit minute has no bar is no trade (base: 6 of 898 eligible days:
  2019-09-11, 2019-12-13, 2020-01-24, 2020-03-09, 2020-03-17, 2020-03-18: a minute with no trade in 2019's thin
  nights, or a stretch without bars in March 2020).
- Early-close days are traded like any other: the regular-session flat bar plays no part; `exit_i` is the sell bar.
- No stop: `r_pts = 0.1 x` the `atr` of the cash day the night leads into (daily ATR(14) through the previous
  trading day, complete at 17:00, before the night begins).

## 2. `run_orders` and the overnight orders (a harness limit, how it was handled)
`core.run_orders` looks up roll, no-ATR and "is a cash day" by the **calendar date of the signal bar**.
- For base, nb1 and nb2 (signal bars 01:29 / 01:59 on the cash day's own date) that is the trading day and nothing
  is lost. The flat bar is no obstacle either: `simulate` only needs `i < exit_i`.
- For `eu` the signal bar is on the previous calendar date. `run_orders` would drop all 183 Sunday-evening orders
  (Sunday is not a cash day: every Monday lost), read the roll flag one day off (trade the night into a roll day
  and skip the night after it) and return 709 trades instead of 892.
- So `trades()` applies the same three filters by **trading day** (`ctx.tdate` of the signal bar), keeps one
  position at a time, and passes each order to `core.simulate`: the harness still does every fill. For base, nb1
  and nb2 the result is identical, trade for trade, to `core.run_orders` on the same orders (892 / 890 / 893,
  checked).
- The contract changes at 19:00 or 20:00 New York on the evening before a roll day (15 changes in sample), inside
  the roll trading day, so no kept trade spans a contract change (checked: 0 in every variant).
