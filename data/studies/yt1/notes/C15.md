# C15 — VWAP trend-day first pullback

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C15.py`.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- VWAP and its standard deviation are computed on the 5-minute bars (`ind.session_vwap`, hlc3 weighted by volume),
  restarting at the 09:30 bar of each day. A bar's close is compared with the VWAP value of that same bar.
- Run = the count of consecutive closes above VWAP reaches `run`; the pullback is looked for from the next bar on.
- "The pullback's low" = the low of the pullback bar as the spec defines it (the first bar with low ≤ VWAP + 4 ticks),
  not the lowest low up to the entry bar. The two differ only when the entry comes one to three bars later and a
  later bar made a lower low.
- ATR(14) = Wilder ATR of the 5-minute continuous series at the signal bar. The stop (pullback low − 0.5 × ATR) is put
  on the tick grid with `core.tick_round` (nearest).
- Exit levels are fixed at the signal bar's close: part 1 = the farther from the entry of (VWAP + 1 sd) and (signal
  close + 1R), part 2 = the farther of (VWAP + 2 sd) and (signal close + 2R), R = signal close − stop, each rounded to
  the tick, half the position at each. **`simulate` cannot move a target with the VWAP bands after entry**, so a
  reading in which the band targets follow the bands is not expressible; fixed levels are the nearest form. The stop
  is not moved after the first half.
- "A close below VWAP first ends the set-up": a close exactly at VWAP neither enters nor ends it.
- After a set-up ends without an entry, a new set-up needs a new run: the count restarts from a close that is not
  above VWAP. An unbroken run therefore gives one "first pullback" only.
- "One per direction per day" = the day's first entry signal in each direction is the only order in that direction;
  if it is blocked by an open position (one at a time) there is no later one that day.
- Entry bars close by 15:00 (common frame); the run and the pullback may be anywhere from 09:30.
- Short mirror: closes below VWAP, pullback = high ≥ VWAP − 4 ticks, entry bar closes below VWAP and below its open,
  stop = pullback high + 0.5 × ATR, targets at VWAP − 1 sd / − 2 sd or 1R / 2R, whichever is farther.
