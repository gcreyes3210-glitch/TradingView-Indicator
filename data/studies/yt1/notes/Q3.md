# Q3 — 1-minute VWAP with EMA 9, pullback version (M14, Modern Scalping)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q3.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q3.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"buy at its close, 09:35 to 15:29"** = the entry bar is *stamped* 09:35 … 15:29 (the spec names a close by its
  bar), on a traded cash day and before the flat bar. Under the clock reading of YT1's common rule the window
  would be the bars stamped 09:34 … 15:28. The touch bar may be earlier than 09:35.
- VWAP = `ind.session_vwap` on the 1-minute bars 09:30–15:59, restarted each day; EMA(9), EMA(21) = `ind.ema` on
  the continuous 24-hour 1-minute closes. A bar's low is compared with the EMA values of that same bar.
- "The up state at the previous close" = at the bar before in the series; it needs a VWAP, so a touch bar is 09:31
  or later.
- Every bar that meets the touch definition is a touch bar, with its own search for an entry on itself and the
  next four bars. An entry bar is therefore a bar that closes above its open and above EMA(9) with the up state,
  for which some touch bar lies 0–4 bars back with (a) no earlier bar of that kind from the touch on (it is "the
  first") and (b) no low at or below EMA(21) from the touch bar through the entry bar.
- When several touch bars lead to the same entry bar, the earliest of them is "the touch", so the lowest low covers
  the whole pullback inside the four-bar reach (the tag `t-N` gives the bars from the touch to the entry).
- "The first bar … that closes above its open and above EMA(9) with the up state" is found without regard to the
  clock; the trade is taken only if that bar is inside the window.
- Stop = lowest low from the touch bar through the entry bar − 1 tick. Target = signal close + k × (signal close −
  stop), `core.tick_round` (nearest; only the 1.5R neighbour ever needs rounding).
- Short mirror: down state = close below VWAP and EMA(9) below EMA(21); touch = high at or above EMA(9) and below
  EMA(21) with the down state at the previous close; entry = first bar closing below its open and below EMA(9) with
  the down state, no high at or above EMA(21) since the touch; stop 1 tick above the highest high.
- One position at a time = `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)`: a signal on a bar at or before the previous trade's
  exit bar is skipped (1,420 of 28,421 signals in base fell on the previous trade's exit bar).
