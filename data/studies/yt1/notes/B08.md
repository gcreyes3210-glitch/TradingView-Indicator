# B08 - edgeful initial-balance retracement entry (`tools/yt1/s_B08.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "In the top quarter" includes the quarter line: 10:29 close >= IB high - W / 4, tested on the unrounded line
  (short: close <= IB low + W / 4). "Above the 09:30 open" is strict.
- "Set before": the 1-minute bar that first set the IB low is earlier than the one that first set the IB high.
- Prices that fall between ticks: the entry (IB high - W / 4, or the midpoint for `opt2`) and the target go to the
  nearest tick; the stop is 1 tick beyond the midpoint and, when the midpoint is a half tick, the next tick away from
  the entry (never tighter than the text).
- "Cancelled if the target trades first": `cancel_hi` (long) / `cancel_lo` (short) at the target price, i.e. a bar
  reaching the target before the limit fills cancels the order; within one bar the harness path decides.
- nb1 "target the IB high" therefore also cancels the order as soon as the IB high trades again.
- The decision bar is the IB's last 1-minute bar (the 10:29 bar on every in-sample day); the limit rests from the
  next bar to the last bar at or before 14:59.
