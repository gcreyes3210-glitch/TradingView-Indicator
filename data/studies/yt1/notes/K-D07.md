# K-D07 - Unfinished auctions (Trader Dale), claim check  (tools/yt1/k_D07.py)

Spec: "5-minute footprint bars that set a new session high: share whose high is traded through by 11:35, split by
unfinished (both buy and sell volume at the high price) and finished (no sell volume there). Low mirror."
No figure is published; the direction claimed is that the market "likes to test" an unfinished auction, so
unfinished > finished. No pass / fail.

Data check of the footprint (0.25 grid, alignment with MNQ, the six other-contract days): notes/D03.md, section 0.
This check reads only footprint prices, so the NQ / MNQ differences and the roll days do not touch it.

## 1. Definitions added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- A bar's high / low = its highest / lowest footprint price. Session high so far = the highest high of ALL the
  day's earlier footprint bars from the 09:30 bar, so the 09:30 bar is never an event.
- Event bars open 09:35 ... 11:25 (family D's sample). "By 11:35" = any later footprint bar of the day up to and
  including the 11:30 bar. Traded through = at least one tick beyond (an equal high is not through).
- Unfinished high = buy > 0 and sell > 0 at the high price; finished high = sell = 0 there. A high with sell volume
  only is neither and is listed apart (`other_one_sided`: 1 high, 2 lows in-sample). Low mirror: unfinished = both
  sides traded at the low price; finished = no buy volume there.
- All 419 footprint days are counted, roll days included. Pooled = high events and low events together.
- Not controlled for: an event late in the window has less time left to be traded through. The two groups are
  compared as the spec states, without matching on time of day.

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
