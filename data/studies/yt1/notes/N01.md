# N01 - NQSimon Asia-range deviations, New York morning reversal  (tools/yt1/s_N01.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **Asia range** = every 1-minute bar stamped 20:00-23:59 on the calendar date before the cash day (Sunday evening
  for a Monday). A day whose window has no bars is skipped (none in-sample: 926 of 926 cash days have one).
- **Manipulation side** = the first 1-minute bar from 00:00 to 09:29 on the cash day's own date whose low is at least
  1 tick below L or whose high is at least 1 tick above H. **One bar beyond both sides = no trade** (the order of the
  two is unknown); this never happened in-sample.
- **"Its level has not traded between 00:00 and 09:29"** = no bar in that window has low <= the level (longs) /
  high >= the level (shorts).
- **Signal bar** of both orders = the 09:29 bar (the last bar before 09:30 if that minute has no bar); the orders rest
  from the 09:30 bar to the 11:29 bar (the last bar before 11:30).
- **Levels on the tick.** Z2 and Z4 are on the tick already. Void = L - 4.5 W falls on a half tick when W is an odd
  number of ticks; it is rounded **away from the range** (down for longs, up for shorts). It only matters on a bar
  that opens at or through Void (the harness cancels a resting order there); otherwise Z4 always fills first.
- **Stop** = the ORDER price -/+ the distance (10 / 7.5 / 15 points; `scaled`: 0.0004 x the order price, the stop
  price then rounded to the nearest tick), not the fill price. **Target** = H for longs, L for shorts, no buffer.
- **`bias`**: longs only when `pdc` > the RTH high of the session before the previous one (`rth_h` shifted two rows),
  shorts only when `pdc` < that session's RTH low (`rth_l` shifted two rows). Days where neither holds, or where it
  disagrees with the manipulation side, are not traded. The current day's `rth_*` are never read.
- **One position at a time, at most 2 trades.** Both orders of a day share one signal bar, and
  `run_orders(one_at_a_time=True)` skips any order whose signal bar is at or before the previous trade's exit bar, so
  it would drop the Z4 order on every day the Z2 order filled (one trade a day, not the spec's two). So `trades()`
  calls `core.run_orders(ctx, orders, one_at_a_time=False, max_per_day=2)` with the Z2 order first in each day's
  list (the harness simulates each resting order on its own and still drops roll and no-ATR days), then applies the
  one-position rule explicitly: a trade filled before the day's earlier trade has exited is dropped; a fill on the
  very bar the earlier trade was stopped is kept, because that stop lies between Z2 and Z4 and so comes first on the
  bar's path. The stop of the Z2 order (at most 15 points) is always nearer than Z4 (2 W >= 20 points away), so the
  Z2 trade is always closed before Z4 can fill: the explicit check dropped **0** trades. Both orders filled on
  **18** days in-sample (Z2 first, then Z4, never on the same bar). For comparison, the literal
  `one_at_a_time=True` call gives 149 base trades instead of 167 (the 18 second trades are lost).
- Harness behaviour that applies here, left as is: a bar that opens at or through Void cancels the order instead of
  filling it; a Z2 order filled at a bar's open that is already beyond its own stop is not a valid order (no trade).
