# G2 - TTrades: candle 4 continuation (`tools/yt1/s_G2.py`)

Spec: `YT4_SPEC.md` Part 2, G2. Daily gate: candle 2 is a swing low (lower low than the candle before it, not
undercut by candle 1) and candle 1 closed above candle 2's body. Void once price trades below EQ after 18:00.
Trigger: the first 3-minute bullish CISD of the morning whose protected low is at or above EQ. Entry at its close,
stop 1 tick beyond the protected low, target 2R, flat bar, one trade a day. Neighbours: 5-minute (nb1) and 1-minute
(nb2) trigger bars. Short = mirror.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- (R) **No `tt.bias` in this rule.** G2 does not say "bias required" (G4-G6 do, G3 says "cont days only"), so the
  direction is the daily gate alone: `tt.closure3` on the `tt.daily` candles, read at candle 1 (bullish -> long,
  bearish -> short; they cannot both hold). On the 129 base trade days `tt.bias` points the same way on 108, is none
  on 8 and points the **other** way on 13 (candle 1 ran candle 2's high and closed back under it = bearish `fail`,
  while still closing above candle 2's body). Those 13 are traded with the gate, as the text says.
- (R) **No gate across a contract roll.** The spec says "no bias across a contract roll" only for the bias. I applied
  the same to this gate: a day is skipped when any of the three daily candles the gate reads, or the day's own
  candle, is flagged `roll` in `tt.daily` (a roll candle mixes two contracts, and EQ comes from candle 1). This is
  not in G2's text. Effect in-sample: 12 closure-3 days left out; 3 are roll cash days the harness drops anyway,
  6 have the roll candle as candle 1 or 2 (what `tt.bias` would also refuse), 3 only because the candle before
  candle 2 is the roll candle (2021-06-17, 2021-12-16, 2022-09-19). The fully literal reading would add at most 9
  trades to the 129.
- (R) "Trades below EQ" = any 1-minute low < EQ (strict; Part 1's "at least 1 tick" is not carried over; the two
  differ only when EQ falls on a half tick). "After 18:00" = from the first bar of the trading day (`ctx.tdate`,
  Sunday 18:00 for a Monday) through the decision bar. Void is permanent for the day.
- (R) "Protected low at or above EQ": `plo >= EQ`. A morning CISD with a lower protected low is passed over (in
  practice the void test has then already ended the day).
- (R) "Of the morning" applies to the CISD bar only (it opens at or after 08:30 and its last minute is 10:59 or
  earlier). The CISD is `tt.cisd` on the continuous N-minute series, so the down-close run it closes through may have
  started before 08:30.
- (R) Only the first qualifying CISD is an order; nothing later is tried that day.
- An N-minute bar is acted on at the close of its last 1-minute bar (`i_last`, the house convention). On all orders
  of all three variants that bar is the bucket's nominal last minute.
- Target = close + 2 x (close - stop), rounded to the tick (YT1 common rule, before slippage).
- nb2 uses `ctx.bars(1)` (1-minute "bars" through the same code path).

## 5. Odd things
- 215 gate days (114 long, 101 short), 214 of them on tradable days: price had already traded through EQ before
  08:30 on 74 and by 11:00 on 105. Base: 129 trades (78 long, 51 short), 89 stops and 40 targets, none held to the
  flat bar.
- Average risk 20.3 points (base); a stop prints about -1.03R to -1.18R in the sample above because of the two
  ticks of slippage and the $2 commission.
- The roll test copies `tt.bias`'s own-candle test and so shares its edge case (see `notes/G1.md`, 2020-03-16); that
  day is a roll cash day and is dropped by the harness.
