# E04 - NQ Stats Noon Curve (`tools/yt1/s_E04.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- "Traded above Q1's high" = Q2's high at least 1 tick above Q1's high; "not below Q1's low" = Q2's low at or above
  Q1's low. Short mirror: Q2 traded below Q1's low and not above Q1's high; stop 1 tick beyond Q2's high.
- Entry = market at the close of the bar stamped at the decision minute (it exists on every in-sample day). A day
  with no bars in Q1 or Q2 is skipped (none in-sample).
- **Neighbours move the decision only.** Q2 stays the clock window 10:00-11:59, as much of it as has traded by the
  decision: 10:00-11:29 for `nb1`; the whole 10:00-11:59 for `nb2` (bars 12:00-12:29 are not Q2). The spec has
  no "if the stop traded first" clause for E04, so at 12:29 the trade is taken even if Q2's extreme was traded
  through after noon, unless the 12:29 close is at or beyond the stop, which is not a valid order (the harness
  drops it: 46 of 727 `nb2` orders).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
