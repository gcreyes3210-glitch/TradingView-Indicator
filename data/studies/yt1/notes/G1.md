# G1 - TTrades: the daily bias on its own (`tools/yt1/s_G1.py`)

Spec: `YT4_SPEC.md` Part 2, G1. Bias bullish -> long at the 09:30 open, bearish -> short; no stop; flat bar;
R unit 0.1 x daily ATR. Neighbours: `cont` days only (nb1); `fail` days only (nb2).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (fixed before the first run)
- (R) "At the 09:30 open" = `etype="open"` with `i` = the last 1-minute bar before the 09:30 bar, so the fill is the
  open of the 09:30 bar plus the house tick. That bar is 09:29 on all but 5 in-sample days (2020-03-09 09:12,
  03-12 09:24, 03-13 09:24, 03-16 07:46, 03-18 09:24: no bars in between); it is always on the same calendar date.
- (R) The bias is `tt.bias(ctx)` as it stands; nothing is added to it. `kind` selects the neighbours.
- (R) No target either (the spec names none); R unit = 0.1 x `days.atr` (ATR(14) through the previous trading day).
- One order a day. Roll days and days without an ATR are dropped by `core.run_orders`.

None. The module is as first written.

## 5. Odd things
- The look-ahead test prints "the sampled order is not reproduced on the 150-day window" on every line. That is the
  ATR, not the rule: `r_pts = 0.1 x ATR` is part of the order key and the RMA ATR on a 150-day window differs from the
  full-history one in the third decimal (2022-11-16: 37.7997 against 37.7954). The two window runs it compares agree.
- nb2 has a positive net and a negative mean R: the R unit (0.1 x ATR) changes from day to day.
- 742 orders, 741 trades: the one dropped is 2020-03-16, a contract-roll cash day. `tt.bias` gives that day a bias
  (+1, fail) although the contract changed inside it (2020-03-15 20:01). Reason: that trading day has 571 one-minute
  bars, so `tt.daily` drops its candle (< 690), and `tt.bias` only tests "the day's own candle is a roll" when the
  candle exists. Reproduce: `tt.bias(ctx).loc["2020-03-16"]` -> bias 1, kind 'fail'; `"2020-03-16" in tt.daily(ctx).index`
  -> False. Two points for whoever owns `tt.py` (I changed nothing and worked around nothing): (a) the docstring says
  no bias when a candle is missing or a roll lies in the candles, and this day gets one; (b) whether the own-candle
  test applies depends on the day's final bar count, which is not known at 09:30. No trade is affected in-sample:
  the harness drops the day as a roll day, and it is the only cash day whose own candle is missing.
