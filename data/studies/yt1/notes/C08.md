# C08 - Bollinger upper-band breakout + 200 MA (long only)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_C08.py`. Variants: `base` 5-minute, `nb1` 3-minute, `nb2` 15-minute bars (`bar` parameter).

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- **"Close crosses above the upper band"** = close[k] > upper[k] and close[k-1] <= upper[k-1] (`ind.crossed_up`),
  Bollinger (20, 2) with the population standard deviation (`ind.bollinger`, TradingView's).
- **Daily filter.** Trading day = 18:00 -> 17:00 (`ctx.tdate`, the same days the harness uses for the daily ATR; 927
  of them in-sample). Daily close = the last 1-minute close of the trading day; EMA9 = `ind.ema` of those closes. A
  signal bar on trading day D reads only day D-1: close(D-1) > EMA9(D-1), the EMA including D-1's own close. Nothing
  of day D enters. True on 61.6 % of bars.
- **Signal window on the neighbours.** "Bars closing 09:35 -> 15:00" = open time + N minutes within 09:35 ... 15:00:
  5-minute bars opening 09:30 ... 14:55, 3-minute 09:33 ... 14:57, 15-minute 09:30 ... 14:45.
- **Swing stop** = lowest low of the 10 bars k-9 ... k of the continuous series, minus 1 tick.
  **Target** = close + 1.5 x (close - stop), `core.tick_round` to the nearest tick (a half tick goes to the even
  tick, the harness's rounding).
- Every qualifying bar is a candidate order; `run_orders` enforces one position at a time (896 candidates, 490 trades).
