# E12 - Larry Williams Oops, long only, end-of-day exit (`tools/yt1/s_E12.py`)

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- Signal bar = the 09:30 bar (the open that defines the set-up is its open). The buy stop at the previous RTH low
  rests from the 09:31 bar through the last bar before 15:00 (the 14:59 bar), never past the flat bar.
- If price is already at or above the low when the order starts to rest (the 09:30 bar ran back through it), the
  harness fills the stop at the 09:31 open, as a stop order placed through the market would.
- Base: any 09:30 open strictly below the previous RTH low. Neighbours: previous RTH low - open >= 0.05 / 0.10 x
  the day's daily ATR(14).
- "End-of-day exit" = the flat bar. No stop, so the R unit is 0.1 x the day's daily ATR(14) (`r_pts`).
- `both`: adds the mirror, 09:30 open strictly above the previous RTH high -> sell stop at that high, same window
  and exit. The two set-ups cannot fall on the same day.
- "Previous RTH low / high" are the day table's `pdl` / `pdh`, i.e. the previous row of the harness day table, which
  includes US-holiday short sessions (e.g. the level on 2019-11-29 is the low of the Thanksgiving-day session).
