# A03 - Casper 30-minute range, sweep and FVG back inside  (tools/yt1/s_A03.py)

## 1. Readings added to the spec text (all fixed before the first run)
- **"5-minute bars from 10:00"** is applied to every bar the rule uses: the first sweep bar and all three FVG candles
  open at or after 10:00, so candle 3 (the signal bar) opens 10:10 ... 10:55. (The 09:50 / 09:55 bars lie inside the
  range window and could not have traded beyond the range anyway.)
- **"Traded above"** = high > range high (on the tick grid the same as >= 1 tick beyond); **"closes below"** =
  close < range high. Long mirror with low < range low, close > range low.
- **First sweep bar** = the first 5-minute bar from 10:00 with high > range high (low < range low for longs). It may
  itself be one of the three FVG candles. The stop is 1 tick beyond the highest max(open, close) of the 5-minute bars
  from that bar through candle 3 inclusive (lowest min(open, close) for longs).
- **Target** = candle 3's close +/- k x |close - stop|, on the tick grid (`core.tick_round`, nearest).
- **Candles 1, 2, 3** are adjacent rows of the 5-minute series inside the window.
