# Q1 — VWAP stop-and-reverse (N20, Zarattini and Aziz)

Module: `tools/yt1/s_Q1.py`. Spec: `YT11_SPEC.md`, Part 4, Q1.

## 1. Readings added beyond the spec text
- VWAP = `ind.session_vwap` (hlc3 × volume) on the 1-minute bars 09:30–15:59, restarted at the first of them each
  day, read at the bar just closed (the spec's shared definition).
- A close equal to VWAP is on neither side: it starts nothing and reverses nothing (the spec states this for the
  09:30 bar; the same is applied to every later close, "on the other side" being strict).
- Each leg is one order: market at the close of its flip bar, `exit_i` = the next flip bar (the close at which the
  position is reversed), or the flat bar. House costs on both fills of every leg. A flip that falls on the flat bar
  is only the exit of the leg before it. No stop, so `r_pts = 0.1 × that day's daily ATR(14)`.
- Neighbours (`nb1` 3-minute, `nb2` 5-minute closes): the decision bars are the 09:30 one-minute bar and then the
  last 1-minute bar of every 3-minute (5-minute) clock bar (`ctx.bars(N).i_last`); VWAP stays the 1-minute VWAP.
  "Wait for the first close that differs" then means the first *decision* close that differs.
- `run_orders(one_at_a_time=False)`: a leg's signal bar is the previous leg's exit bar, which `one_at_a_time=True`
  drops. The legs cannot overlap by construction; `trades()` asserts that every leg's fill bar is at or after the
  previous leg's exit bar. Roll days and days without a daily ATR are dropped by `run_orders` as usual.
