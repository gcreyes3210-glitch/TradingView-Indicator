# H1 — Pat's manipulation-candle fade (YT8_SPEC.md, Part 2)

Module `tools/yt1/s_H1.py`. Variants: `base` stop = target distance ÷ 1.5, `nb1` ÷ 1.0, `nb2` ÷ 2.0.
Written from the spec text, `core.py`, `run.py`, `s_Z00.py`; `L_playlist.md` (Model B) opened for the wording behind
"candle size" and "about a 1.5". In-sample bars only (2019-06-03 → 2022-12-30). Day rules: `core.run_orders(...,
max_per_day=1, skip_roll=2)`.

## 1. Readings (R) — all fixed before the first run
1. **The 96 bars (R).** The 96 clock-aligned 15-minute bars of the continuous 1-minute series (`ctx.bars(15)`) that
   come immediately before the bar stamped 09:30, whatever buckets exist. The 17:00–17:59 break has no bars (and, to
   mid-2021, neither has 16:15–16:29), so the 96 bars start at 08:15 or 08:30 of the previous trading day (Friday's,
   on a Monday). True range = max(high − low, |high − previous close|, |low − previous close|); the first of the 96
   uses the close of the bar before it. Simple mean, the 09:30 candle excluded (both as the spec says).
2. **"Larger than" (R)** is strict: high − low > mean true range.
3. **Down-close / up-close (R).** 09:44 close < 09:30 open → buy limit at the candle's low; close > open → sell limit
   at its high; close = open → no trade (4 days).
4. **Target and stop prices (R).** Target distance = 0.382 × (high − low), exact. Target = edge ± that distance;
   stop = edge ∓ that distance ÷ div; each put on the tick grid with `core.tick_round` (nearest). Both are fixed from
   the limit price when the order is placed; a better fill at a gap open does not move them (1 base trade filled at a
   gap open).
5. **"Rests 09:45–11:59" (R).** Decision bar = the candle's last 1-minute bar (09:44); the limit is live in the bars
   stamped 09:45 … 11:59, the 11:59 bar included (`expire` = the last bar before 12:00), then cancelled.
6. **No other cancel (R).** The spec gives H1 no cancel rule, so the limit stays even if price trades through the
   target price before reaching the limit. That happened before the fill in 364 of the 719 base trades.
7. Unfilled-at-target-or-stop trades are closed at the flat bar (none in-sample: every trade ended at its stop or
   target).

## 5. Odd
- **The size test hardly filters.** The 09:30–09:44 candle is larger than the 96-bar average true range on 910 of
  925 days (98 %): most of the 96 bars are overnight bars. As registered.
- On 2020-03-09 and 2020-03-12 the 09:30 candle has 5 and 7 one-minute bars (trading halt); the decision bar is the
  last bar before 09:45 (09:35 / 09:36) and the order is live from the first bar at or after 09:45 (09:49 / 09:50).
  2020-03-16 (1 bar) is a roll day and not traded.
- The harness day table counts exchange holidays with a half futures session (for example 2019-07-04, 2020-01-20,
  Thanksgiving Thursdays) as cash days with an early flat bar, so the rule trades on them like every YT1 module.
  `ctx.days.loc["2019-07-04"]` → `n_bars 198, early True`. Not changed (harness, not mine).
