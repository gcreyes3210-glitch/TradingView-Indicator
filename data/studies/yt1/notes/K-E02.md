# K-E02 - IB breaks, claim check (`tools/yt1/k_E02.py`)

Claim (nqstats.com/ib_breaks.html, video 24P7rsb9P_8; 2016-2026, 2,571 qualifying sessions): see the table. No pass /
fail: measured next to claimed.

## 1. Definitions (the source's, research note E entry 2; all fixed before the first run)
- IB = 09:30-10:29 1-minute bars; midpoint = (high + low) / 2; IB close = the 10:29 close.
- Qualifying session: the first bar after the IB (10:30) opens inside the IB range, edges included. In-sample every
  one of the 926 cash days qualifies. Roll days are included (the measure is within one session).
- Break = a bar after the IB trading >= 1 tick beyond the level; by noon = bars 10:30-11:59, by close = bars
  10:30-15:59. An early-close day (29) ends at its halt; the table without those days is printed beside.
- Close above / below the midpoint: strict (1 day closed exactly at it). Low set first: the bar that first set the
  IB low is earlier than the bar that first set the high (same bar: never).

## 2. Coding errors found after first seeing results
None. The module was not changed after its first run.
